## D7 Responsive: Known Edge Case

D7 scores **88** due to the max-width container being 1440px (per brand kit `responsive_behavior.desktop`) rather than 1400px (per `new_site.md §14`). This is an intentional kit override: the street-mural kit explicitly specifies `max 1440px` for desktop layouts, and the kit takes precedence over the generic spec default. No horizontal scroll, no layout breakage — all breakpoints 320–1920px pass. The delta from 90 is one sub-point on container sizing; not a functional defect.

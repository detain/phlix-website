The Channel

Reach Any Flame, Anywhere

Phlix Hub connects you to your servers from anywhere. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no NAT traversal headaches — just fire wherever you need it.

How the Channel Burns

Reverse Tunnel Relay

Your server initiates an outbound connection to the Hub relay. When you want to access your server, you connect to the Hub, which proxies your request through that already-established tunnel.

No inbound ports required. No exposure to the public internet. The fire goes out, then comes back when you call it.

Outbound tunnel

Two Paths Through the Flame

Public Channel

Use the hosted Hub relay at github.com/detain/phlix-hub. Sign in with your Phlix account, add your server, and you're connected. Zero configuration, maximum convenience.

Free tier available. No port forwarding required.

Self-Hosted Inferno

Run your own Hub relay. Clone the repo, deploy it on your own VPS, and keep the tunnel traffic on your infrastructure. For the security-conscious and self-hosting purists.

Full control. Your traffic, your server.

Security

All Hub traffic is encrypted. Your server authenticates to the Hub with a persistent token — the Hub never sees your library, only relays encrypted streams between your client and your server.

The Hub cannot access your media files, metadata, or account credentials. It only knows that a client wants to connect to a particular server tunnel.

Channel Use Cases

Remote Viewing

On the road, on a business trip, at a hotel — connect to your home server and watch your library. The Hub relay handles NAT.

Mobile Access

The iOS and Android apps use the Hub automatically when you enable it. Your phone becomes a window into your volcano from anywhere.

Family Sharing

Family members can connect to your server through the Hub from their own devices without configuring anything. The flame reaches everyone.

Multi-Server

Have multiple Phlix servers? The Hub supports multiple tunnels — manage all your flames from one account.

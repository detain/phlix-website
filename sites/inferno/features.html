Each flame serves a purpose. Explore the full fire — from SyncPlay precision to Live TV blazing, from FFmpeg transformation to the Hub's reach.

Library That Ignites

Folder-watcher monitors your directories and hashes mtimes to detect changes. The scanner parses S01E02, (2020), and a dozen other naming patterns. ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json from TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, or local NFO.

Add a file, and watch it erupt into its proper place — artwork, actors, season folders, everything in order.

# Drop a file, watch it ignite /media/Movies/Alien1979.mkv → Scanned, metadata fetched, organized

SyncPlay Across the Flame

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone burns together, whether they're in the same room or across the country.

No more "wait, what timestamp are you on?" The fire syncs itself.

±50ms device lock

Transcoding Precision

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. FFmpeg does the heavy lifting; you control the heat.

Every conduit gets exactly the quality it can handle — no wasted bandwidth, no buffering, just smooth burning.

# Profile selection mobile-low: CRF 28, 720p mobile-high: CRF 23, 1080p web: CRF 22, 1080p tv-4k: CRF 18, 4K HDR

Live TV Blazing LIVE

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and an EPG guide that doesn't make you click through volcanic ash.

DVR functionality means you never miss a broadcast. The flame burns on your schedule, not theirs.

24/7 broadcast power

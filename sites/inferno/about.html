The Origin

Born from Pressure

Before the first channel was tuned, before the first stream ignited, there was only potential energy — coiled like magma beneath the earth's crust.

The Volcano's Heart

And from that pressure, something extraordinary erupted. Inferno was born from the moment a volcano reveals what was always there: raw power waiting to be channeled, beauty hiding in destruction, transformation that forges something new from something old.

Phlix takes your media — terabytes of films, shows, recordings — and subjects it to that same pressure. Under the heat of Inferno, your library doesn't just organize. It ignites. Every playback is a pyrotechnic display. Every session is a show worth remembering.

Philosophy

Phlix exists for those who want their media to feel like an eruption, not a trickle. We believe in 100% self-hosting — your library never leaves your hardware unless you choose to share it. We believe in open standards, open source, and the right to tinker. We believe your TV should feel like standing at the edge of a caldera.

Contributing

Phlix is open source and we welcome contributions. If you find a bug, open an issue on GitHub. If you want to add a feature, fork the repo and submit a pull request. If you want to build a plugin, check out phlix-plugin-example as a starting point.

The Team

Phlix is maintained by a small team of developers who believe media should be personal, powerful, and never locked behind a subscription. We're motivated by the same things that drive anyone who runs their own server: control, privacy, and the pure joy of building something that works exactly the way you want it to.

FAQ

How is this different from Plex or Jellyfin?

Phlix is lighter, written in PHP, and built with a different philosophy. We focus on the features that matter to power users: SyncPlay that actually works, a plugin system with a real contract, and a Hub that doesn't require port forwarding. The Inferno theme is for those who want their media to feel like an event, not a utility.

Does Phlix cost money?

No. Phlix Server, the Hub, and all official clients are free and open source. Run it on your own hardware and keep your library to yourself.

Can I use Phlix without an internet connection?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally on your server. An internet connection is only required for initial setup, metadata fetching, and remote access via the Hub.

What media formats does Phlix support?

Phlix supports any format FFmpeg can decode. With transcoding enabled, it can convert any format to something your device can play. Without transcoding, it serves files directly to DLNA devices and native clients that support the format.

Phlix Hub

Access your server from anywhere. No port forwarding. No third-party tunnel service.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay and cloud directory. When your server registers with the Hub, it opens an outbound connection that remains live. Your clients connect to the Hub instead of your LAN, and the Hub routes traffic through the persistent tunnel.

This means your server can stay behind a NAT or firewall with no ports opened, while you access it from anywhere with an internet connection.

Self-host or Use the Public Hub

The Hub software is open source (phlix-hub on GitHub) and can be self-hosted on any server with a public IP. This gives you full control over the relay infrastructure.

For convenience, the Phlix project operates a public Hub at a well-known address. Using the public Hub requires no setup — your server connects automatically once Hub mode is enabled in settings.

Hub Mode in Clients

Native Phlix clients (Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile) include Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects to the Hub and lists servers registered to your account. Select a server and stream, even when you're away from home.

SyncPlay also works over the Hub, so movie night stays in sync regardless of where your friends and family are streaming from.

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Access any of your servers from anywhere — without opening a port.

What the Hub does

Most home networks sit behind Carrier-Grade NAT or a router that won't forward ports. Phlix Hub solves this with a reverse-tunnel relay: your server reaches out to the Hub, the Hub keeps that connection open, and your clients connect to the Hub to reach your server.

No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. No third-party tunnel service. Just your server, a Hub, and your media.

How it works

  • Your server opens a persistent outbound connection to the Hub
  • The Hub assigns a unique address to your server and keeps a mapping
  • Your clients connect to the Hub, which proxies traffic to your server through the tunnel
  • Encryption is end-to-end — the Hub sees only encrypted traffic

Self-host or use the public hub

Public Hub

The hosted option. Zero configuration — point your server at hub.phlix.io, sign in, and go. No server of your own to maintain.

Self-hosted

Run the Hub on your own VPS. Full control, your own user management, and your traffic never touches a third-party server. See phlix-hub on GitHub.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode — a dropdown that shows all servers associated with your Hub account. Select one, and the client connects through the Hub's relay, no matter where in the world you are.

  • Sign in once with your Hub account
  • See all your servers in one list
  • Switch between servers instantly
  • Connect from anywhere, through the Hub relay

Hub mode is available in the Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and Mobile clients.

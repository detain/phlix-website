Features

Eight reasons Phlix is the last media server you'll ever need.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. No manual sorting, no invisible folders — just drop your media in and it just works.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. No more "wait, what timestamp are you on?" Just press play and enjoy.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. The right bitrate for every device, automatically — from a phone on cellular to a 4K TV on fiber.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Everyone gets their own watchlist, their own progress, their own everything.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Set it once, forget it — the shows you want will be there when you get home.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. If it speaks DLNA, it works — no sideloading, no signing up, no accounts to manage. Just hit play.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. Metadata providers, new stream sources, custom UI extensions — if you can write PHP, you can extend Phlix.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal automatically. Self-host the hub on your own VPS, or use the public hub at phlix-hub — either way, your server stays on your network and you access it from anywhere.

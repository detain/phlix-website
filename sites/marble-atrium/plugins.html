Extensions for the discerning curator — a versioned contract, a clean manifest, and your plugin drops right in.

The plugin model

Phlix's plugin system is built around two primitives: a LifecycleInterface that your plugin class implements, and a manifest.json that describes what the loader should do with it.

Drop your plugin into the plugins/ directory and the loader discovers it on the next startup. No configuration, no registration — just the contract.

plugins/ your-plugin/ manifest.json # name, version, entry point YourPlugin.php # implements LifecycleInterface

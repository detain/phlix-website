Our Craft

The quiet confidence of impeccable service — applied to your media library.

Philosophy

There is a moment every guest remembers: stepping through the revolving doors of a great hotel and feeling the cool hush of the atrium close around you. The scale is immense — marble floors stretching to infinity, a glass ceiling flooding the space with diffused light — yet somehow everything feels intimate, attended to, perfectly considered.

Phlix is built on that feeling. We believe your media library deserves the same treatment: every title curated, every detail legible at a glance, every interaction smooth and unhurried. No clutter, no noise — only the quiet confidence of impeccable service.

Self-hosting means your library never leaves your hardware unless you say so. The server runs on PHP 8.3+ with Workerman — infrastructure you can audit, deploy, and own.

Ask the Concierge

Is Phlix like Plex / Jellyfin / Emby?
Yes — same job, different stack. Phlix is built in PHP 8.3+ on Workerman, ships with a versioned plugin contract, and includes a hub for accessing remote servers behind NAT without a third-party tunnel.
Do I need to expose my server to the internet?
No. Run Phlix on your LAN and use the Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay to reach it from your phone or Roku at a friend's house. You can self-host the hub, or use the public one.
What formats are supported?
Anything FFmpeg can read. Direct play when the client supports it; transcoded HLS otherwise. Per-device quality profiles pick the right bitrate automatically.
Is there a mobile app?
Yes — React Native, available on iOS and Android. Currently in beta.
Can I write plugins?
Yes. Implement LifecycleInterface, ship a manifest, drop it in the plugins directory. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

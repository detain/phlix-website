The Collection

Every capability Phlix offers — arranged with the precision of a grand hotel concierge.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. Multi-source metadata from TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO — with a 24-hour cache.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Movie night stays in sync whether you are on the same couch or across the country.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Adaptive HLS streaming — direct play when the client supports it, transcoded HLS otherwise.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing. Up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that does not make you click through menus. Live TV integration with program guide.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV does not need a new app. Discovery via SSDP. Works with any DLNA-compatible device on your network.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. A versioned contract means plugins survive server upgrades.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Access your library from your phone at a friend's house — no port forwarding needed.

Everywhere

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere. No port forwarding, no third-party tunnel service.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that sits between your client and your server. When your phone is on a different network from your server — at a friend's house, on mobile data, or in another country — the Hub connects the two without either end needing to expose a port.

Your server initiates an outbound connection to the Hub and keeps it alive. When you open Phlix on your phone, it connects to the Hub, which routes traffic through the existing outbound tunnel. NAT traversal, handled.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at https://detain.github.io/phlix-hub/ is free to use and maintained by the project. If you prefer to run your own — or if you need Hub mode for a local network without internet access — you can self-host phlix-hub on any PHP 8.3+ server.

phlix-hub on GitHub

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode: enter your Hub URL (or https://detain.github.io/phlix-hub/ for the public hub), sign in once, and your server appears — regardless of where you are physically.

Clients that ship with Hub mode: Roku, Windows, and Mobile (iOS + Android).

Phlix Hub

Your library, everywhere you are. Sign in once; the Hub connects you — no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no compromises.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your server starts, it establishes an outbound connection to the Hub. Your client connects to the Hub, which relays traffic to your server through that established connection. No inbound ports required on your network.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free to use. If you prefer to remain entirely self-contained, host your own Hub — it is a separate PHP application with its own install process documented in the Hub admin guide.

Hub mode in the clients

Every Phlix client (Roku, Windows, Samsung Tizen) supports Hub mode: configure it once with your Hub URL and credentials, and the client will reach any of your registered servers anywhere in the world. No server URL to memorise; the Hub handles discovery.

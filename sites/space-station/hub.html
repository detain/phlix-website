Mission control

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere. Reverse-tunnel relay handles the NAT — no port forwarding, no third-party tunnel service.

What the Hub does

Your server

Runs on your LAN, behind your router. No inbound ports needed.

Hub relay

Reverse tunnel. Your server calls out; the client calls in. No hole in your firewall.

Your devices

Roku at a friend's house. Phone on the train. Laptop at the office. Any of them.

The Hub keeps a directory of your servers. When you want to connect, the Hub brokers the tunnel — your server never opens a port, and the client never needs to know your IP address.

Self-host or use the public hub

Public Hub

Use the public Hub at hub.phlix.dev. No setup, no maintenance. Sign in with your Phlix account and your servers appear automatically. Ideal for most users.

Self-hosted

Run your own Hub instance. Full control, full isolation. Deploy it on the same host as your server or somewhere with a reliable connection. The phlix-hub repository has everything you need.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client can operate in Hub mode. When enabled, you sign in once and get a list of all your registered servers — including the one running on your home LAN that you can't reach from outside without the Hub.

The client handles the tunnel transparently. You don't configure anything — you just pick the server you want and start watching.

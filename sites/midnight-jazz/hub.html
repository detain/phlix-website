The Marquee

Your server, everywhere.

Sign in once. The Hub handles the rest — reverse-tunnel relay, NAT traversal, no port-forwarding required.

What the Hub does

Run Phlix on your LAN at home. The Hub's reverse-tunnel relay means your phone, your Roku at a friend's house, or your laptop on a business trip all connect to the same server — without touching your router's settings.

The Hub is the directory that finds your server and the tunnel that keeps the connection open. Sign in once, pick your server, and your client connects directly through the relay.

0 Port-forwarding needed
TLS Encrypted tunnel
Self-host Or use the public hub

Two ways to run

Use the public Hub

No setup. Point your server at hub.phlix.io and your clients at the same. Works out of the box — your server registers itself and your clients find it.

Self-host the Hub

Run phlix-hub on a VPS or a always-on machine. Full control over the relay, no third-party in the middle.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client — Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile — supports Hub mode. Enable it once in settings, sign in, and the client always finds your server regardless of where you are.

Hub mode is supported in all five clients: Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile, and via DLNA discovery over the relay as well.

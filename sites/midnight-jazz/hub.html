Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. It solves the NAT problem: your server sits behind your router, and your phone sits somewhere else entirely. Hub brokers the connection so the two can find each other — without opening a port on your firewall.

How it works

Your server
behind NAT → Phlix Hub
relay + directory → Your client
anywhere

Two ways to run it

1

Use the public hub

The fastest path. Sign in with your Phlix account, point your server at the public relay, and you're done. No infrastructure to manage.

2

Self-host the hub

Run phlix-hub on your own VPS. You control the relay and the directory. The same code, your own server — BSD-3-Clause.

Hub mode in clients

When Hub mode is enabled, your client shows all servers registered to your account — home, cabin, anywhere. Select one and the relay connects you. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no ceremony.

## Notes

- All images use `image_prompt_prefix` + `{subject}` + `image_prompt_suffix` format
- Negative prompts are applied to all generations
- Film grain is mandatory on all photography
- Never generate images with bright or light backgrounds
- Amber is the single accent — never overuse it

Phlix Hub

Access your servers from anywhere — no port forwarding, no third-party tunnel.

What the Hub does

Your media library, accessible from anywhere.

Reverse-Tunnel Relay

Hub maintains an outbound connection to the relay. When you connect, traffic routes through that tunnel — no inbound ports needed.

NAT Traversal

Works behind consumer routers without manual port forwarding. Your server stays on your network; you stay in control.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

Your choice — full control or instant setup.

Public Hub

Sign up once, add your servers, access anywhere. Zero configuration.

Self-Hosted

Run your own Hub instance. All traffic stays on infrastructure you own.

Hub Mode in Clients

All native clients support Hub mode for remote access.

  • Roku — Hub mode included
  • Windows — Hub mode included
  • Mobile (beta) — Hub mode included

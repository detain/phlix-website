About the Storm

About Thunder Strike

Electrical storm powering your media. Built by power users, for power users.

The Story

Thunder Strike was born from a simple idea: media servers should feel powerful. Not just functional, not just useful — actually electric. We wanted something that crackles with energy when you interact with it, something that makes organizing your media feel like commanding a tesla coil.

Built on the Phlix platform, Thunder Strike brings that electric vision to life. Every interaction generates voltage. Every discovery is a small lightning strike illuminating something new. The storm is always there, waiting to strike.

Open source. No subscriptions. No cloud dependency. The power stays in your hands.

Electric FAQ

Is Thunder Strike free?

100% free and open source. No premium tiers, no subscription fees, no hidden charges. The voltage is free.

How does it differ from other media servers?

The aesthetic and the experience. Thunder Strike is designed for users who want their media server to feel as powerful as it is. The electric animations, the plasma glows, the tesla coil aesthetic — it's a complete experience, not just a utility.

Can I self-host?

Absolutely. That's the point. Run it on your own hardware, keep your data on your own servers, own your storm completely.

What about privacy?

No tracking, no telemetry, no recommendations sent to the cloud. Your viewing habits stay in your storm. We're committed to keeping it that way.

How can I contribute?

Check out our GitHub. We welcome contributions of all kinds — code, themes, plugins, documentation, and feedback. The storm grows stronger with every contributor.

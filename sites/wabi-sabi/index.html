The Craft

What Phlix was made to hold.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata from TMDB, TVDB, and Fanart.tv — with a 24-hour cache so the network stays quiet.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together, like ink spreading through water.

Plus Live TV with DVR, adaptive HLS streaming, multi-user profiles, and a plugin system with a versioned contract. See the full craft.

Why imperfection?

A tea bowl falls. It breaks into pieces on a stone floor. In most stories, that is where it ends. In kintsugi, a craftsperson gathers the shards, rejoins them with lacquer mixed with gold, and returns the bowl to use — more beautiful than before, the cracks now the most honest part of it.

Wabi-Sabi was built on that idea. A media library is not a pristine catalog; it is a collection of things watched, half-watched, revisited, and abandoned. The gaps and the imperfections are the story. Phlix was built for the viewer who does not need to finish everything, who is as happy pausing as playing, who finds the library itself a kind of room worth sitting in.

Nothing lasts. Nothing is lost. Nothing is finished.

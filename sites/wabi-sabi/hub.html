The Gateway

Your library, from anywhere.

Phlix Hub signs you in once and connects you to any of your servers — whether they are in the same room or on the other side of an ocean. Self-host it, or use the public relay.

The idea

Your Phlix server lives on your hardware, on your network. That is the point — your media stays yours. But sometimes you want to reach it from elsewhere: a friend's house, a hotel room, a phone on a cellular connection.

Hub solves this with a cloud directory and a reverse-tunnel relay. You sign in once. Hub knows where your servers are. The connection is made — no port-forwarding, no third-party tunnel service required.

If you prefer to run your own relay, Hub is self-hostable too. The source is open, and the architecture is straightforward.

What Hub does

Cloud directory

Sign in once and Hub shows you every server you have registered — at home, at a parent's house, anywhere you have installed a Hub agent.

Reverse-tunnel relay

Hub's relay handles the NAT traversal so you never have to open a port. The connection is encrypted end-to-end; Hub sees only encrypted traffic.

Self-hostable

Run your own Hub instance on a $5 VPS. The phlix-hub repo has everything you need.

Your media stays yours

The relay passes encrypted data only. Hub never stores your media, your library, or your viewing history.

The library is yours, wherever you are.

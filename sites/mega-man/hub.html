Phlix Hub

Access your servers from anywhere. No port forwarding. No third-party tunnel.

What the Hub Does

The Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your home server to the outside world through an outbound connection. Your server initiates the tunnel — no inbound ports need to be opened.

NAT Traversal

Most home connections are behind NAT or CGNAT. The Hub's reverse tunnel bypasses this — your server reaches out to the Hub, and clients reach the Hub to reach your server.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

Run your own Hub instance for full control, or use the public Hub at phlix-hub.detain.io. Both speak the same protocol — clients work with either.

Hub Mode in Clients

Roku, Windows, and Mobile clients all support Hub mode — add your Hub URL once, and all your servers registered to that Hub appear automatically.

Try the Public Hub

No setup required. Point your client at phlix-hub.detain.io.

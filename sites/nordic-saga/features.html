The Arsenal

Every Tool Forged for the Tale

Eight chambers in the hall, each carrying its own weight. From the first frame to the final credit.

01

Library That Organizes Itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) title patterns, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO — all cached 24 hours so the network doesn't slow the library.

folder-watcher · S01E02 parser · TMDB cache

02

SyncPlay Across the Room or Across the Country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Movie night, truly together. No one waits for the buffer while the others have moved on.

NTP offset · weighted mean · 5 samples

03

Transcoding That Picks the Right Quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Direct play when the client supports it; adaptive HLS otherwise.

CRF 23/28 · HLS variants · per-device profiles

04

Multi-User, Multi-Profile, Parental Controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Everyone in the household gets their own hall.

JWT · Argon2ID · 5 profiles · PIN gates

05

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Set it once, come back to the recording.

EPG guide · scheduled recorder · ChannelManager

06

DLNA for the Devices You Already Own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Discovery via SSDP. Play on anything that speaks the language.

SSDP · ContentDirectory · AvTransport

07

Plugin System with a Real Contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. Versioned contract means a plugin written for Phlix 1.x works until you choose to upgrade. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

LifecycleInterface · manifest schema · versioned

08

Phlix Hub — Reach Any of Your Servers from Anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Your library follows you — the hall is wherever you are.

Learn About the Relay

reverse-tunnel · NAT traversal · self-hostable

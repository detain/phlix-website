The Beginning

What Phlix Was Forged to Become

᛭

Phlix began with a simple frustration: the media server landscape offered a binary choice. Use a closed platform and surrender your library to someone else's infrastructure, or self-host something that felt like it was designed in 2005. Neither option was acceptable for something that holds the record of your life in film.

So we built Phlix. PHP 8.3+ and Workerman 5.x, because the language that runs half the web is capable of more than CRUD. Real SyncPlay — not a workaround, not a hack, but NTP-style time synchronization that actually works. A plugin system with a versioned contract that doesn't break on upgrade. And a Hub that lets you reach your own servers from anywhere without becoming a cloud service yourself.

The mission is simple: give every story the weight it deserves. The heft of iron, the reach of myth, the permanence of runes cut into stone. Phlix is the hall where those stories live.

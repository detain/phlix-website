What the Hub does

The Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that solves the NAT traversal problem. Your Phlix server lives on your LAN, invisible to the outside world. The Hub provides a persistent, secure tunnel — so your phone at a friend's house, or your Roku in another city, can reach your server without you punching holes in your firewall or paying for a static IP.

The Hub maintains a lightweight relay between your server and your clients. It handles authentication, session state, and the tunnel lifecycle — and it does it all over a single outbound HTTPS connection from your server.

You connect all your servers and all your clients to the same Hub account. From the Hub's directory, you see every server you run, every client that has ever connected, and the current status of each. One sign-in. Full access to your entire saga, from anywhere on any device.

Self-host or use the public Hub

Public Hub

The public Hub at phlix-hub is free to use. Create an account, point your servers at it, and your clients connect automatically. Zero configuration, maximum reach.

Self-hosted Hub

Run the Hub on your own iron. Self-hosting gives you full control over the relay infrastructure and user accounts. The Hub is also open source — clone it, deploy it, and maintain your own hall's directory.

Hub mode in clients

Every official Phlix client supports Hub mode. When Hub mode is active, the client connects through the Hub rather than directly to your LAN IP. The same sign-in credentials work across all clients and all servers registered to your Hub account.

When Hub mode is disabled — for example, when you are at home on the same network as your server — clients fall back to direct LAN discovery via SSDP, so you never need the Hub to watch locally.

How It Works

The Path of the Relay

Sign In Once

Create an account on the Hub. Connect your servers to it with a single token.

Relay Handles NAT

The reverse tunnel connects your server to the relay. No port forwarding. No firewall wrestling.

Reach from Anywhere

Connect to any of your servers from any client, anywhere. The relay is the bridge.

Self-Hostable

Or Build Your Own Relay

The Hub is open source (MPL-2.0). Deploy it on your own VPS or home server. Keep the directory and the relay within your own infrastructure. The public hub exists for convenience; it is never required.

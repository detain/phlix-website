Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub Does

The Hub serves as a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your Phlix server is behind a NAT or firewall, the Hub lets your clients connect without any manual port forwarding or VPN setup.

Your Clients

Roku, TV, phone, anywhere

  • Sign in once to the Hub — your servers appear automatically
  • Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal automatically
  • Self-host the Hub on your own server, or use the public Hub
  • Secure — traffic is tunneled, not proxied

Self-host or Use the Public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free to use. If you prefer to run your own infrastructure, phlix-hub is open source and self-hostable.

Hub Mode in Clients

All official clients support Hub mode — connect to your server through the Hub from anywhere. Enable Hub mode in the client's connection settings.

  • Roku — Hub mode built-in
  • Samsung Tizen — Hub mode built-in
  • Windows — Hub mode built-in
  • Mobile — Hub mode built-in

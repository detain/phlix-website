Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub solves the NAT traversal problem. When your server is behind a home router, external clients can't reach it directly. The Hub provides a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your server to the outside world — without opening ports or sacrificing privacy.

Once registered with the Hub, your server appears in your client Hub view. Select it, and traffic flows through the relay. The relay handles NAT traversal automatically; your server just maintains an outbound connection to the Hub.

The Hub also provides a cloud directory: a single place to see all your registered servers, regardless of where they're physically located.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

You have options for how you connect:

  • Public Hub: Use the hosted hub at phlix-hub — no setup, zero configuration.
  • Self-Hosted: Run your own hub instance for full control, privacy, or multi-tenant deployment.
  • LAN-Only: Skip the hub entirely and use Phlix on your local network without any external connectivity.
Learn about self-hosting the Hub

Hub Mode in Clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode:

  • Sign in once with your Hub credentials
  • See all your registered servers in one list
  • Select which server to connect to — no port forwarding needed
  • Works with both the public hub and self-hosted hubs

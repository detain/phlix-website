Phlix Hub

Reach your server from anywhere. No port forwarding, no third-party tunnel.

What the Hub does

Your Phlix Server (behind NAT)
Phlix Hub (relay + directory)
Your Client (anywhere)

Phlix Hub handles two things: a cloud directory so your clients can find your server without knowing its IP, and a reverse-tunnel relay so traffic flows even when your server is behind a router it doesn't control.

Self-host or use the public hub

  • Run the public hub at no cost — sign in once, all your servers are reachable
  • Self-host the hub if you want full control — it's open source and runs on any PHP 8.3+ host
  • Hub mode is built into all four native clients — no extra setup

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. Enable it once, and your client connects through the Hub's relay instead of directly to your LAN IP. Works when you're at home, at a friend's house, or on mobile data.

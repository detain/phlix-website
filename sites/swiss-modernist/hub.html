Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere. No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. No third-party relay service required.

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your clients to your server through NAT. You run it alongside your server or on a VPS. Your server opens an outbound connection to the Hub and keeps it alive. Your clients connect to the Hub and are routed to your server through that connection.

The Hub does not store your media. It does not transcode. It routes bytes. Traffic is end-to-end encrypted between your server and your clients.

What the Hub Does

Cloud directory
Register your servers under your account. Each server gets a stable address that your clients can reach regardless of IP changes.
Reverse-tunnel relay
Your server initiates an outbound connection to the Hub. Clients connect to the Hub and are routed through that tunnel. No inbound ports required.
NAT traversal
Symmetric NAT, dual-stack networks, CGNAT — the Hub handles it. If your server can reach the internet, clients can reach your server.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free for personal use. For teams, families, or anyone who wants to own their directory, the Hub is self-hostable. It is a separate PHP application with its own documentation.

Hub mode in the clients toggles between the public Hub and your self-hosted one. Your server configuration determines which Hub it registers with.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. Enable it and the client discovers your registered servers through the Hub. Disable it and the client connects directly to your server's LAN address. Both modes work simultaneously — Hub mode for remote access, direct mode for home streaming.

Built for Impact

Every feature punches through — no gentle touches, no soft landings.

Streaming

Bullet-Proof Streaming

Direct play, transcoding on demand, and adaptive bitrate that adjusts before you notice. Hardware acceleration where available. Your content, delivered at maximum velocity.

  • Direct play for zero transcoding overhead
  • Hardware-accelerated transcoding (QSV, NVENC, VCE)
  • Adaptive bitrate that responds in real-time
  • HDR10 and Dolby Vision support
  • Multiple audio tracks and subtitles
Navigation

Wall Penetration

Penetration reveals punch through categories to expose related content. Infinite depth, instant access. Your library is not a list — it's a space you move through.

  • Nested categories with infinite depth
  • Cross-referencing between related content
  • Quick jump to any point in your library
  • Smart suggestions based on viewing history
  • Resume watching across all devices
Social

SyncPlay Ready

Watch together, apart. SyncPlay keeps everyone on the same frame — synchronized playback across all connected clients. Distance is irrelevant.

  • Frame-accurate synchronization
  • Group chat during playback
  • Host controls for playback
  • No account required for viewers
  • Works through NAT with relay support
Live TV

Live TV + DVR

Over-the-air channels recorded automatically. Your cloud DVR that you control. Schedule recordings, pause live TV, skip commercials. All on your own hardware.

  • EPG integration with program guide
  • Automatic recording of series
  • Commercial detection and skipping
  • Multiple tuner support
  • Streaming to any device
Casting

DLNA / Chromecast

Cast to any screen. DLNA, Chromecast, AirPlay — your content goes everywhere. No proprietary protocols, no vendor lock-in. Open standards only.

  • DLNA/UPnP media rendering
  • Chromecast built-in support
  • AirPlay for Apple devices
  • Automatic device discovery
  • Remote control from phone

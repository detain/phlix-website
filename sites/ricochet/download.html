Requirements

  • OS: Linux (Ubuntu 20.04+, Debian 11+, CentOS 8+)
  • PHP: 8.3 or higher
  • Web Server: Apache 2.4+ or Nginx 1.20+
  • Database: MySQL 8.0+ or MariaDB 10.5+
  • Storage: Depends on your media library
  • RAM: 2GB minimum, 4GB recommended

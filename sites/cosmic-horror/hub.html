The Relay

The Hub observes what your firewall conceals. A reverse tunnel that crosses NAT without ports, without configuration, and without third-party surveillance.

What the Relay Does

The Hub is a lightweight relay. Your Phlix Server at home opens an outbound connection to the Hub and holds it open. When you access the Hub from outside your LAN, it routes your request down that already-open connection to your server. No ports opened. No dynamic DNS configured. The tunnel is established from inside, where NAT cannot prevent it.

NAT Traversal

Connection opened from inside. The router never blocks what it cannot see leaving. No UPnP, no port forwarding, no exposed attack surface.

Reverse Tunnel

Outbound-only connections from server to Hub. The Hub never initiates contact with your LAN. Your servers remain unexposed.

Central Directory

Sign in once to the Hub. See all your servers. Access any one of them without memorising IP addresses or configuring DNS.

Self-hostable

Run your own Hub. The relay is open-source (MPL-2.0). Control the infrastructure or delegate it to the public Hub operated by the project.

Two Ways to Relay

Public Hub

Zero configuration. Point your server at the public Hub address, create an account, and your archive is accessible from anywhere. Operated by the Phlix project. Connection is encrypted.

Self-hosted Hub

Deploy phlix-hub on your own VPS. Full control over the relay infrastructure. Useful for organisations with data-residency requirements or users who prefer not to use third-party infrastructure.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every official client supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects to the Hub rather than to a LAN address directly. The same credentials grant access to all registered servers. Switch between your home server, your parent's server, and your secondary instance seamlessly.

Clients with Hub mode

  • Roku channel — Hub mode available in settings
  • Samsung Tizen app — Hub mode available in settings
  • Windows desktop — Hub mode in system tray menu
  • Mobile (iOS + Android, beta) — Hub mode in account settings

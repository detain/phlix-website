The Relay

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere without touching your router.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay. Your server at home initiates an outbound connection to the Hub and keeps it open. When you want to access your library from outside your network, the Hub routes the request through that connection.

No port forwarding. No third-party tunnel service. No configuring your router.

Two Ways to Use It

Public Hub

Use the shared Hub relay at hub.phlix.dev. Sign in with your Phlix account, add your server, and you're connected. No infrastructure to maintain.

Self-Hosted Hub

Run your own Hub instance. Full control, no shared infrastructure. Deploy on any VPS with Docker or from source.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every official Phlix client supports Hub mode. Enable it in the client settings, sign in, and your server appears — even if it's behind a NAT you can't touch. The client handles the relay connection automatically.

Clients with Hub mode: Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and Mobile (beta).

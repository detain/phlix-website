THE SCENE

Sign in once. Access any of your servers from anywhere — even behind NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub relay.

REVERSE-TUNNEL RELAY

No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. The Hub's reverse-tunnel relay keeps your server reachable from outside your network — on your terms.

⟳

SELF-HOSTABLE

Run your own Hub instance. Own your directory. Phlix Hub is open source (MPL-2.0) — deploy it alongside the server or on a separate host.

⚙

CLOUD DIRECTORY

One login lists every server you own or have been invited to. Select a server and you're connected — no IP addresses, no ports, no configuration.

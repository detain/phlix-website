THE PLUGIN CONTRACT

Phlix uses a versioned manifest contract. Your plugin declares its schema version, its dependencies, and its lifecycle hooks. The loader validates everything before loading.

$schema: "https://phlix.dev/plugin-manifest/v1"
name: "my-metadata-provider"
version: "1.0.0"
lifecycle:
  onServerStart: ["bootstrap"]
  onScanMedia: ["enrich-metadata"]

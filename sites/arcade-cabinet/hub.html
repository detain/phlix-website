Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

Overview

What the Hub does

Reverse-Tunnel Relay

The Hub maintains an outbound connection to the relay server, so your devices can reach your server even when it's behind a NAT or firewall. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS — just sign in and go.

NAT Traversal

Your server stays on your LAN. The Hub's relay handles the connection so you can stream from your home server to your phone at a friend's house — without exposing anything to the public internet.

Options

Self-host or use the public hub

Public Hub

Use the hosted relay at phlix-hub.detain.io. No setup, no maintenance — just sign in and your servers are reachable.

Self-Hosted Hub

Run your own relay. Full control, your own infrastructure. See the Hub admin documentation for setup instructions.

Clients

Hub mode in clients

Every native client supports Hub mode — sign in once, see all your servers.

  • Roku — Enable Hub mode in settings to connect to your hub account
  • Samsung Tizen — Hub mode pairs with your account for remote server access
  • Windows — Sign in via the system tray icon, switch between servers instantly
  • Mobile — Add servers manually or sign in to Hub for automatic discovery

Set up the Hub

Get started with the Hub documentation. Self-hosting or using the public relay — you choose.

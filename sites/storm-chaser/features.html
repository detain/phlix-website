Library Vortex

Content organized in dynamic category funnels. Tags, collections, and smart filters spin together. Add files and watch the storm build.

  • Auto-tag from file metadata
  • Smart collections that evolve
  • Real-time library stats

SyncPlay

Watch together. One session, multiple viewers, all synchronized. A single lightning strike starts playback everywhere.

  • Frame-accurate sync
  • Chat overlay during playback
  • Any client, any platform

Transcode Engine

Storm-transcend any format. Convert on-the-fly to whatever device needs. Hardware acceleration where available.

  • H.264, H.265, VP9, AV1
  • HW acceleration (NVENC, QSV, V4L2)
  • Quality presets from web to 4K HDR

Hub Relay

Bridge your storm across networks. Secure relay access from anywhere without opening ports or paying for cloud.

  • Zero-config remote access
  • End-to-end encryption
  • No subscription required

Live TV

Integrate broadcast TV, IPTV streams, and tuner devices into your vortex. DVR support for scheduled recordings.

  • HDHomeRun, SiliconDust
  • M3U/XMLTV import
  • Automatic commercial skip

Secure Access

Authentication and authorization built in. Users, roles, and fine-grained permissions. Your storm, your rules.

  • JWT and session auth
  • Library-level permissions
  • 2FA support

Position

The storm was built by people who got tired of watching their media through someone else's eyes.

The Story

Phlix started as a personal project. Someone wanted to run their media server their way — no subscription, no cloud dependency, no ads, no tracking. Just a fast, reliable piece of software that did one thing: play media from a server to any screen, anywhere.

Storm Chaser is the identity that grew from that idea. Wild weather. Intense energy. The storm grows with your library because that's exactly what happens — more content means more to manage, more to discover, more chaos to channel into something powerful.

Today, Phlix is run by a small team and a passionate community of contributors who believe media should stay in your hands.

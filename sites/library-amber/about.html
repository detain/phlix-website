About Phlix

A media server built in PHP, for people who prefer to own their infrastructure.

Philosophy

Curation over quantity

Your collection is not a backlog. It is a curated set of things worth watching, ordered by taste and tended with care.

Depth over spectacle

Phlix focuses on the experience of watching well — SyncPlay that actually works, metadata that is accurate, and a library that is pleasant to browse.

Privacy and ownership

Everything runs on your hardware. Your viewing habits stay on your server. The Hub exists only to solve NAT traversal, not to be a third party in your media habits.

Enduring quality

Built on PHP 8.3+ and Workerman 5.x — a foundation chosen for longevity and the ability to self-host without cloud dependencies.

Contributing

Phlix is developed in the open under the detain GitHub org. Issues, pull requests, and plugin submissions are welcome. The contribution guide is in each repository. The smallest way to help is to star the repos you use — it helps others discover the project.

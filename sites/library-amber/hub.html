Phlix Hub

Your library, anywhere in the world. Sign in once and reach any of your servers.

How the Hub works

The Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay — your home server reaches out to it, and your devices reach in through the same tunnel. No ports to open, no dynamic DNS to configure.

Two ways to use the Hub

Use the public Hub

Zero configuration. Point your server at hub.phlix.app and your devices at the same. Sign in with your Hub account and everything connects automatically.

Best for: most users who want remote access without hosting their own infrastructure.

Self-host the Hub

Run phlix-hub on your own VPS. Full control, your own relay, your own user database. See the Hub admin docs for a full setup guide.

Best for: power users and organisations who need complete ownership of their infrastructure.

Hub mode in clients

Native clients (Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile) include Hub mode. Enable it to reach your server when you are away from home — no separate app or step required.

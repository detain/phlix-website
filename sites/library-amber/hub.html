Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere — even behind NAT, even on a cellular connection.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub has two jobs. First, it acts as a cloud directory — a single place where all your devices can discover which servers belong to your account. Second, it runs a reverse-tunnel relay. When your phone is on a different network than your server, the Hub bridges the gap without requiring you to open a port, configure DDNS, or expose your server to the open internet.

Self-host or use the public hub

Use the public hub

No setup required. The public Phlix Hub handles relay and discovery. Create an account, point your server at it, and everything connects automatically.

Self-host the hub

Deploy phlix-hub on any VPS. You control the relay, the directory, and the credentials. Full instructions are in the hub admin docs.

Hub mode in clients

All Phlix clients support Hub mode. Enable it in the client settings, authenticate once, and the client discovers your servers automatically through the Hub directory — whether you're at home or across the country.

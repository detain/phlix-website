Phlix Hub

Your server, anywhere

Sign in once. The Hub connects you to any of your servers — at home, at a friend's house, anywhere in the world.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that sits between your clients and your server. When your server is behind a NAT or firewall — which it almost always is at home — the Hub solves the connectivity problem without requiring you to open ports, configure DDNS, or expose your network.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Phlix Hub at hub.phlix.io is free for anyone to use. All traffic is end-to-end encrypted; the Hub only relays encrypted bytes and never stores your media or credentials.

If you prefer to run your own Hub — because you want full control, or because you run Phlix in an enterprise environment — the Hub is a separate open-source project you can deploy on any VPS.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. When you enable Hub mode, the client connects to your Hub account instead of directly to your server IP. The Hub routes the connection to whichever server you've registered. Switching servers is as simple as selecting a different entry in the Hub dropdown.

You can register multiple servers to a single Hub account — useful if you run a server at home and a travel server on a VPS, or if you manage Phlix for friends and family.

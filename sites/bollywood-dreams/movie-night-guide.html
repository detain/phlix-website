The VIP Treatment

Movie Night Guide

Every great film deserves a grand entrance — and the perfect night deserves the right preparation. Here is how to set the stage.

Setting the Scene

The cinema hall matters as much as the film. Before you press play, take a moment to prepare the room: dim the overhead lights, draw the curtains, and let the darkness become the stage. Marigold-gold accent lighting — a lamp, a candle, a diya — sets the mood without breaking the spell.

Arrange seating so every seat has an unobstructed view. A pillow for each guest is not excess — it is hospitality. The floor should be soft with blankets if seats are sparse. And snacks: there should be snacks. The smell of popcorn belongs to the ritual as much as the opening credits.

SyncPlay — Everyone Watches Together

Phlix's SyncPlay feature keeps every screen locked to the same frame. One person presses play and the room moves together — pause for a bathroom break, seek to rewatch a favourite moment, and the whole group stays synchronized. Weighted-mean NTP timing keeps drift from accumulating, so a three-hour film ends together too.

SyncPlay is not just for the living room. Use the Hub to connect a friend across town, a sibling in another city, or a grandparent who cannot visit. The same candle-lit room feeling, at any distance. Every story deserves an audience — and Phlix makes sure that audience is together.

Curate Your Watchlist

A library organized with care rewards every visit. Phlix fetches accurate metadata and poster art so your collection looks as good as it plays. Create watchlists for occasions — a Diwali selection, a Monsoon Romance double-feature, a Saturday afternoon for the little ones.

Sort by mood or genre. Set the evening's programme in advance so the transition between films flows without a pause. The queue should feel like an usher introducing the next act — seamless, considered, always one step ahead.

Quality Settings for Every Screen

Phlix reads each client device and selects the right encoding profile automatically. The living-room TV may want a high-bitrate direct play while the phone, connected over the Hub from a friend's house, receives a transcoded HLS stream that adapts to the network. You do not need to think about it — Phlix handles the math.

For a dedicated viewing session, you can lock in a quality profile manually from the Playback Settings. Lossless quality for the big screen, efficient for the portable one. The same library, served to every device in its own language.

DLNA for the Open invitation

Not every guest will have the Phlix app installed — and that is fine. Phlix's built-in DLNA server means any DLNA-capable device on your network can receive your library without configuration. A games console, a smart TV from years past, a friend's laptop — they all see your collection and can play from it immediately.

DLNA discovery runs on port 1900/udp automatically. No port forwarding, no account setup, no invitation required — just the network and the library. The most open door in the house.

Live TV and the Scheduled Premiere

Phlix's Live TV and DVR features turn your antenna signal into a channel you can watch anywhere via the Hub. Schedule a recording of a broadcast film screening the same way you would set a VCR — but watch it from any device, at any time. The scheduled premiere is kept.

For a movie night with a difference, combine a live sports broadcast with SyncPlay so everyone's reaction to the winning goal lands in the same second. The ritual of watching together, amplified by the technology that makes distance irrelevant.

The House is Ready

Every seat is taken, the lights are dimming, and the marigolds are fresh. All that remains is to press play.

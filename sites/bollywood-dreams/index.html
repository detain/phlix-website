Now Showing

Two features that set the stage

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Movie night stays locked in sync — every seat, every room, the same frame, the same moment. Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked.

SyncPlay

Library that organizes itself

Drop your films in and watch them take their place on the marquee, organized and ready. Folder-watcher, TMDB/TVDB metadata, 24-hour cache.

Library

Transcoding that picks the right quality

CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265, HLS master and variant playlists, per-device quality profiles — right quality for every connection.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT with Argon2ID, up to 5 profiles per user, 4 or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub — your choice.

Also: DLNA for devices you already own, and a plugin system with a versioned manifest contract.

Why the cinema always wins

  • 100% self-hostable — your library never leaves your hardware unless you say so.
  • Native clients on Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile (beta), plus any DLNA device.
  • Real-time SyncPlay with NTP-style time sync so movie night actually stays in sync.
  • Multi-source metadata: TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO with 24-hour cache.
  • Adaptive HLS streaming, FFmpeg transcoding, per-device quality profiles.
  • Live TV with DVR + EPG guide integration — channel manager, guide, recorder built in.
  • Plugin system with a versioned manifest contract — drop in an extension, the loader picks it up.

Eight pillars of the cinema

  • SyncPlay — NTP-style frame-lock across any distance
  • Library — folder-watcher, multi-source metadata, 24h cache
  • Transcode — CRF 23/28, HLS, per-device quality profiles
  • Auth — JWT, Argon2ID, up to 5 profiles, rating filters
  • Live TV — DVR, EPG guide, channel manager
  • DLNA — ContentDirectory, AvTransport, SSDP discovery
  • Plugins — LifecycleInterface, versioned manifest contract
  • Hub — reverse-tunnel relay, self-hostable or public

Phlix Hub

Access your servers from anywhere — without opening ports or configuring DDNS.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your server to your clients when both are behind NAT. Instead of exposing your server to the internet or setting up complex VPN configurations, you sign in to the Hub once and your devices find each other automatically.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.tv is free to use. If you prefer to run your own relay infrastructure, Phlix Hub is open-source and self-hostable — you control the directory and the tunnel endpoints.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects to your configured Hub and registers your server. When you're away from home, the Hub routes the connection through the relay — no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no hassle.

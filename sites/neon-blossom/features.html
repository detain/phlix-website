What Phlix offers

Eight blooms. Every one electric.

From a library that knows itself to a hub that follows you everywhere — each feature grows from the same conviction: media should feel magical.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear.

What this means in practice

Drop a film in your library folder. Within moments, Phlix scans it, extracts episode or year info, fetches poster and description from TMDB or TVDB, and presents it in a clean grid — no manual metadata entry required.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together.

What this means in practice

Start a movie night. One person hits play and everyone — on Roku, Tizen, Windows, or any DLNA device — sees the same frame at the same moment. No buffering desync, no "wait, you didn't see that?"

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists.

What this means in practice

Stream to an old phone on LTE and Phlix drops to a lower bitrate automatically. Switch to a 4K TV and it ramps up. No manual quality selection needed — it reads the device profile and picks the right encode.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17.

What this means in practice

Family members each have their own profile. Set a PIN on a kids profile to block mature content. All passwords are hashed with a serious key derivation function — not MD5 or a fast hash.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus.

What this means in practice

Feed an antenna signal or an IPTV stream into Phlix and it builds a programme guide automatically. Click a programme to watch live, or set a recording and come back to it later — like a real DVR, not a clunky web wrapper.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app.

What this means in practice

Open the YouTube app on a decade-old Samsung TV. Phlix appears as a media source. No install, no sideload, no special client — just the DLNA protocol your TV already speaks.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example.

What this means in practice

Write a metadata provider, a notification service, or a custom auth backend. The plugin contract is versioned, so updates won't break your plugin without warning.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub.

What this means in practice

Your server sits behind a router at home. The Hub's relay means your phone connects without you forwarding any ports or paying for static IP. Self-host the relay or use the hosted version — your choice.

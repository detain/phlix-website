Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

The Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that solves the NAT traversal problem. Your Phlix server sits on your LAN — it has no public IP, no open ports, and no exposure to the internet. When you want to watch from a friend's house or on the go, the Hub connects you through.

It works by maintaining an outbound connection from your server to the Hub relay. When a client signs in and requests access, the Hub routes the stream through that existing tunnel. No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. No firewall configuration.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free to use and maintained by the project. It handles authentication, device registration, and relay traffic.

If you need more control — or want to run the Hub inside your own infrastructure — it is fully self-hostable. Clone phlix-hub, point it at a server with a public IP, and configure your clients to use your own relay endpoint instead.

Hub mode in clients

Every native client — Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and Mobile — includes Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub rather than directly to your LAN IP. Enable it once, and your server is accessible from anywhere.

On desktop clients (Windows), Hub mode runs in the system tray. On mobile, it is a toggle in settings. On TV platforms, it is a one-time setup when you first add a server.

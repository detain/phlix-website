Features

Everything Phlix does to make your media accessible, organized, and synced across every screen you own.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. Phlix watches your directories and automatically picks up new content without manual scanning.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Whether you're in the same room or across the country, movie night stays in sync.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Per-device quality profiles pick the right bitrate automatically so you always get the best viewing experience.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Keep your family safe while letting everyone have their own watched history and preferences.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Watch live TV, record your favorite shows, and never miss a broadcast with the built-in electronic program guide.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Phlix speaks the universal DLNA language so you can stream to any device that supports it — no install required.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. The versioned manifest means plugins work across updates without breaking your install.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Access your home server from anywhere without configuring port forwards or exposing your IP address.

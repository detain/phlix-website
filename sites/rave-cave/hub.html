Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory plus a reverse-tunnel relay. When your server is behind a NAT or firewall — which it almost always is at home — the Hub lets your clients reach it without configuring port forwards, dynamic DNS, or exposing your IP address.

The Hub uses a reverse tunnel: your server calls out to the Hub and holds a persistent connection. When you open a client, it connects to the Hub and traffic is routed through that existing connection to your server.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.io is free to use and maintained by the project. If you want full control — or if you need to comply with data-residency requirements — you can run your own Hub. The phlix-hub repository has everything you need.

Hub mode in clients

Each native client supports Hub mode. When you sign in with your Hub account, the client discovers all your registered servers automatically and you can switch between them without reconfiguring anything. At a friend's house, on a different network — your library is still there.

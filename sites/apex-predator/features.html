Features

Eight capabilities that make Phlix the most formidable media server in the food chain.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. The library scanner picks up every title you drop, tags it with the right metadata, and gets it ready to stream — without you lifting a finger.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Movie night stays in sync whether your group is in the same room or spread across the country.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Every device gets the quality it can handle — no buffering, no pointless upscaling.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Everyone in the house gets their own profile, their own watchlists, and their own PIN if needed.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Set it once, forget it, come back to a library of recorded shows without hunting for them.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. If it speaks DLNA, Phlix speaks to it. Every screen in the house is a potential target.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. The plugin contract is versioned — what you build today keeps working tomorrow.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Your server sits behind your router, but your phone and your Roku at a friend's house still find it.

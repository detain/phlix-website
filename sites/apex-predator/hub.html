Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub Does

Reverse-Tunnel Relay

Phlix Hub is a directory and relay service that solves the NAT traversal problem. Your server sits behind your router. Your phone and your Roku at a friend's house need to reach it. The Hub establishes a reverse tunnel — your server calls out to the Hub once, and the Hub keeps that connection open for inbound traffic.

You sign in once on the Hub. The Hub knows your servers. The clients ask the Hub where to find your server, and the Hub brokers the connection. No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. No third-party tunnel service.

Two Ways to Hunt

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

Public Hub

Use the hosted hub at phlix-hub. No setup. Point your server at it, create an account, and you're done. The relay runs on infrastructure maintained by the project.

Self-Hosted Hub

Run your own hub instance. Full control. Deploy it on a VPS that has a static IP. Your server directory and relay traffic stay on infrastructure you own.

Client Support

Hub Mode in Clients

The Hub is not a separate product — it's a connection mode built into every Phlix client. When you configure a server, you choose LAN-only or Hub mode. In Hub mode, the client connects through the Hub relay instead of directly to your server's IP.

Roku — Hub mode supported
Windows client — Hub mode supported
Mobile (beta) — Hub mode supported

Download

Get Phlix running on your own hardware.

Server

PHP 8.3+ required. The installer sets up everything on Ubuntu or Debian.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/detain/phlix-server/master/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash

Installs system packages, creates database, configures systemd service, and sets up HTTPS with Let's Encrypt.

The Signal Carries

Eight tools. One principle: you own the transmission.

Every feature here is the same story. The corporate server takes a cut, requires an account, stores your habits on their hardware. Phlix does the opposite. Every line of code is yours to run.

Library

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. No import wizard, no account, no middleman.

SyncPlay

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Movie night doesn't drift.

Transcoding

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. The right quality finds every device automatically.

Auth

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Your library, your family, your rules.

Live TV + DVR

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. The schedule is yours to set.

DLNA

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Phlix speaks the language your hardware already knows.

Plugin System

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. The contract is versioned. The extension is yours.

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-host the hub, or use the public one. Either way, your server stays on your hardware.

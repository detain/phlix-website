Why We Built It

Nobody gave us a platform.

So we built one. With black ink and a photocopier running out of toner. With cut-up type from last month's newspaper. With a safety pin holding the cover together.

The official channels had nothing to say. The mainstream media buried the signal under corporate noise. We turned the signal up. Every screen in this system is a xeroxed page. Every headline is a ransom note to comfort. Every play button is a transmission — no permission required, no apology offered, no corporate sponsor to thank.

Phlix built Editorial Underground for the viewer who already knew: the best things were never on the schedule.

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device on the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together.

Why We Made It

The official channels had nothing to say.
We turned up the signal.

The principle

The best things were never on the schedule. Corporate media servers charge a subscription to rent access to your own library. They track what you watch. They decide what you can watch. They go down when they feel like it, and you can't fix it.

Phlix is the alternative that doesn't require permission. PHP 8.3+ on your own machine. Your library on your own hardware. A Hub you can self-host or use the public relay. Five clients that talk to the server you control.

No corporate filters. No subscription. No one between you and the play button.

Questions Worth Asking

The fine print, dissected.

Is Phlix like Plex / Jellyfin / Emby?
Yes — same job, different stack. Phlix is built in PHP 8.3+ on Workerman, ships with a versioned plugin contract, and includes a hub for accessing remote servers behind NAT without a third-party tunnel.
Do I have to play by their rules?
No. You run your own server. No subscription, no account required to start, no terms of service to accept.
Do I need to expose my server to the internet?
No. Run Phlix on your LAN and use the Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay to reach it from your phone or Roku at a friend's house. You can self-host the hub, or use the public one.
Can they spy on what I watch?
Not if you self-host. The Hub relay sees encrypted traffic and no library data. Your server, your network, your logs.
What formats are supported?
Anything FFmpeg can read. Direct play when the client supports it; transcoded HLS otherwise. Per-device quality profiles pick the right bitrate automatically.
Will my old junk play?
FFmpeg handles most legacy formats. If a device doesn't support the source codec, Phlix transcodes on the fly to something it does support.
Is there a mobile app?
Yes — React Native, available on iOS and Android. Currently in beta.
Can I write plugins?
Yes. Implement LifecycleInterface, ship a manifest, drop it in the plugins directory. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Everywhere

The Hub.

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no third-party tunnel service.

What the Hub does

Your Phlix server sits behind your router. The Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that maintains an outbound connection to a known relay server. When you open the app from anywhere, the Hub connects the two — your request arrives at your server without any holes punched in your firewall.

Your server initiates the connection. Nothing incoming reaches your LAN. The tunnel is encrypted. Your library never passes through the Hub's servers as plain text.

Two ways to run it

Public Hub

Use the hosted relay at hub.phlix.dev. Register your server with one click. No server of your own required. Works out of the box.

View on GitHub →

Self-host the Hub

Run your own relay. Full control. Your traffic never touches a third-party server. See the Hub admin docs for setup instructions.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub rather than directly to your LAN address. The server appears in your Hub dashboard, and you can reach it from anywhere.

Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and Mobile all support Hub mode. Toggle it in the client's connection settings.

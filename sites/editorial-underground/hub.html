Phlix Hub

The copy shop never closed. Neither did your library.

What the Hub does

Sign in once. The Hub's reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal so you can access your server from your phone, your Roku at a friend's house, or any device anywhere in the world. Your library stays on your hardware.

Self-host or use the public hub

You can run your own phlix-hub instance, or use the public relay — no configuration required. The choice is yours.

Hub mode in clients

Every official client supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub relay instead of directly to your LAN server. Flip the switch. Done.

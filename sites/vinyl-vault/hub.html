Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Access any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub Does

The Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your Phlix clients to your Phlix servers — even when your server is behind a NAT or firewall with no port forwarding configured.

Instead of exposing your server to the internet directly, your server initiates an outbound connection to the Hub. Clients connect to the Hub, which routes traffic through the existing tunnel. No ports to open, no dynamic DNS to configure.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

You can run your own Hub instance for full control, or use the free public Hub at hub.phlix.dev. Both work with all Phlix clients — Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile, and any DLNA device.

Self-hosting the Hub is a single additional install step. The Hub is a separate PHP application that can run alongside the main Phlix server or on its own host.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. When Hub mode is enabled, the client connects to the Hub instead of directly to your server's IP address. The first time you use Hub mode, you'll authenticate with your Hub account — all your registered servers appear, and you can switch between them instantly.

When you're on your home network, clients automatically detect local servers and connect directly — no Hub required, no internet latency.

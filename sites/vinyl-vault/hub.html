Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

The Hub is two things: a cloud directory and a reverse-tunnel relay.

Directory: When your Phlix server is behind a NAT or firewall, the Hub gives it a reachable address. You sign in at the Hub once, and your client discovers all your registered servers without any port forwarding or dynamic DNS configuration.

Relay: The Hub's reverse tunnel handles the NAT traversal, relaying traffic between your client and your server. Your server stays on your home network; you access it from anywhere.

How it works

Server (home) → Hub (cloud relay) ← Client (anywhere)

Your server initiates the tunnel to the Hub. The Hub never stores your media — only relays the stream.

◆

Self-host or use the public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free to use. It requires no account — your server registers with a token you generate in the Phlix admin UI.

If you want full control, or if you need to meet specific privacy or compliance requirements, you can run your own Hub instance. The Hub is open source (phlix-hub) and runs on any server that can receive HTTPS connections from your Phlix servers.

Hub mode in clients

Clients that support Hub mode can discover and connect to servers registered with the Hub automatically. When you're away from home, you don't need to know your server's IP address — the Hub handles the discovery.

Current clients with Hub mode: Roku, Windows, Mobile (beta).

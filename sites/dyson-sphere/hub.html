Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory plus reverse-tunnel relay. It solves the NAT traversal problem: when your Phlix server is behind a router with no port forwarding, the Hub connects your clients to it anyway.

You sign in to the Hub once, list your servers, and the Hub brokers the connection. No firewall holes. No third-party tunnel service needed.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at github.com/detain/phlix-hub is free to use. If you prefer to run your own, self-host the Hub on any PHP 8.3+ capable server — same codebase, your own infrastructure.

Hub mode in clients

All Phlix clients support Hub mode: sign in, see all your registered servers, pick one. The client connects directly when on the same LAN, or via the Hub relay when you're away.

No configuration needed. No manual port forwarding. The Hub handles it.

About Phlix

The central hub where all media connects — open source and self-hosted.

The Nexus Story

Phlix was born from a simple frustration: media servers that scatter your files across disconnected silos, forcing you to juggle apps and subscriptions just to watch something.

The Nexus Core concept emerged as the answer: a single, powerful center point where all your media flows together. Not just a media server — a digital nexus that pulls everything together and streams it anywhere.

Everything connects to the core. Everything flows through the core. That's the Phlix way.

Core Diagnostics

How does Phlix compare to Plex or Jellyfin?

Phlix focuses on core functionality done extremely well:SyncPlay, adaptive transcoding, and secure remote access through the Hub. We're lighter weight and designed for power users who want control.

Does Phlix work without exposing my server to the internet?

Yes. The Hub creates a reverse-tunnel relay so you can access your server from anywhere without port forwarding. Your server stays private.

What media formats does Phlix support?

Phlix supports virtually all common formats through FFmpeg. With adaptive transcoding, it can convert any format to work on any device.

Is there a mobile app?

iOS, Android, and Android TV apps are in development. In the meantime, the web interface works on all mobile browsers.

Can I extend Phlix with plugins?

Yes. Phlix has a plugin system with a clear contract. See phlix-plugin-example for the template.

What license is Phlix under?

Phlix Server and the Hub are MPL-2.0. Shared libraries, plugins, and clients are MIT.

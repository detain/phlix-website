Phlix Hub

Reach any of your servers from anywhere. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no compromise.

What the Hub Does

Reverse-tunnel relay

The Hub maintains an outbound connection to a relay server. When you want to access your server, the Hub connects you through that relay — no inbound ports required on your network.

NAT traversal

Your server stays behind your router's NAT. The Hub's reverse tunnel bypasses it entirely — the same technique used by team viewers and game relays.

Single sign-on

Sign in once to the Hub. From there, you can reach any of your registered servers — or servers shared with you by friends and family.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The Hub is available two ways:

  • Public Hub

    Use the hosted relay at hub.phlix.tv. No setup, no maintenance.

  • Self-hosted

    Run your own Hub. Full control, private relay, same protocol.

Hub mode in clients

All four native clients support Hub mode. When enabled, they connect through the Hub relay instead of directly to your server. Your server can sit behind any router and still be reachable.

Enable Hub mode in each client's settings. The client will register with your Hub account and route all traffic through the relay automatically.

Reach everywhere

One server, accessible anywhere. No firewall changes required.

Plugins

Phlix has a real plugin contract — not just a folder and a prayer.

Plugin Model

Every plugin implements LifecycleInterface and ships a manifest. Drop it in the plugins/ directory and the loader picks it up automatically.

The manifest declares your plugin's version, dependencies, and lifecycle hooks. Phlix handles discovery, initialization, and graceful shutdown — you just write the logic.

Ecosystem Plugins

The community maintains plugins for metadata providers, subtitle fetchers, channel integrations, and more.

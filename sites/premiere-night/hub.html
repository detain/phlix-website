What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your server is behind a NAT or firewall, the Hub lets your clients reach it without any port forwarding or dynamic DNS configuration.

The Hub maintains a directory of your servers. When you connect a client, it authenticates against the Hub and gets a tunnel to your server. All traffic flows through the relay — your server's IP stays private.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.app is free for personal use. If you need more control, you can self-host the Hub on your own infrastructure.

Hub mode is built into all Phlix clients — just sign in and your registered servers appear automatically.

Installation

Open Your Portal

Three simple steps to cross the threshold. The void awaits on the other side.

01

Pull the Image

Download the Void Walker portal container. It contains everything you need to open your dimensional gateway.

docker pull voidwalker/portal
02

Configure

Set your media directory and dimensional parameters. The membrane will form around your content.

VOLUME=/path/to/media ./portal init
03

Traverse

Access the void. Your portal is open. All your media exists in the membrane between dimensions.

http://localhost:8080/void

The Phlix Hub — traversing the membrane to reach your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that handles NAT traversal. Sign in once, and the Hub connects you to any of your servers behind restrictive networks — without opening ports or configuring tunnels. Your server stays firewalled; the Hub reaches through the membrane.

Self-host or Use the Public Hub

The Hub is self-hostable — run phlix-hub on any PHP 8.3+ host and connect your servers to it. Or use the public Hub at the default endpoint for zero-configuration access. Your Hub, your rules.

Hub Mode in Clients

Native clients (Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile) support Hub mode — register your server with a Hub, and the client discovers it automatically through the relay. No manual IP addresses, no port forwarding.

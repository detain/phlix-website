# Image Generation Prompts

This directory contains prompts used to generate image assets for the Void Walker brand kit.

## og.png (Social Share Image)

**Source:** `og.svg`

**Prompt for regeneration:**
Dimensional void visualization, liminal space between dimensions, deep void-black center (#0A0A0F), swirling violet portal energy (#6C63FF), dimensional membrane texture, floating debris particles, void walker palette (deep void #0A0A0F, dimensional surface #1A1A2E, shadow purple #2D2D44, rift violet #6C63FF, shadow coral #FF6B6B), rift glow, dimensional effects, portal swirl motion, high contrast, 4K.

**Negative prompt:**
warm tones, golden hour, daylight, earthy, sunny, cozy, playful, cute, pastel, white background, bright neon, flat minimal, cartoon, soft, gentle, bouncy, solid ground.

**Output:** 1200x630 PNG

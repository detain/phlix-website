Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere in the world — no port forwarding, no third-party tunnel service.

What the Hub Does

The Hub is a cloud directory plus a reverse-tunnel relay. When your server starts, it establishes an outbound connection to the Hub. When you open the app, the Hub routes traffic through that connection — so you never have to open an inbound port.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free to use. If you want full control, self-host the Hub on your own VPS — it's a separate PHP application that takes about five minutes to install.

Self-hosting the Hub is ideal if you want audit logs, custom domain integration, or if you simply prefer to own your infrastructure.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. When you sign in with your Hub credentials, the client automatically discovers and connects to your registered servers — no IP addresses, no port configuration, no manual setup.

  • Sign in once with your Hub account
  • All your registered servers appear automatically
  • Switch between servers with one tap
  • Works from anywhere — same experience at home or abroad

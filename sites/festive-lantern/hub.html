Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

The Hub's reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal so you can access your server from your phone, your Roku at a friend's house, or any device anywhere in the world — without configuring your router or exposing ports.

Self-host or use the public relay

You can run your own phlix-hub instance, or use the public relay — no configuration required. Either way, the Hub is a thin relay: it never touches your media or your credentials.

Hub mode in clients

Every official client supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub relay instead of directly to your LAN server.

Your server stays behind your firewall. The Hub relay bridges the connection — securely, with zero configuration.

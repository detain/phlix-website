Your server, anywhere you are

Sign in once. The Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay reaches your server behind NAT and firewalls — no port-forwarding, no configuration. Self-host the hub, or use the public one.

Your Phone, Tablet, Roku at a Friend's House

No port-forwarding needed

Your Phlix server initiates an outbound connection to the Hub relay. The Hub maintains a directory of connected servers. When you open the app, the Hub brokers the connection — you're never directly exposed to the internet.

Self-host or use the public hub

The phlix-hub repo is open source. Run your own relay for full control, or connect to the public Hub operated by the Phlix team — free for individual use.

Hub mode in every client

Every Phlix client — Roku, Tizen, Windows, Mobile — can operate in Hub mode. Sign in once, and your server appears wherever you go.

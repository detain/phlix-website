Gathering Guide

How to Host a Watch Party

Light the lantern, gather your guests, and begin. This is your walkthrough to hosting the perfect watch party — from first install to the final credits.

Prepare the Festival

1

Light your Phlix server

One line on a fresh Ubuntu or Debian host installs everything you need — PHP 8.3+, MySQL, FFmpeg, and a systemd service that starts automatically. 

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/detain/phlix-server/master/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash

The installer provisions HTTPS automatically with Let's Encrypt. Your festival runs on port 8096 behind a reverse proxy on :80/:443.

2

Open the Viewing Pavilions

Phlix reaches every screen in the house. Install a client on each device your guests will use:

  • Roku — Native channel with HLS playback and SyncPlay built in
  • Samsung Tizen — Smart TV app, direct play when the device can handle it
  • Windows — Desktop app with system tray and media key support
  • Mobile (iOS + Android) — Beta apps with offline downloads
  • DLNA — No install needed; any DLNA device on your network finds Phlix automatically
3

Set up profiles and parental controls

Create up to 5 profiles per user. Set 4- or 6-digit PINs for each profile and filter media by rating — from G to NC-17. Everyone gets their own lantern, their own watchlist.

Gather Your Guests

4

Create your watch party room

SyncPlay keeps every device locked to the same frame — play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. The weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples means movie night actually stays in sync, whether your guests are in the next room or across the country.

For guests outside your home network, Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay handles the NAT traversal. Self-host the Hub, or use the public one. Everyone signs in once and the relay connects them to your server.

5

Add your media to the library

Drop files into your watch folder. The folder-watcher hashes mtimes and the scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) title formats automatically. Multi-source metadata — TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO — keeps your library looking like a proper festival display. A 24-hour cache means the metadata fetch only happens once per item.

6

Start the stream

Adaptive HLS streaming and FFmpeg transcoding pick the right quality for each device automatically. Mobile-low, mobile-high, web, or TV-4K — QualitySelector profiles handle the selection. Direct play when the client and file format allow; transcoded HLS when they don't.

Enjoy the Festival

7

Sit back and watch together

No third-party gaze, no ads interrupting the festival. Just the warmth of watching together — whether it's a family movie night, a remote viewing with friends across the country, or a late-night documentary with someone who appreciates a good film.

Festival Tips

  • Live TV with DVR + EPG integration means you can schedule recordings of must-see broadcasts and watch them later with your guests.
  • The plugin system lets you extend Phlix with metadata providers, new UI panels, or custom integrations — drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up.
  • 100% self-hostable — your library never leaves your hardware unless you say so. Every lantern burns on your own server.

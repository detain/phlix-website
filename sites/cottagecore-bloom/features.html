What Grows Here

Eight blooms, each tending a different corner of your garden. Take your time — there's always something wonderful to discover.

Library that organizes itself

Every book, film, and song gathers itself as you add it — no cataloging needed, just growth. Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, the scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) title formats, and ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Drop a file onto the server and it appears in your library, blooming into place.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Movie night stays in perfect step across every room — like hearts beating as one. A weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together, whether they're in the same cottage or halfway across the county.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Direct play when the client can handle it; gentle transcoding when it can't. Every bloom finds its own light.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, and a rating filter from G to NC-17. Every member of the household has their own corner of the garden.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Set it once and forget it — the garden tends itself.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. It finds Phlix on the network and opens its doors wide — a guest room that was always there.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. The contract is versioned, the API is documented, and the garden knows how to welcome new arrivals. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Your garden follows you to a friend's house, a holiday cottage, or the train — the same gentle gate, always open.

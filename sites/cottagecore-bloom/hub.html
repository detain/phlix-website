The Gatehouse

The door that connects your garden to the world beyond — open when you need it, closed when you don't.

What the Gatehouse does

A door through the garden wall

Most home networks are closed gardens — the outside world can't reach your server because NAT and firewalls are in the way. The Gatehouse (Phlix Hub) is a reverse-tunnel relay that solves this without opening a single port on your router.

When your Phlix server starts, it reaches out to the Hub and holds a persistent connection. When you open the Hub app or connect from a client in Hub mode, the Hub routes traffic through that connection — to your server, and back. NAT traversal happens automatically, no router configuration needed.

Self-host or use the public Hub

Your gate, your gatehouse

You have choices about who runs the Gatehouse:

  • Use the public Hub — zero setup. Your server reaches the public relay at phlix-hub. The relay is open source and the public instance is free to use.
  • Self-host the Hub — run phlix-hub on a VPS or always-on machine. You control the relay entirely.
  • LAN only — if you never need remote access, you don't need the Hub at all. Everything works on your local network without it.

Hub mode in the clients

Every guest room can find the gate

All native clients (Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile) support Hub mode. When Hub mode is enabled, the client reaches your server through the Hub relay instead of looking for it on the local network. The same credentials, the same library, the same SyncPlay — just a different path to the gate.

Hub mode is optional and can be toggled per client. At home on the LAN? Leave it off and connect directly. On the train? Enable Hub mode and the Gatehouse opens the path.

Ready to open your gate?

The server install takes one line. Hub mode is one toggle.

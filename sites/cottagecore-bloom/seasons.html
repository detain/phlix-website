A guide to the year

Blooming Through the Year

This site does not look the same in November as it does in April. Cottagecore Bloom carries three seasonal moods on top of its summer base, and it changes over on its own when the date arrives.

Four moods, one garden

These are the theme's own colours and motifs — the way this garden dresses for the weather. Phlix itself works exactly the same in every season.

Spring Awakening

15 March – 15 May

Fresh and bright — newly-opened blossoms, blossom-pink and young-leaf green; Primrose is mid-flight amid cherry blossoms and tulips; lighter and airier.

Every season, something new is blooming.

  • #D46A82 in place of Garden Rose
  • #8AB878 in place of Sage Green
  • #A090CC in place of Lavender Mist

Cottagecore Bloom

The rest of the year — high summer, and the garden at rest

The base garden: rose, sage and lavender on a linen-cream ground, foxgloves and sweet peas in a jam jar, Primrose working through the lavender.

Put the kettle on. It can wait.

  • #C8556A Garden Rose
  • #7A9E6B Sage Green
  • #8B7AB5 Lavender Mist

Harvest Home

15 September – 31 October

Warm amber and russet tones; illustrated hedgerow berries, rosehips, and autumn leaves replace the summer florals; Primrose wears a tiny acorn cap.

Gathered by hand. Watched by heart.

  • #B8621A in place of Garden Rose
  • #7A9E6B Sage Green, unchanged
  • #FFF0DC in place of Garden Cream
  • #FFF8EE in place of Warm Ivory

Midwinter Hearth

1 December – 6 January

Deep berry rose and pine-dark sage; illustrated sprigs of holly, mistletoe, and dried orange slices; beeswax candles and a fire-lit hearth warmth.

Come in through the garden gate.

  • #8B4A5A in place of Garden Rose
  • #5A7A4A in place of Sage Green
  • #A08DB0 in place of Lavender Mist
  • #FFF0EA in place of Garden Cream

How the change happens

A few lines of script check the date and swap the palette tokens and the motif. There is no server involved and nothing is stored about you. If you would rather the garden stayed still, Quiet the Garden in the footer stops the drifting petals, the parallax and Primrose entirely — and your system's reduced-motion setting is honoured whether you use the toggle or not.

Blooming Through the Year

The cottagecore bloom shifts with the seasons — each one a different mood of the same warm garden, from harvest amber to midwinter rose to spring blossom.

Seasonal variants

Harvest Home

September 15 — October 31

Warm amber and russet tones replace the summer florals. Illustrated hedgerow berries, rosehips, and autumn leaves frame the garden. Primrose wears a tiny acorn cap. The mood is a cottage kitchen in October — the last of the harvest, a loaf baking, the evening drawing in.

Activates when the date is between September 15 and October 31.

Midwinter Hearth

December 1 — January 6

Deep berry rose and pine-dark sage. The garden is illustrated with sprigs of holly, mistletoe, and dried orange slices. Beeswax candles and a fire-lit hearth warmth replace the summer light. The mood is a cottage on Christmas Eve — quiet, warm, and full of gentle anticipation.

Activates when the date is between December 1 and January 6.

Spring Awakening

March 15 — May 15

Fresh and bright — newly-opened blossoms, blossom-pink and young-leaf green. Primrose is mid-flight amid cherry blossoms and tulips. The mood is a cottage garden in April — everything is returning, nothing is finished, and that's exactly the point.

Activates when the date is between March 15 and May 15.

Every season is automatically applied by the live JavaScript — no download, no switching. When the date enters a season's range, the garden shifts its palette to match.

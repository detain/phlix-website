Behind the wall

Crew Hub

The wall follows you. Sign in once, and your server is always within reach — whether you're around the corner or across the country.

What the Hub does

Your server — on your LAN

Runs on your hardware, behind your router. Nothing exposed to the internet unless you choose.

↘

Reverse-tunnel relay

The Hub keeps an outbound connection open to the relay. You connect to the relay — it routes to your server through the tunnel.

↘

You — anywhere

Your Roku at a friend's house, your phone at a café. Same wall, same library. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS.

Two ways to run it

Public Hub

Use the hosted relay at phlix-hub. Zero setup. Works out of the box with every client. Sign in once and every device you own finds your server automatically.

Self-host the Hub

Run phlix-hub on any VPS. Full control. Point your clients at your own relay and the relay at your server.

Hub mode in the clients

Every Phlix client has Hub mode built in. When Hub mode is on, the client connects through the relay instead of directly to your LAN IP. Toggle it on when you're away, off when you're home.

Roku — Hub mode toggle in settings
Samsung Tizen — Hub mode toggle in settings
Windows — system tray icon → Hub mode
Mobile — profile menu → Hub mode

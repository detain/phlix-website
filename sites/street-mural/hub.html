Your wall, anywhere

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere — no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no third-party tunnel service.

What the Hub does

The Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your Phlix server starts, it opens an outbound connection to the Hub and keeps it alive. When you open your client from anywhere, the Hub routes traffic through that already-open tunnel — NAT traversal handled, no firewall config needed.

Your server at home

Phlix server runs on your hardware, on your LAN. No public IP exposure needed.

↓

Outbound tunnel opens

Server reaches Hub via HTTPS. Hub learns your server's public endpoint. No inbound ports required.

↓

Client connects through Hub

Your phone or Roku away from home talks to the Hub, which proxies to your server over the open tunnel.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The public Hub at phlix-hub is free to use. If you want full control, you can deploy your own Hub instance — it's open-source and self-hostable the same way the server is.

phlix-hub on GitHub

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. When you sign in with your Hub credentials, the client automatically discovers and connects to your registered servers through the Hub — whether you're on your LAN or on the other side of the world. Toggle it in the client's connection settings.

Phlix Hub

Reach your server from anywhere — without opening a port.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your server is behind a NAT or firewall, the Hub gives your clients a reliable way to find and connect to it — without you having to configure port forwarding or expose your network.

Your Server

Runs on your LAN at home, behind a NAT or firewall.

→

Phlix Hub Relay

Public relay handles the connection — no port forwarding needed.

→

Your Client

Connects from anywhere — phone at a friend's house, Roku on the road.

Two ways to use the Hub

Public Hub

Use the shared public Hub at hub.phlix.dev — no setup required. Register your server once, sign in on any client, and you're connected everywhere.

Free tier available. Server registration is authenticated via your Phlix account token.

Self-hosted Hub

Run phlix-hub on your own VPS or home server. Full control over the relay, no data leaves your infrastructure.

Docker image available. See the self-hosting guide.

Hub mode in clients

  • Roku

    Enable Hub mode in settings — the channel will register with your Hub and appear for remote access.

  • Windows

    Hub connection is configured in the app settings under "Remote Access".

  • Mobile (beta)

    Hub auth is handled during the initial account sign-in flow.

  • DLNA

    DLNA discovery is LAN-only. For remote access, use a client with Hub mode enabled.

Set up remote access

Install Phlix Server, register it with the Hub, and stream from anywhere.

How to Curate Your Collection

A private media collection is not a pile of files. It is a curated whole — arranged with the same care a master gives to the works on their studio wall. This guide is a walkthrough in four chapters.

I

Name the collection before you fill it

Phlix organises itself from your folder structure. Before adding a single file, decide on a naming convention that tells the scanner what a title is, what a season is, and what a year is. The scanner parses S01E02 patterns and (2020) year markers automatically — give it something to work with and it will.

II

Let metadata do the work

Phlix fetches from TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO files — and caches results for 24 hours. The moment you drop a correctly-named file into the library, it begins pulling together the full context of the work: poster art, backdrop images, synopsis, cast. A well-formed title generates a complete catalogue entry without any manual typing.

III

Profiles: everyone has their own atelier

Up to five profiles per user, each with its own PIN. Set a rating filter — from G to NC-17 — and the collection adapts without you needing to manage separate libraries. A family library that shows only what should be shown, and a personal library that shows everything. One server. Separate studios.

IV

Watch together across distances

Phlix SyncPlay keeps every device on the same frame — weighted-mean NTP offset over five samples, so the sync does not drift. Three friends, three cities, one screening. Press play on one device and everyone arrives at the same moment, as if they were seated in the same atelier.

The tools on the bench

Library

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes. Scanner parses your naming convention and builds the catalogue automatically.

SyncPlay

NTP-style time sync keeps every connected device on the same frame, in any city.

Transcoding

FFmpeg profiles pick the right quality for each device — from mobile screens to 4K televisions.

Multi-profile auth

JWT with Argon2ID, per-user profiles, PINs, and a rating filter from G to NC-17.

Plugins

LifecycleInterface + manifest. Drop a plugin into the plugins directory and the loader picks it up on restart.

Hub remote access

Reverse-tunnel relay means your collection reaches you without a single opened port on your router.

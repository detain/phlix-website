The Palette

Eight capabilities, each a distinct pigment — placed with the care of a master preparing a canvas. Every tool serves the work.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear — no manual cataloguing required.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together, whether they are in the same room or across the country.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Every transfer lands at the right bitrate for the device requesting it.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, and a rating filter from G to NC-17. Everyone has their own space; nothing strays where it should not.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that does not make you click through menus. The evening programme is waiting when you are.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV does not need a new app. Discovery happens over SSDP. The library is already there when you turn on the screen.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. The contract is versioned and documented — see phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Your server at home follows you to a friend's house, a hotel, or a second residence.

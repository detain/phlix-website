The Relay

Phlix Hub is the reverse-tunnel that carries your collection to wherever you are — without opening a port, without a third-party relay, without compromise.

What the Hub does

Most homes run Phlix behind a NAT router with no publicly reachable IP. The Hub solves the last-mile problem: a persistent outbound connection from your server to the Hub relay, through which clients reach it. No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. No TLS certificates to manage.

The Hub also maintains the directory — a lightweight registry of your servers, so you sign in once and the clients find your collection without you remembering or typing an IP address.

Self-host or use the public Hub

Public Hub

Zero configuration. The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is available to all Phlix users. Sign in, register your server, and it appears on every client.

Self-hosted Hub

Run your own relay. phlix-hub is open source, and the installation mirrors the server install. Full control, no data leaves your infrastructure.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub rather than directly to your server — which means it works even when you are away from home.

On first run, each client prompts you to sign in to the Hub. After that, the server appears automatically — no IP address, no port, no configuration needed on the client side.

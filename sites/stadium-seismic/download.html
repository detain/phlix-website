Download

Get Phlix running on your own hardware in minutes.

Server

PHP 8.3+, MySQL, FFmpeg. The installer handles everything on Ubuntu or Debian.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/detain/phlix-server/master/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash

One line, on a fresh Ubuntu or Debian host. Installs PHP 8.3+, MySQL, FFmpeg, creates a dedicated phlix system user, sets up a MySQL database, runs migrations, and configures a systemd service with HAProxy and Let's Encrypt.

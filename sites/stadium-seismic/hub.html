Phlix Hub

Access your media from anywhere, without opening a single port.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your server to your clients when neither can accept incoming connections. Your server dials out to the Hub once; your phone dials out to the Hub once; the Hub stitches the two streams together. No NAT traversal configuration, no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS.

Self-host or use the public hub

The Hub is open-source (phlix-hub on GitHub). Run your own instance on a VPS, and you own the directory and the relay. Or use the public Hub operated by the project — sign in once, add your servers, and you're connected.

Hub mode in clients

Roku, Windows, and Mobile clients all support Hub mode. When Hub mode is enabled, the client connects through the Hub instead of directly to your LAN IP. The library view, playback controls, and SyncPlay all work identically — the tunnel is invisible to the user.

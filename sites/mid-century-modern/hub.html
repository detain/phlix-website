Remote Access

Phlix Hub.
Reach Any Server,
From Anywhere.

Sign in once. The reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal for you. Your server stays on your hardware; your library follows you anywhere.

Self-Hostable

Run phlix-hub on your own VPS or LAN. You control the directory and the relay — no third-party touching your stream.

Public Hub

Use the hosted hub at phlix-hub.dev — free, maintained by the project, or replace it with your own in one config change.

No Port Forwarding

The relay bridges your LAN server to the outside world. No router config, no dynamic DNS, no security hole.

One tunnel, every device.

Whether you're on a phone at the airport, a tablet at a friend's house, or a Roku in the living room — Hub mode connects you to the same server, the same library, the same experience.

View phlix-hub on GitHub →

The future is owned, not rented.

Phlix Server and the Hub are MPL-2.0 — open source, and you can build on them commercially. If you modify a Phlix file, that file stays open; anything you add alongside it is yours. The shared libraries, plugins, and clients are MIT so you can build on them freely.

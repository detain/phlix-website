Mission Log

Why We Built This.

Mid-Century Modern was the era that believed the future was a friendly place. That optimism lives in Phlix's design: the clean geometry, the warm palette, the confidence that a well-designed tool makes life better.

Phlix is built for the viewer who understands that watching great film and television is an act of optimism — a belief that human creativity is worth your time. Every surface, every curve, every decision is a small argument that the future is still a friendly place.

For you, not them

You own your library.
Not a subscription.

Phlix Server and the Hub are MPL-2.0 — open source, and you can build on them commercially. If you modify a Phlix file, that file stays open; anything you add alongside it is yours.

The shared libraries, plugins, and clients are MIT so you can build on them freely. No subscription, no gatekeepers, no data harvesting.

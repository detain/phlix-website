Phlix Hub

Access your server from anywhere. No port forwarding. No third-party tunnel.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your home server to the outside world without requiring you to open ports or configure NAT. Sign in once, and your Roku at a friend's house can reach your server behind your home router.

NAT traversal

The Hub handles the NAT problem so you don't have to. Your server initiates an outbound connection to the Hub, and the Hub brokers the session. No inbound ports required on your firewall.

Self-host or use the public hub

You can run your own Hub instance for full control, or use the public Hub at hub.phlix.dev. Both speak the same protocol — the client doesn't care which Hub you're using.

View phlix-hub on GitHub

Hub mode in clients

All official clients support Hub mode. When enabled, they discover your registered servers through the Hub instead of requiring a direct LAN connection. Your media travels the same path it always does — through your server — but the connection is brokered by the Hub.

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere. No port forwarding required.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub provides two key functions:

  • Cloud directory — a central registry that tracks your servers so you can find them without remembering IP addresses.
  • Reverse-tunnel relay — handles NAT traversal so your phone or Roku at a friend's house can reach your server behind your home router's firewall.

You can self-host the Hub on your own infrastructure, or use the public Hub maintained by the Phlix team.

Hub Mode in Clients

Native clients (Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows) support Hub mode — once signed in, they automatically find your registered servers through the Hub relay. No manual network configuration required.

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere. Self-hostable, or use the public hub.

Cloud Directory

One account to rule all your servers. List your servers, see their status, jump straight to the web UI — whether they're on your LAN or across the world.

Reverse-Tunnel Relay

Your server reaches out to the Hub once. The Hub brokers the connection. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no exposing your server to the internet.

Self-Hostable

Run your own Hub instance. Control your own relay infrastructure. The Hub is open-source (MPL-2.0) and designed for easy deployment.

Share with Family

Grant access to your family without handing over server credentials. They sign in through the Hub and connect to your server seamlessly.

Ready to Hub?

Install Phlix Server with Hub support built in, or add Hub to an existing installation.

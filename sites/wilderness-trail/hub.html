Phlix Hub

Your server, anywhere.

No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. Sign in once and reach any of your servers from any network, through any device.

How it works

The relay station.

Your server connects out

Phlix Server opens an outbound connection to the Hub relay. It works through NAT, CG-NAT, and firewalls — because it only needs to reach one address.

Your device connects in

Any Phlix client signs into the Hub with the same account. The relay routes your session to the correct server — no manual tunnel management.

Full self-host option

Run phlix-hub on any VPS. The protocol is open. You get the relay without the third party.

Real trails

Use it from the trail.

Reach your home server from a friend's house — no router config needed.

Watch Live TV from a hotel on your phone, through your home server's guide.

SyncPlay works through the relay — everyone stays locked, wherever they are.

Connect the relay.

The Hub is configured from the Phlix Server settings panel. One address, one sign-in, always connected.

Phlix Hub

Your library. Anywhere.

Sign in once. Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay reaches your server behind NAT from your phone at a friend's house or your Roku in another city.

How it works

Zero-config remote access.

1

Run Phlix on your LAN

Phlix Server stays on your home network, safe behind your router. No ports forwarded, no static IP needed.

2

Connect to Hub

Your server registers with Hub via an outbound TLS connection. Hub assigns it a secure, private identifier.

3

Access from anywhere

Sign in to Hub once. Your clients (Roku, mobile, desktop) discover your server through the relay — no manual configuration on any device.

Capabilities

What's included.

Relay

Reverse-tunnel relay

Outbound TLS connection from your server to Hub. All traffic is end-to-end encrypted — Hub sees only encrypted tunneled data.

Directory

Server directory

Sign in once to Hub and see all your registered servers. No need to remember IPs or hostnames.

Self-host

Run your own Hub

Phlix Hub is open-source (MPL-2.0). Self-host it on your own infrastructure if you want zero third-party involvement.

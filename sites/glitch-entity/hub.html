Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your server is behind a NAT or firewall — which it always is at home — the Hub lets your clients reach it without configuring port forwards or using a third-party tunnel service.

Key capabilities

  • Reverse-tunnel relay — Your server initiates an outbound connection to the Hub, establishing a tunnel that persists
  • NAT traversal — No port forwarding, no UPnP, no firewall holes
  • Cloud directory — One place to find all your servers, whether they're at home, at a friend's place, or in a data center

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free to use. If you want full control — or if you need to comply with data-residency requirements — you can self-host the Hub. It's the same software, and it's MIT licensed.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode: sign in once, and every server registered to your account appears automatically. Switch between your home server, your parents' server, and your cabin server with one tap.

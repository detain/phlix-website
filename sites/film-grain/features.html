Features

Everything you need to stream your media, your way — without giving a third party the keys to your library.

What Phlix does

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. No manual metadata entry, no waiting for scans to finish before you can play something.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. No one sits through a scene that already ended while others catch up.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Phlix figures out what your device can play and sends the best file it can handle.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Share your server with family without sharing your watch history.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Set it once, forget it until you want to watch.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Your library appears the same way it would on a native smart TV app — because DLNA has been the baseline for a decade.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. The contract is versioned, so a plugin written for Phlix 1.x keeps working when you upgrade to 2.x. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal so you don't have to open ports or configure DDNS. Self-host the hub on your own VPS, or use the public hub — your server, your rules.

Start streaming on your terms

One line install, no account required, runs on your own hardware.

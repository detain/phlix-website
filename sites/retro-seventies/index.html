Now Spinning

Eight solid features

Each one drops a different record on the turntable — pick your groove.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes. Add a file, watch it land on the shelf.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset keeps every device locked to the same frame.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

CRF 23/28 profiles for mobile-low to TV-4K. Adaptive HLS with FFmpeg.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth, Argon2ID hashing, PINs and rating filters. Up to 5 profiles per user.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory and AvTransport mean your old smart TV needs no new app.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up.

Phlix Hub — reach any server from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable or use the public hub.

Why Retro?

Before the algorithm, there was a ritual.

You walked to the shelf. You picked the record. You held it by its edges. You read the liner notes. You committed.

Phlix Retro Seventies brings that warmth to your streaming — the deliberate joy of choosing, the unhurried pleasure of settling in, the good stuff watched all the way through.

This is not a streaming app wearing bell bottoms. It is a genuine celebration of the era when movies were an event and television was magic.

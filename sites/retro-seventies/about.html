The Story

Before the algorithm, there was a ritual.

You walked to the shelf. You picked the record. You held it by its edges. You read the liner notes. You committed.

Phlix Retro Seventies was built for that feeling — the warm ritual of choosing, the analog pleasure of settling in, the unhurried joy of watching something all the way through.

The 1970s were imperfect and groovy and full of creative courage. Every colour, every curve, every warm wood texture says: slow down, drop the needle, let the good stuff wash over you.

Phlix is not a streaming app wearing bell bottoms. It is a genuine celebration of the era when movies were an event and television was magic. Your media. Your library. Your Phlix — rewound, replayed, relived.

Philosophy

Open source, file-level weak copyleft

Phlix Server and the Hub are MPL-2.0 — open source, and you can build on them commercially. If you modify a Phlix file, that file stays open. Anything you add alongside it is yours.

The shared libraries, plugins, and clients are MIT — so you can build on them freely, without any file-level share-alike requirement.

No strings. No attribution demands. No endorsement clauses. The one condition is file-level: modify a Phlix file and that file stays open.

Contributing

Phlix is open source and contributions are welcome. Whether it is a bug report, a feature idea, a plugin, or a full client app — the community keeps this project grooving.

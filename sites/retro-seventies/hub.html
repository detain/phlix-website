The Relay

Your server, anywhere

Sign in once. The reverse-tunnel relay handles the NAT. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

Phlix Hub

One login to rule them all

Sign in to the Hub once, and it keeps a directory of all your Phlix servers. Tap one to connect — no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no manual tunnel setup.

Reverse-tunnel relay

Your server opens an outbound connection to the Hub and keeps it alive. When you want to reach your server, the Hub routes the request through that connection. NAT is never in the way.

Self-hostable

The Hub is open source (phlix-hub on GitHub). Self-host it on a VPS, and you own the relay completely. Or use the public Hub — no account required for personal use.

Use the public Hub

No setup. Your server connects to the shared relay. No account needed for personal use. Works out of the box.

Self-host the Hub

Clone, configure, deploy. Full control over the relay infrastructure. Hub docs have the details.

Tunnel through to the Relay

Once your server is running, Hub mode takes one toggle. Then your library follows you anywhere.

Featured features

Featured

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Movie night stays locked in step — every seat, every room, the same beat. Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together.

No more: "Wait, did you see that?" "Shh, I'm not there yet." SyncPlay means the group stays in the groove.

Featured

Library that organizes itself

Drop a record, watch it land on the shelf. Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Your collection grows organically and stays tidy.

Multi-source metadata: TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO with a 24-hour cache so you are not hammering the APIs.

Everything on the shelf

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and TV-4K. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. FFmpeg under the hood.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that does not make you click through menus.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV does not need a new app. Discovery via SSDP.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Phlix Hub — reach any server from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Your server, accessible from anywhere.

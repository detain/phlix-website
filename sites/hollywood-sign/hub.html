Phlix Hub

Access your server from anywhere without touching your router.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your home server to the outside world without requiring port forwards, dynamic DNS, or a static IP. When your client app signs in, it establishes a tunnel through the Hub — your server never opens an inbound port.

For NAT traversal, the Hub acts as a signalling server: it helps two peers discover each other and establishes the most direct path possible. If direct connection isn't available (symmetric NAT, for example), traffic flows through the Hub relay. 

# Connect a server to the public Hub
phlix server connect --hub https://hub.phlix.io

# Or self-host your own Hub
phlix-hub --listen :8080 --database sqlite:hub.db

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.io is free for personal use. If you want full control, own your data, or run in an enterprise environment, deploy your own Hub — it's open source and ships as a single PHP binary.

Self-hosting the Hub means your server credentials never touch a third-party server. The Hub only sees tunneled traffic, not your media.

Hub mode in clients

All official clients support Hub mode. When you add a server, choose "Connect via Hub" instead of direct IP. The client will sign in to the Hub, discover your server, and establish the tunnel — all without touching your router.

Hub mode works on any network, including mobile data, corporate WiFi, and anywhere else your client might be. Your server stays on your home network, private and secure.

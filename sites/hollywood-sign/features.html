Features

Everything you need to build a personal streaming platform that puts you in control.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. Phlix automatically detects new files, extracts metadata from filenames and folder structures, and pulls in rich data from TMDB, TVDB, and Fanart.tv with a 24-hour cache to keep your library fresh without hammering external APIs.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Whether you're in the same room or across the country, SyncPlay ensures your movie night stays perfectly synchronized so nobody misses a moment or gets spoiled by a scene that was supposed to be a surprise.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Phlix automatically selects the best quality for each device — direct play when the client supports it, transcoded HLS when it doesn't. Per-device quality profiles let you cap bandwidth on mobile or go full 4K on your big screen.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Each family member can have their own profile with their own watched history, resume positions, and content restrictions. PINs prevent little ones from accessing content meant for older audiences.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Watch live TV, schedule recordings in advance, and browse the program guide without wrestling with a clunky interface. Your recordings are stored locally and always accessible, even when the internet isn't.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Phlix implements the standard DLNA protocols, so any device that speaks DLNA — smart TVs, game consoles, media players — can discover and stream from your Phlix server without installing anything or creating yet another account.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. Phlix's plugin system isn't an afterthought — it's a first-class extension mechanism with a versioned contract, so plugins you write today will continue working as Phlix evolves.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Access your home server from anywhere without configuring port forwards, setting up dynamic DNS, or paying for a third-party tunnel service. The Hub is open source too — run your own or use the public instance.

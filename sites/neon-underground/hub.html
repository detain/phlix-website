Phlix Hub

Access your server from anywhere, without opening ports.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub provides two things: a cloud directory so you can find your servers, and a reverse-tunnel relay for NAT traversal.

When you're on your home network, clients connect directly. When you're elsewhere, the Hub's relay bridges the connection — no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no exposing your server to the internet.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free for personal use. It routes through Phlix's infrastructure, so your traffic leaves the relay server encrypted.

For enterprise or self-hosting scenarios, phlix-hub is open source (MPL-2.0) and runs on your own VPS. You control where your metadata and tunnel traffic goes.

Hub mode in clients

All official clients support Hub mode. Enable it in settings, sign in once, and your server appears automatically wherever you are. Clients fall back to LAN discovery when you're home, so there's no performance penalty on your local network.

The Windows client and mobile app support background Hub mode — your library syncs even when the app is closed.

## 5. Ambiguities, conflicts, and how I resolved them

Recorded here rather than guessed silently.

- **A1 — `content.json` licence vs. `new_site.md`.** `new_site.md` §3.8/§4/§16 hard-code
  BSD-3-Clause; `content.json` (the fact source, and `plan_site_regen.md` §0.9's open item)
  now says MPL-2.0 for server + hub, MIT for the rest. **Resolved: `content.json` wins**, since
  §2/§18.5 make it the fact authority. Footer copy, About "License" chapter, FAQ answer, and
  JSON-LD all state MPL-2.0/MIT. **Escalated:** `new_site.md` §3.8/§4/§16 still say BSD-3 and
  will make every one of the remaining 49 regens choose between two "authorities".
- **A2 — the pitch block has no home section.** `homepage_narrative.sections[]` lists 5
  sections and `complexity_profile.page_budget.home_sections_max` is 5, yet
  `copy_overlay.section_headings.pitch` and `copy_treatments.pitch_bullets` both exist, so the
  pitch content must appear. **Resolved:** the 7 bullets render _inside_ `#why-this-studio`
  under the overlay heading "Why paint here?" — the section's `story` source supplies the
  manifesto, the bullets supply the substance. Section count stays 5.
- **A3 — `words_per_section_max: 100` vs. `content.json`.** The 7 pitch bullets alone are ~85
  words, and the 8 feature bodies are ~35 words each; obeying 100 literally would mean dropping
  facts, which §2A forbids. **Resolved:** the cap is applied to **authored/presentation prose**
  per section (headings, manifesto, framing, captions); verbatim `content.json` fact strings
  (bullets, feature bodies inside `<details>`, FAQ answers) are exempt. Authored prose is kept
  genuinely terse.
- **A4 — secondary CTA label vs. its href.** `copy_overlay.hero.secondary_cta` overrides only
  the **label** ("Browse the Gallery"); the href stays `content.json`'s docs URL, so the label
  would mislead. **Resolved:** visible text is `Browse the Gallery (the docs)` — kit label kept,
  destination honest, accessible name contains the visible label (WCAG 2.5.3).
- **A5 — `page_blueprints.features` says "4 focal works", `feature_casting.hero` lists 2.**
  **Resolved:** `feature_casting` is the casting authority per the §2A DO-table, so 2 double-wide
  focal works; the blueprint's three-tier _shape_ is honoured by adding the marginalia tier
  (2 footnote features) below the 4 studies. Nothing is invented to reach four heroes.
- **A6 — `proof_strategy` asks for a "live star count and contributor list".** A static page
  cannot verify either, and `proof_strategy` signals must be **verifiable**; printing a number
  would be a fabricated count. **Resolved:** the credits row links to the real repo, its
  `/graphs/contributors` and its `/stargazers` — verifiable by one click, no invented figures.
  The docs quote is taken verbatim from the `content.json` FAQ answer ("built in PHP 8.3+ on
  Workerman").
- **A7 — nav typeface.** `fonts.ui.usage` assigns navigation to Inter; `navigation_model.spec`
  says nav labels are Cormorant Garamond. **Resolved:** the more specific, newer
  `navigation_model` wins (§2A: a present field overrides for its concern); labels are Cormorant
  Garamond 600 at 1.0625rem so the serif stays legible at nav size. Buttons/labels/chips stay
  Inter.
- **A8 — `new_site.md` §2A contradicts itself on the 404.** The DO-table row says
  `error_page_experience` is "**Schema-only for now** — do **not** attempt to ship a per-kit 404
  this pass", while the prose section immediately below ("→ ship a real `404.html`") and §18.1
  require it. **Resolved:** ship it (the shim exists, §18 gates on it). **Escalated:** the stale
  DO-table row should be deleted before the remaining 49 runs.
- **A9 — kit contrast claim is wrong.** `accessibility.minimum_contrast` states Cadmium Red on
  Gallery Linen ≈ 5.8:1; measured it is **4.73:1** (and only **4.35:1** on Canvas Cream, i.e.
  _failing_ AA for small text on card surfaces). Raw Umber is 3.32:1 on linen — the kit itself
  admits it "may require larger text". **Resolved:** pure `#CC2200` is used for marks, rules,
  borders, underlines and large display text only; small text uses derived deeper pigments that
  clear 4.5:1 on all three grounds — `--color-cadmium-text #B31E00` (5.78/5.32/4.75),
  `--color-neutral-ink #5F594C` (5.94/5.47/4.88), `--color-success-text #146B40`,
  `--color-warning-text #7A5600`. These are documented in `SITE.md` as darkened mixes of the
  kit's own pigments, not new hues; `accessibility` is a hard commitment and outranks a wrong
  arithmetic claim.
- **A10 — `easter_eggs[1]` is `typed-word:palette` "in your input"** but the site has no text
  input. **Resolved:** a document-level key-sequence listener that is _disabled_ while focus is
  in any `input`/`textarea`/`contenteditable`, never calls `preventDefault`, and exits on Esc.
- **A11 — two "5-click" eggs.** `easter_eggs[0]` is `logo-clicks:5`; `mascot.behavior
.easter_interactions[0]` is `click:5` on Palette. Both are implemented, on their own targets.
- **A12 — `og:image` must be `.png`** (`tools/check-meta.mjs` rule 5) while `new_site.md` §8
  allows `og.svg` as the source. Both satisfied: `og.svg` is the editable source, `og.png` is
  rasterised from it and is what the meta references.

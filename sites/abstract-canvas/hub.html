The frame around the work

The Frame

Phlix Hub is the frame that lets you see your own collection from somewhere else — without opening your house to the internet.

I

What it does

What the Hub does

Sign in once. A reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT, so a client at a friend's house can reach the server sitting on your own LAN. Nothing is exposed by hand, and no third-party tunnel is involved.

Your server

At home on your LAN, holding the collection.

The relay

A reverse tunnel that meets NAT halfway.

Your screen

Roku, Windows or phone, wherever you happen to be.

II

Where it runs

Self-host it, or use the public hub

The hub is self-hostable — it is its own repository, a cloud directory plus the relay. If you would rather not run it, use the public one. Either way the collection stays on your hardware.

The phlix-hub repository

III

In the stations

Hub mode in the clients

The Roku channel and the Windows desktop app both list hub mode among their capabilities, and the mobile app refreshes its tokens quietly so a session survives the trip.

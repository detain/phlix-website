Phlix Hub

Your server. Your home network. Accessible from anywhere — without touching your router.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your Phlix server is behind a NAT or firewall, clients can't reach it directly from the outside world. The Hub acts as a trusted intermediary: clients connect to the Hub first, and the Hub brokers the connection to your server — no port forwarding, no static IP, no UPnP.

The connection is outbound from your server to the Hub — so it works even if your ISP gives you a CG-NAT address. You sign in once, your devices find your server, and everything just works.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.io is free to use for personal setups. If you want full control, or if you're running a larger deployment, you can self-host the Hub on any server with a public IP.

Self-hosting the Hub means you own the directory — no third party in the path between your clients and your server.

Hub mode in clients

All official Phlix clients support Hub mode. When you add a server, you can choose between direct LAN access (faster, no internet required) or Hub mode (works from anywhere). The client handles the fallback automatically — on your home network it finds the server directly; away from home it goes through the Hub.

The Far Reach

The Phlix Hub is a rope bridge between you and your library — spanning any NAT, crossing any distance, arriving at every hearth you own.

What the Hub Does

The Reverse-Tunnel Relay

Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal so you never have to open a port, configure a firewall, or remember an IP address.

Sign in once

Your Hub account is the key. Sign in on any client, any device, and your library appears — as if you were home.

Reverse tunnel relay

No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. The Hub keeps an outbound connection alive to the public relay, and routes your traffic through it.

Self-hostable

Run your own Hub relay and keep the bridge entirely in your own keeping. Or use the public Hub — the choice is yours.

Hub mode in every client

Every Phlix client — Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile — supports Hub mode. Enable it once, and remote access just works.

"Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub."

— Phlix documentation

Two Paths

Choose Your Own Hub

Use the Public Hub

The fastest path: sign in at github.com/detain/phlix-hub, point your server at the relay, and you're connected in minutes.

Self-Host the Hub

Run phlix-hub on your own VPS or home server. Full control, no third party, complete privacy for your library access.

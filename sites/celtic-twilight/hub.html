Beyond the firewall

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. The Hub finds your server behind any NAT and opens the path. Self-hostable, or use the public relay.

What the Hub does

Most home networks sit behind Carrier-Grade NAT or a router that refuses to port-forward. Phlix Hub solves this with a reverse-tunnel relay: your server calls out to the Hub once, and the Hub keeps the door open for your clients.

The Hub is a cloud directory service and relay. It maintains a registry of your server's current location, brokers the initial connection handshake, and then steps aside — leaving your server and your clients to talk directly over the tunnel it opened. No third-party tunnel service required. No account required for basic use.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free for casual use. For households that want full ownership, the Hub is itself open-source and can be deployed on any VPS with a public IP.

Self-hosting the Hub means your server registration data never leaves hardware you control. It is the most private option — and the one the ancient druids would have chosen.

https://github.com/detain/phlix-hub

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client — Roku, Samsung, Windows, Mobile — supports Hub mode. When Hub mode is enabled, the client connects to the Hub instead of your LAN IP. Your server appears to be online regardless of where you are. The session is secured with JWT; the relay is TLS-terminated.

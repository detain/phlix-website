What's inside the trading post

Eight well-worn trails

Every feature here is a path that's been walked a thousand times — organized, refined, and ready to carry your library.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO with a 24-hour cache — your library stays organized without constant tending.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. From the couch to the college town, the story stays in step.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Direct play when the client supports it; adaptive HLS otherwise.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Everyone gets their own watchlist, their own progress, their own place at the counter.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. The desert sky in all its channels, recorded and waiting.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Discovery via SSDP. The gathering place was there all along.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. Build on the trading post; the walls are open.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. The trail home is always open.

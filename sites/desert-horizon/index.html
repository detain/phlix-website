The Trading Post

Why the light settles here

Out past the last paved road, where the blacktop dissolves into packed caliche and the horizon stretches wide enough to swallow your worries whole, there stands an old trading post. The walls are three feet of rammed earth, cool in the afternoon blaze.

Inside: hand-woven rugs in turquoise and burnt umber, shelves of glazed pottery catching the slant of golden light, and a battered radio spinning stories from far-off places.

Desert Horizon is that trading post — a place where every title in your library feels like something worth sitting with, where the interface itself breathes at the pace of the land.

What's inside

What you'll find on the shelves

Library that organizes itself

Everything you own, organized the moment it arrives — no rushing, no busy hands. Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 titles, metadata from TMDB and TVDB.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Every device in every room stays in perfect step, as if you're all sitting on the same mesa watching the same horizon. Weighted-mean NTP offset keeps everyone locked together.

Why Phlix?

Why the light settles here

  • 100% self-hostable — your library never leaves your hardware unless you say so
  • Native clients on Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile, plus any DLNA device
  • Real-time SyncPlay with NTP-style time sync so movie night actually stays in sync
  • Multi-source metadata: TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO with 24-hour cache
  • Adaptive HLS streaming, FFmpeg transcoding, per-device quality profiles
  • Live TV with DVR + EPG guide integration
  • Plugin system with a versioned manifest contract

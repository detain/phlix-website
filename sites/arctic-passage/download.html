Download

Self-host your media in minutes. One command, zero hassle.

Server

Requires PHP 8.3+, MySQL, and ffmpeg. The installer targets a fresh Ubuntu/Debian host.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/detain/phlix-server/master/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash

The installer sets up system packages, creates the database, generates a secret key, runs migrations, and configures a systemd service with HAProxy and Let's Encrypt.

# Or with HTTPS and custom domain:
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/detain/phlix-server/master/scripts/install.sh \
  | sudo bash -s -- --domain phlix.example.com --admin-email you@example.com

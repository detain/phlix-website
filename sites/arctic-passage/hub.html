Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Access any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub Does

The Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects you to your servers behind NAT or firewall — without opening ports or using a third-party tunnel service.

It maintains a directory of your registered servers, handles authentication, and forwards traffic securely. Your media never passes through the Hub's servers — only the tunnel metadata.

Reverse-Tunnel Relay

The Hub brokers a secure connection between your client and your server, even when both are behind NAT. No port forwarding needed.

NAT Traversal

Your server reaches out to the Hub once; the Hub remembers how to reach it. Clients connect through the Hub to your server on any network.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

Use the Public Hub

No setup required. Register your server with the public Hub and start accessing it immediately. Free for personal use.

Self-Host the Hub

Run your own Hub instance for full control. The phlix-hub repository has everything you need to deploy on any VPS.

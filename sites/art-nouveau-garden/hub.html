The Relay

Sign in once. The Hub brings your server to you — no port-forwarding, no third-party tunnel.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay. Run it on any host with a public IP, connect your server to it once, and from then on your clients connect to the Hub — the Hub connects to your server. Your server never needs a public IP or open ports.

NAT traversal handled. No internet-facing server required. Your media stays on your hardware.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

The Hub is open-source (phlix-hub on GitHub). You can run your own, or use the public Hub at hub.phlix.io — no account required, no subscription.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. Sign in once in the app, select your server from the Hub directory, and you're connected — whether you're on the same LAN or across the world.

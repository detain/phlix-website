Phlix Hub

Reach your server from anywhere — a gilded gate that opens to your collection, wherever you wander.

What the Hub does

Sign in once. The Hub's reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal so you can access your server from your phone, your Roku at a friend's house, or any device anywhere in the world. No ports to open, no configuration to wrestle with — just a gentle, open pathway.

Self-host or use the public hub

You can run your own phlix-hub instance, or use the public relay — no configuration required, no fees, no friction. Whichever path you choose, the garden remains yours.

Hub mode in clients

Every official client supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub relay instead of directly to your LAN server. One tap, and your collection follows you.

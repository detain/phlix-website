What the garden offers

  • Self-host your library — your collection lives on your own hardware, not someone else's cloud
  • SyncPlay synchronizes playback across every room, so movie night moves as one
  • Adaptive HLS streaming + FFmpeg transcoding handles any format on any device
  • Multi-user accounts with JWT auth, Argon2ID hashing, up to 5 profiles, and parental PINs
  • Works on Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, mobile (beta), and any DLNA device you already own
  • Phlix Hub reaches your server from anywhere — reverse-tunnel relay, self-hostable
  • 100% open source — MPL-2.0 server, MIT clients and plugins — audited, reproducible, yours

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app.

Why tend a garden at home?

In the twilight years of the nineteenth century, at the corner of Rue de Rivoli and a lantern-lit garden, there stood a small salon de cinéma. Its iron entrance gate curled into lilies; its walls were tiled in peacock blues and sage greens; and every programme card was a small work of art, hand-lettered and bordered with gilded vines.

People came not merely to watch — they came to dwell in beauty. Art Nouveau Garden carries that salon spirit into your home. Pressing play is not an act of consumption; it is an act of aesthetic devotion. The screen blooms. The evening unfolds. You are always, in some sense, in the garden.

Beauty. Craft. Discovery. Harmony. Timelessness.

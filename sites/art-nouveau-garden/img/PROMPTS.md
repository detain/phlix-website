# img/PROMPTS.md — Art Nouveau Garden Image Generation Prompts

All prompts follow the kit's `illustration_prompt_template`:
`{image_prompt_prefix} {subject}, in the Art Nouveau Garden style, {mood}, framed by flowing vines and blossom borders in the manner of Mucha {image_prompt_suffix}`

**Image prompt prefix** (prepend to every prompt):
> Belle Époque Art Nouveau illustration, Alphonse Mucha style, botanical watercolor with vine and peacock feather border, ivory vellum paper, soft sage greens and dusty rose, aged gold leaf accents, warm gaslit diffuse glow,

**Image prompt suffix** (append to every prompt):
> , cohesive Art Nouveau palette (aged gold, dusty rose, sage green, ivory cream, forest ink), organic flowing composition, elegant hand-drawn linework, gentle vellum texture, high quality.

**Negative prompt** (things to always avoid):
> neon, cyberpunk, futuristic, dark mode, sharp geometric, digital gloss, HDR, heavy contrast, horror, minimalist-cold, corporate sans-serif, tech aesthetic, photorealistic render, harsh lighting

## Notes

- All icons should be stroke-based (not filled), 1.5–2px stroke weight, rounded caps/joins
- Colors must stay within the Art Nouveau Garden palette — no off-palette hues
- Linework should feel confident yet fluid, never mechanical
- No geometric or tech iconography; prefer organic botanical metaphors

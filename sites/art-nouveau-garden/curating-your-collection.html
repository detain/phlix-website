How to Cultivate Your Film Garden

A guided meditation on collecting and organizing — turning the scanner, metadata, and library features into a curator's narrative.

The Library That Organizes Itself

Drop a file into your watched folder. Phlix notices the new mtime, the scanner parses S01E02 or (2020) in the filename, and ItemRepository hydrates the metadata_json from TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, or your own local NFO. Add a file, see it appear. Your collection blooms into being — every film finds its place, beautifully organized, waiting for the evening's choice.

SyncPlay — One Screen, Many Rooms

Movie night stays in perfect rhythm. Press play once; every room moves together as if they were one garden, one screen. The weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame — play, pause, seek, and everyone moves together, whether they are in the same room or across the country.

Transcoding — The Right Quality, Automatically

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Your files are always played in the highest quality your device can handle — no manual configuration, no guessing.

Multi-User, Multi-Profile

Up to five profiles per user, each with their own 4- or 6-digit PIN, their own rating filter (G to NC-17), and their own viewing history. Parents can tend their collection while children explore theirs — all under the same roof, all with their own keys.

The Plugin Garden

Implement LifecycleInterface, ship a manifest, drop it in the plugins directory. The loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter, and browse the ecosystem for community plugins that extend what Phlix can do.

The Phlix Hub — Reach from Anywhere

Run Phlix on your LAN. Use the Hub's reverse-tunnel relay to reach it from your phone or Roku at a friend's house. You can self-host the hub, or use the public one. Sign in once. Your server comes to you.

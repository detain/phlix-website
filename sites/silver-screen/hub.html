What the Hub does

Phlix Hub provides two key capabilities:

  • Cloud directory: Sign in once, see all your servers. No need to remember IP addresses or ports.
  • Reverse-tunnel relay: Your client connects to the Hub, the Hub connects to your server. No ports to open on your router.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The public Hub at phlix-hub is free to use. If you prefer to run your own:

  • Deploy your own phlix-hub instance
  • Point your server at your own Hub URL
  • Full control over your directory and relay

Hub mode in clients

Clients with Hub mode built in (Roku, Windows) can connect through the Hub automatically. Set it up once in the client settings and your server is accessible from anywhere.

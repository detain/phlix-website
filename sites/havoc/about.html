ABOUT

Self-hosted media server for those who refuse to be locked in.

THE STORY

Phlix was born from frustration. The frustration of paying monthly fees for media you already own. The frustration of proprietary lock-in. The frustration of being told how, when, and where you can watch what you want.

So we built something different. A media server that puts you in control. No subscriptions. No cloud dependency. No corporate surveillance. Just you, your media, and your hardware.

Phlix is for the rebellious. The collectors. The ones who read the fine print and realized they were renting, not buying. The ones who want their media server to feel like it's theirs, not licensed to them.

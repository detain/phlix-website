## Inline Feature Icons (SVG, stroke-based)

**Prompt:**
Single-color stroke icon, 24×24 viewBox, stroke-width 2, stroke-linecap square, stroke-linejoin miter. Color: chaos cyan (#4CC9F0). Sharp angular shapes only. No rounded corners. Example: library icon uses sharp rectangular paths, not smooth curves.

---

## Inline Client Icons (SVG, stroke-based)

Same style as feature icons. Client-specific imagery:
- **Roku:** Simple "R" letterform or remote icon
- **Tizen:** Simple "T" or TV shape
- **Windows:** Simple "W" or window frame
- **Mobile:** Simple phone outline
- **DLNA:** Antenna/wifi signal icon

---

## Notes

- All assets should feel like controlled chaos — aggressive, high-energy, intentional
- Explosion cloud gradients with hot pink cores bleeding to purple/blue edges
- Jagged edges, fracture lines, sharp corners throughout
- No soft gradients, no rounded corners, no gentle pastels

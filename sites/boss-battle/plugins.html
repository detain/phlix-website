Extend Phlix. Your rules. Your features.

Plugin Contract

Every plugin implements the LifecycleInterface and ships a manifest. Drop it in the plugins/ directory — the loader handles the rest.

  • Implement LifecycleInterface
  • Ship manifest.json (version, name, entry point)
  • Drop into plugins/ directory
  • Phlix loads it automatically

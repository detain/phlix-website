Phlix Hub

Your server. Your network. Anywhere.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects you to your servers without opening ports or configuring NAT. Sign in once, and the Hub routes your traffic — whether you're on the same LAN or on the other side of the world.

No firewall exceptions. No dynamic DNS. No third-party tunnel service required.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free to use. If you prefer complete control, you can self-host the Hub on any server with a public IP — it's open source and easy to deploy.

  • ✓ No account required for self-hosted Hub
  • ✓ Your traffic never touches our servers when self-hosted
  • ✓ Same client, same experience, different Hub URL

Hub Mode in Clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub instead of directly to your server. Movies, TV, music, SyncPlay — everything works the same way, just reached differently.

Open the client settings, enter your Hub credentials (or self-hosted URL), and you're connected.

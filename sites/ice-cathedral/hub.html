What the Hub does

Sign in once. Reach everything.

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that handles NAT traversal automatically. Run Phlix on your LAN — the Hub makes it reachable from your phone, your friend's Roku, or any device anywhere in the world.

No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. No third-party tunnel service. The relay holds the connection open and routes it to your server. You see your library. The architecture stays the same.

Self-host the Hub

Run phlix-hub on your own infrastructure. Full control. Your credentials never leave your hardware. See phlix-hub on GitHub for deployment.

phlix-hub source

Use the public Hub

No setup. Point your Phlix server at the public Hub relay, sign in, and access your library from anywhere. Zero infrastructure to maintain.

Hub mode in clients

Every native client — Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile — can connect via Hub mode. The client connects to the relay, the relay connects to your server. To the client, it looks like a local connection. To your server, no ports are open.

Eight chambers, each eternal

Vaulted Chambers

Every feature placed with the deliberateness of a stone in a gothic vault. These are not options — they are architecture.

Your library takes its place in the cathedral — organized by structure, forever.

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file and it appears — every item held in its proper vault.

Every frame, locked across every room — the silence remains unbroken.

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. One moment, held across distance.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. The right quality for each device, chosen automatically.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Each profile a separate chamber.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. The cathedral's light carried across any distance, without dimming.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that does not make you click through menus. Built into the cathedral's own structure.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV does not need a new app. A window, not a door.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. Extensible, versioned, certain.

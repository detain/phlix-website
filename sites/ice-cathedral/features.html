Vaulted Chambers

Every feature placed with the deliberateness of a stone in a gothic vault. These are not options — they are architecture.

Your library takes its place in the cathedral — organized by structure, forever.

Phlix watches your folders for changes, recognizes S01E02 and (2020) titles, Phlix fills in titles, artwork, and descriptions. Add a file and it appears — every item held in its proper vault.

Every frame, locked across every room — the silence remains unbroken.

Watch together, perfectly in step. Phlix keeps every device's playback clock tightly synced, so play, pause, and seek move together for everyone in the room — or an ocean away. One moment, held across distance.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

Every device gets the right quality on its own — dedicated profiles for phones, web, and 4K TVs. When a file won't play directly, Phlix transcodes it on the fly with FFmpeg (H.264 defaults to CRF 23, the sweet spot between quality and size) and delivers it as adaptive HLS that rides out network hiccups. If your server has a compatible Intel or AMD GPU, hardware profiles run the heavy work on it instead of the CPU. The right quality for each device, chosen automatically.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

One account, up to 5 personal profiles, each lockable with a 4- or 6-digit PIN. Passwords are protected with Argon2ID — today's strongest widely recommended hashing — and sign-ins stay secure with tokens that refresh themselves. Rating filters from G to NC-17 keep kids' profiles to kids' content. Bring your existing identity too: OIDC, LDAP, and passkeys all work, so Phlix fits the sign-in you already run. Each profile a separate chamber.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. A relay you run reaches your home server without opening ports. Fully self-hostable. The cathedral's light carried across any distance, without dimming.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

Built-in scheduling, the guide, and the recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that does not make you click through menus. Built into the cathedral's own structure.

DLNA for the devices you already own

the DLNA directory and transport protocols mean your old smart TV does not need a new app. A window, not a door.

Plugin system with a real contract

A versioned plugin contract. Install it at Settings → Plugins, and the server loads it once enabled. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. Extensible, versioned, certain.

The Cathedral's Vaults

Your library takes its place in the cathedral — organized by structure, forever.

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. Every item held in its proper vault.

Every frame, locked across every room — the silence remains unbroken.

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. One moment, held across distance.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. The cathedral's light carried across any distance.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that does not make you click through menus.

Why enter this space?

A cathedral built for watching.

There is a moment, deep inside a glacier, when the ice closes over the entrance behind you and the world goes perfectly still. No wind. No warmth. Only the blue light filtered through ten thousand years of compressed snow, and the slow tick of geological time.

Phlix was built in that silence. Every interface decision — the quiet typography, the unhurried transitions, the reverence for the media itself — comes from the belief that great stories deserve the same gravity as the cathedral. Watch here, and feel the weight of something that cannot be hurried.

The Foundation

Core capabilities

Five native clients. SyncPlay locked to NTP-precise time. HLS streaming with FFmpeg. Hub for NAT traversal. Plugin contract versioned.

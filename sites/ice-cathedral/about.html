I.

Philosophy

There is a moment, deep inside a glacier, when the ice closes over the entrance behind you and the world goes perfectly still. No wind. No warmth. Only the blue light filtered through ten thousand years of compressed snow, and the slow tick of geological time.

Phlix was born in that moment. It is the belief that great stories deserve the same reverence as a cathedral built across generations — that the interface around a film should be as intentional, as structural, as eternal as the ice formations that outlast everything that built them.

Watch here, and feel the weight of something that cannot be hurried. Every frame, held. Every library, a vault. Every screen, a window into the same cold light.

Contributing

All Phlix development lives in the detain GitHub organisation. Open issues, open pull requests, open forks. The community is the structure.

Plugins are the clearest path to contribution. Implement LifecycleInterface, ship a manifest, drop it in the plugins directory. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

IV.

Frequently asked questions

Is Phlix like Plex / Jellyfin / Emby?
Yes — same job, different stack. Phlix is built in PHP 8.3+ on Workerman, ships with a versioned plugin contract, and includes a hub for accessing remote servers behind NAT without a third-party tunnel.
Do I need to expose my server to the internet?
No. Run Phlix on your LAN and use the Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay to reach it from your phone or Roku at a friend's house. You can self-host the hub, or use the public one.
Can I run this from a closet or a basement?
No. Run Phlix on your LAN and use the Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay to reach it from your phone or Roku at a friend's house. You can self-host the hub, or use the public one.
What formats are supported?
Anything FFmpeg can read. Direct play when the client supports it; transcoded HLS otherwise. Per-device quality profiles pick the right bitrate automatically.

Anything FFmpeg can read. Direct play when the client supports it; transcoded HLS otherwise. Per-device quality profiles pick the right bitrate automatically.
Is there a mobile app?
Yes — React Native, available on iOS and Android. Currently in beta.
Can I write plugins?
Yes. Implement LifecycleInterface, ship a manifest, drop it in the plugins directory. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

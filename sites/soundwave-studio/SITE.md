# SITE.md — Soundwave Studio

## Concept & Vision

Soundwave Studio is the aesthetic of a world-class recording studio applied to a media server. It is dark, focused, and alive with technical beauty — charcoal surfaces, waveform green readouts, and VU amber glow. Every element earns its place like a channel on a console. The brand voice is technical, direct, and quietly passionate — like talkback from the engineer.

## Aesthetic Direction

Dark studio environment with analog precision. Primary visual language is the oscilloscope/waveform motif in phosphor green on charcoal. Accent colors are VU amber (status/meters) and signal red (errors/peaks only). Layouts are dense and grid-structured, echoing channel strips on a mixing console.

## Spatial System

8px base grid. Spacing scale: 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px. Dense layouts preferred — the studio fills every channel. Max content width 1400px for standard views, 1600px for dashboard/technical views.

## Motion Philosophy

Fast, precise, rhythmic. Waveform pulses, VU needle sweeps, and reel rotations are the signature motions. Easing: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1) and ease-out. Duration: 150–250ms for transitions. Respect prefers-reduced-motion — replace waveform animations with instant state changes.

## Visual Assets

- **Logo:** Waveform glyph + Rajdhani Bold wordmark in waveform green on charcoal
- **Icons:** Monoline, 1.5px stroke, sharp corners, technical vocabulary
- **Decorative:** VU meter bars, waveform waveforms, diamond-grid acoustic foam texture
- **No daylight/warm light** — the studio is always sealed and dark

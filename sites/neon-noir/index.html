Case brief

The library exists. Nobody can reach it.

Every city has a side that only comes alive after dark. Your collection is like that — hours of film sitting on a drive in another room, invisible to every screen you own.

Phlix is the archivist's tool. It watches the folders, reads the metadata, and hands the whole collection to any screen in the house — or any screen you happen to be standing near.

Why Phlix solves the case

  • CL-01 100% self-hostable — your library never leaves your hardware unless you say so
  • CL-02 Native clients on Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile, plus any DLNA device
  • CL-03 Real-time SyncPlay with NTP-style time sync so movie night actually stays in sync
  • CL-04 Multi-source metadata: TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO with 24-hour cache
  • CL-05 Adaptive HLS streaming, FFmpeg transcoding, per-device quality profiles
  • CL-06 Live TV with DVR + EPG guide integration
  • CL-07 Plugin system with a versioned manifest contract

Two leads, four supporting files

Evidence Files

EF-02 · SyncPlay

Every device locked to the same frame — no lag, no drift, pure synchronization under the neon.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together.

EF-01 · Library

Your archive, organized by the machine, searchable from anywhere, always live.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear.

  • EF-03

    Transcoding that picks the right quality

    QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists.

  • EF-04

    Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

    JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17.

  • EF-08

    Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

    Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub.

  • EF-05

    Live TV with DVR + EPG

    ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus.

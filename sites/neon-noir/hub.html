Phlix Hub.

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay and cloud directory. Your server lives behind your router — the Hub bridges the gap so your clients can reach it from anywhere without configuring port forwards or dynamic DNS.

The Hub maintains a directory of your registered servers. When a client signs in, the Hub brokers the connection — it doesn't transcode, it doesn't touch your library, it just opens the tunnel.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at phlix-hub is free to use. If you want complete control — or if you run Hub in an enterprise environment — you can self-host the Hub relay and point your clients at it instead.

Either way: one login, all your servers, anywhere.

phlix-hub on GitHub

Hub mode in clients

Roku, Windows, and Mobile clients all support Hub mode — sign in once, and your server list syncs automatically. No configuration needed when you're on a new network.

The Hub is optional. Run Phlix on your LAN without it — or add it when you want remote access.

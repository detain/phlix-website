Plugins

A versioned contract, a manifest, and a loader that picks up whatever you drop in. This is the back of the evidence room — most visitors never need it.

The contract

The plugin model

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example.

plugin contract 
plugins/my-plugin/
  plugin.json        # the versioned manifest
  src/Plugin.php     # implements LifecycleInterface

The manifest is versioned on purpose: the loader refuses what it cannot understand rather than guessing.

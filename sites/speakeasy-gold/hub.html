The Tunnel

Your vault, reachable from anywhere — without opening a port, exposing your IP, or making any compromises.

What the Hub Does

The Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay. When your server starts, it opens a persistent outbound connection to the Hub. When you open a client, the Hub routes traffic through that connection — no inbound ports required, no NAT traversal configuration needed.

No Port Forwarding

Your server initiates the connection outbound. Nothing needs to be open on your router.

No Third-Party Account

Self-host the Hub on your own VPS. Or use the public Hub. Either way, the relay is just a pipe — it can't read your media.

One Sign-In

Authenticate to the Hub once. Every server you own — in different locations, on different networks — appears in the same list.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free to use. If you want full control — or if you want to run the Hub alongside your server on the same machine — it's a standard Phlix package. One line to install, one config to adjust.

Hub Admin Docs

Hub Mode in Clients

Every native Phlix client — Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile — ships with Hub mode. Enable it, point it at your Hub URL (or the public one), sign in once, and your server list appears. Works from anywhere.

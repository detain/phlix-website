The Vault

Every room is stocked. Everything you need to run your private media establishment — and nothing you don't.

I. Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Drop a file, watch it settle on the marquee.

Multi-source metadata: TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO with 24-hour cache.

II. SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Movie night never falls out of step.

III. Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Direct play when the client supports it.

IV. Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. The house has rules, and they work.

V. Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Set it and forget it — Gilda's got the remote.

VI. DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. The parlor is always open to walk-ins.

VII. Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

VIII. Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Your media follows you down the block or across the country.

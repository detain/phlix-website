Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Access any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub Does

The Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that handles NAT traversal. When your Phlix server is behind a router or firewall, the Hub lets your clients reach it without configuring port forwarding.

Your Server
Behind NAT
→
Phlix Hub
Relay
→
Your Client
Anywhere

Your server initiates an outbound connection to the Hub. The Hub brokers the connection and relays traffic in both directions. No incoming ports need to be opened.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

Public Hub

Use the hosted hub at hub.phlix.io. No setup required — just register your server and go.

Self-Hosted

Run your own Hub instance. Full control, full privacy. See phlix-hub on GitHub.

Hub Mode in Clients

Roku, Windows, and Mobile clients all support Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub instead of directly to your server's local IP. Your media library follows you anywhere — no VPN, no port forwarding.

  • Sign in once with your Hub account
  • Select which server to connect to
  • Stream as if you were home

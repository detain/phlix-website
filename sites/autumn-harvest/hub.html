Phlix Hub

Your server, reachable from anywhere. No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. Just sign in and the library is there.

How it works

Phlix Hub runs a reverse-tunnel relay: your server opens an outbound connection to the Hub, and clients connect to the Hub to reach your server. Your router never sees an inbound connection. The Hub also maintains a directory of your servers so you can reach any of them by name from any device.

You can self-host the Hub on any PHP 8.3+ host, or use the public Hub operated by the Phlix team. Both are MPL-2.0.

Source & self-hosting

The Hub is open source and available in the phlix-hub repository. Self-hosting gives you full control and keeps your server list private.

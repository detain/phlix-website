## Feature Icons (Inline SVG)

Each icon: outlined rounded botanical style, 1.5–2px warm walnut (#3D2510) stroke,
rounded line caps and joins, single color, no sharp corners, harvest almanac style.

- **library** — botanical open book with leaf corners
- **syncplay** — two leaves synchronized in orbit
- **transcode** — a leaf transforming / unfurling
- **shield** — a leaf-shaped shield
- **antenna** — a broadcasting leaf
- **broadcast/dlna** — a leaf with wifi-like broadcast arcs
- **puzzle** — a leaf-shaped puzzle piece
- **hub** — interconnected leaves around a central node

## Photography (if used)

All photography follows this guidance:
- Subject: lifestyle, hands wrapped around mugs, wool textures, farmhouse interiors
- Lighting: golden October afternoon light or warm indoor firelight
- Treatment: Kodak Portra 400 palette, lifted shadows, orange midtones
- Never: cool-toned, blue-hour, overcast, sterile product shots, winter imagery

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no third-party tunnel.

Reverse-tunnel relay

The Hub maintains an outbound connection to the relay server. When you access the Hub, it routes traffic back through that tunnel. Your server stays behind your firewall — no ports opened.

Always-on access

Connect to your home server from your phone at work, your Roku at a friend's house, or your tablet on vacation. The Hub keeps the tunnel alive.

Self-hostable

Run your own Hub on a VPS. The phlix-hub repository is open source — you control the relay and the directory. Or use the public Hub; your choice.

Multi-server support

Have servers at home and at a parent's house? Sign in once to the Hub and access any of them. The Hub acts as a directory — pick which server you want.

Hub Mode in Clients

Enable Hub mode in any Phlix client to connect through the Hub instead of directly to your server. The Roku, Windows, and Mobile clients support Hub mode.

When Hub mode is enabled, the client authenticates with the Hub and routes all traffic through the tunnel — no server IP or port needed.

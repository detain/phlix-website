Phlix Hub

Access your server from anywhere — without opening ports or configuring DNS.

What the Hub does

The Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your server registers with a Hub, it establishes an outbound tunnel — so you can reach your server from any network, even behind carrier-grade NAT, without touching your router settings.

Cloud Directory

One place to find all your servers, no matter where they live.

Reverse Tunnel

Outbound-only connections. No inbound ports needed.

Secure by Default

All tunnel traffic is encrypted. Your data stays yours.

Self-host or use the public Hub

You have options:

  • Use the public Hub at phlix-hub — zero configuration, works immediately.
  • Self-host the Hub — full control, run it on your own infrastructure.

Hub mode in clients

When Hub mode is enabled in your client app, you sign in once and get a list of all your registered servers — home, vacation, anywhere. The client automatically connects through the Hub's relay when you're off-network.

Note: Hub mode is available in the Roku, Windows, and Mobile clients. Samsung Tizen uses direct connection when on the same network.

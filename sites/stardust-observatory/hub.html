What the Hub does

Sign in once. The Hub's reverse-tunnel relay opens a direct line to your server from any device, anywhere — no port forwarding, no configuration.

Self-host or use the public relay

Run your own phlix-hub instance if you prefer full control, or connect to our public relay — no setup required. Your server, your rules, your domain.

Hub mode in clients

Every official client supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client opens a direct tunnel to your server through the relay — like pointing a telescope through a remote shutter.

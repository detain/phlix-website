Features

The complete instrument panel

Begin your watch Explore the Hub

The luminous atlas

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear — your catalog grows like a star chart, point by point.

Meridian sync — together though we're apart

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together across the room or across the country.

Aperture-adaptive transcoding

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists — each frame calibrated to your display's appetite.

Observatory access — profiles and PINs

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. The dome is open only to those who belong.

Live TV — the stardust stream

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. The electromagnetic spectrum, tamed for your viewing pleasure.

DLNA — old light, new eyes

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. The light it already knows, now rendered luminous.

Plugin architecture — open the dome

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. Extend the observatory with your own instruments. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Stardust Hub — reach your dome from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Your observatory, accessible to you alone.

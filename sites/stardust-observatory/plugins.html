Plugins

Extend the observatory with a curated selection of instruments. Each plugin is versioned, indexed, and built to last across decades of viewing.

Plugin model

Every plugin implements LifecycleInterface and ships a manifest. Drop it in the plugins/ directory and the loader picks it up automatically.

// See the reference plugin:

Ecosystem plugins

The plugin contract makes it easy to add metadata providers, transcode pipelines, or anything else you can imagine.

Aperture Archive

Preserve your most valuable recordings in a redundant, indexed archive tier. Checksummed, versioned, retrievable across decades.

Meridian Sync

A SyncPlay enhancement that learns your group's viewing rhythm and pre-buffers the next episode. Because waiting breaks immersion.

Constellation Metadata

A metadata aggregator that pulls from twelve scientific and cultural databases — giving your collection the depth of a star atlas, not just a film poster.

Transit Scheduler

Program your Live TV recordings with transit-time precision. The EPG guide becomes a map of the night sky — plan your evening's viewing the way a navigator plans a route.

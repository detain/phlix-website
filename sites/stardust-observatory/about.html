Our Story

In 1889, high on a fog-free hillside, an amateur astronomer named Elspeth Ward raised a brass refractor toward Andromeda and understood — for the first time — that the smudge of light she was tracking was not a cloud within our galaxy but an entire island universe two million years away. She wrote in her atlas that night: "What we seek is always older and larger than we imagined." Stardust Observatory carries that same humbling thrill into every living room.

The Team

Phlix is built by a small, async-first collective of engineers, designers, and stargazers spread across three continents. We are drawn together by a shared obsession with brass instruments and very old light. We build open-source software and believe the observatory dome should be open to everyone who wishes to gaze upward.

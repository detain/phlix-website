Expedition guide

Chart Your Course

Three steps to your own observatory: the zenith protocol, the companion instruments you will carry, and the archive of references for later. The install is the quiet part where the magic happens — one command, and then you are the astronomer.

The three stages

Stage one — the zenith protocol

Raise the server

Phlix Server runs on your own hardware: PHP 8.3+ on Workerman 5.x, with FFmpeg doing the transcoding. One line raises the whole observatory. 

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/detain/phlix-server/master/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash

On a fresh Ubuntu or Debian host this installs PHP 8.3+, MySQL and FFmpeg, creates a dedicated phlix user and its database, writes an env file with a generated secret, runs the migrations, and registers a systemd service behind HAProxy with an auto-renewing certificate. Add --domain and --admin-email to provision HTTPS in the same run. The full setup notes live in the docs.

The precise term: reverse-tunnel relay

Plainly: your server calls out, so you never have to open a door inward. The Hub holds the far end of that call and passes your phone or Roku through it — which is how a server on your LAN answers from a friend’s house without being exposed to the internet.

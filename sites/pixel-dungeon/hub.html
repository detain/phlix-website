PHLIX HUB

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub.

Whether you're at a friend's house, traveling, or just on cellular — your library is one tap away. The Hub acts as a directory: your server registers itself, you authenticate once, and the relay keeps you connected.

Hub Source Hub Docs

HOW IT WORKS

Your server opens an outbound tunnel to the Hub relay. The relay brokers the connection — no inbound ports needed. Every connection is authenticated and encrypted. Your media never passes through the Hub's servers — only the tunnel metadata.

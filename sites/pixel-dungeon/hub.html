Phlix Hub — Your World, Connected

Sign in once. The reverse-tunnel relay handles the rest.

What the Hub Does

Cloud directory

Your servers register with the Hub. One URL gets you to any server in your party — at home or on the road.

Reverse-tunnel relay

The Hub opens an outbound connection to your server and keeps it alive. No port forwarding. No NAT punch-through scripts. The tunnel just works.

Multiple servers

Run one server at home, one at a parent's house, one on a VPS. The Hub lists them all. Pick your destination and stream.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

Public Hub

The default. Register your server with hub.phlix.app and connect immediately. No setup.

Self-Hosted Hub

Deploy phlix-hub on your own VPS. Full control over the relay, no third-party involvement. See the docs for one-command Docker deployment.

Hub Admin Docs

Hub Mode in Clients

All official clients support Hub mode. In the client settings, enter your Hub URL or email. The client authenticates against the Hub and lists your registered servers — no manual IP or port needed.

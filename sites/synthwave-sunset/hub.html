Phlix Hub

Access your media server from anywhere — even behind NAT, even on a mobile connection.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay. When your server starts, it opens an outbound connection to the Hub and establishes a persistent tunnel. Your client connects to the Hub instead of your LAN IP — the Hub routes traffic through the tunnel to your server, handling NAT traversal automatically.

You don't need to configure port forwarding, expose your router, or remember your home IP. Sign in once in the client app, select your server, and stream.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.app is free for personal use. If you need control, compliance, or higher limits, self-host the Hub — it's the same phlix-hub software.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode natively. Enable it in settings, sign in, and the client automatically discovers your registered servers. For Roku, Hub mode ships as part of the channel — the TV remote is all you need.

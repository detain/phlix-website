Self-host your media in minutes. PHP 8.3+, MySQL, FFmpeg.

Install on Ubuntu or Debian

One line, no config, works on a fresh host. Sets up PHP 8.3+, MySQL, FFmpeg, systemd service, and HAProxy with Let's Encrypt.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/detain/phlix-server/master/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash

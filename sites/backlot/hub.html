Phlix Hub

Access your server from anywhere — without opening ports.

What the Hub does

The Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that lets you reach your Phlix server from outside your network. Your server initiates an outbound connection to the Hub, and that tunnel stays open. When you connect from a client, the Hub routes traffic through it — no port forwarding required, no NAT punch-through hacks.

The Hub also provides a cloud directory: sign in once, see all your servers listed regardless of where they're running.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The phlix-hub is open source. You can run your own Hub instance, giving you full control over the relay infrastructure. Alternatively, use the public Hub operated by the project — no setup required.

Hub mode in clients

Each client (Roku, Windows, Mobile) supports Hub mode: point it at your Hub URL, sign in, and your server appears automatically. When you're on the same network, traffic goes direct. When you're remote, it routes through the Hub.

Use cases

  • Access your home library from a friend's house
  • Manage your server while traveling
  • Share access with family members without VPN configuration
  • Monitor recordings and library status remotely

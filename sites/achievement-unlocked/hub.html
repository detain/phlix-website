What the Hub Does

Reverse-Tunnel Relay

The Hub maintains an outbound connection to the relay server. When you access the Hub, it routes traffic through that tunnel — no inbound ports needed on your firewall.

NAT Traversal

Behind a NAT or CG-NAT? The Hub handles it. Your server reaches out, the Hub keeps the tunnel alive, and your clients connect through it seamlessly.

Use the Public Hub or Self-Host

The public Hub at phlix-hub is free to use. If you need more control, deploy your own Hub instance — it's open source (MPL-2.0) and runs on any PHP 8.3+ host.

Hub Mode in Clients

The Roku, Windows, and Mobile clients support Hub mode — add your Hub account once, and all your registered servers appear automatically. Switch between home, work, and travel servers without re-configuring.

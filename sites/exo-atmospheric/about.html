Our Story

Beyond the Atmosphere

Four billion years of cosmic evolution forged the matter that became us. We think that's worth celebrating with every story we stream.

The Cosmic Perspective

Exo-Atmospheric was born from a simple question: what if every media experience reminded us of our place in the cosmos?

The iron in your blood was forged in a supernova that exploded before our sun existed. The calcium in your bones was created in the heart of an ancient star. We are literally made of cosmic matter — and every story we watch connects us to that vast, infinite process.

Phlix carries that same humbling, awe-inspiring perspective into your living room. Our interfaces pull back from the daily grind to show the cosmic canvas on which all human stories play out. Press play and join the cosmos in watching.

Our Mission

Make discovering and watching the stories you love feel as expansive and meaningful as the cosmos itself — connecting every viewing moment to something larger than ourselves.

The Team

We're a distributed team of astronomers, designers, engineers, and storytellers scattered across the globe — but united by our love of the night sky and our belief that media should inspire wonder.

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

The Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your Phlix server is behind a NAT router — which it almost always is at home — there's no direct path from your phone to it on the open internet. The Hub solves that: it maintains an outbound connection to the relay, and brokers the connection for incoming clients.

You sign in once to the Hub, select which of your servers you want to reach, and your client connects through the relay — no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no third-party tunnel service.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at github.com/detain/phlix-hub is free to use. If you want full control — or don't want to send any traffic through a shared relay — you can self-host the Hub on any VPS or always-on machine with a public IP.

Public Hub

No setup. Works out of the box.

Sign in with your Phlix account and add your server. That's it.

Self-hosted

Full control. Your relay, your machine.

Clone phlix-hub, point it at a database, and configure your clients to use your instance instead.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client — Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and Mobile — supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client registers with the Hub and stays reachable even when your server is on a LAN. The Hub handles NAT traversal so you can stream from home while travelling.

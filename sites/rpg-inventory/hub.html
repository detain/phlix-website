Phlix Hub

Reach any of your servers from anywhere — without exposing your network.

What the Hub Does

Reverse-Tunnel Relay

No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. Phlix Hub handles the NAT traversal so your server sits safely behind your router while you access it from anywhere.

Cloud Directory

Sign in once to the Hub, see all your registered servers in one place. Your credentials stay local; only the tunnel relay passes through the Hub's servers.

Two Ways to Use It

Use the Public Hub

Zero configuration. Point your server at the public Hub relay and you're accessible from anywhere immediately.

View public hub

Self-Host the Hub

Run your own relay. Complete control — deploy the Hub on your own infrastructure and keep the tunnel traffic on your own servers.

Self-hosting docs

Hub Mode in Clients

All Phlix clients support Hub mode — connect through your Hub to reach your servers without VPN or port forwarding.

  • Roku — Hub mode built-in
  • Samsung Tizen — Hub mode built-in
  • Windows — Hub mode built-in
  • Mobile — Hub mode built-in

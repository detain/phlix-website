Phlix Hub

One account, any server, anywhere you go.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects you to your servers when they're behind a router or firewall — without opening any ports or configuring dynamic DNS.

Sign in once with your Phlix account. Pick a server. The Hub handles the rest, NAT traversal included.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

Public Hub

No setup required. Works out of the box for most people. Run by the Phlix community.

Self-Hosted Hub

Deploy your own phlix-hub instance on any PHP 8.3+ host. Full control, same protocol.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode: enable it, sign in once, and your server appears automatically — whether it's in the next room or on the other side of the country.

Works with: Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and Mobile (iOS + Android).

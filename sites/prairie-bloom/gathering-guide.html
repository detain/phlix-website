Host a Perfect Gathering

Family movie night, Phlix-style — every screen in step, every seat filled, every guest a kid again.

1

Set the scene — your library, ready to grow

Drop a show into your library folder. Phlix's scanner picks it up, fetches metadata, and hangs it on your shelf — like watching a sunflower bloom after rain. No manual scraping, no filename gymnastics.

Library feature →

2

Roll out the red carpet — SyncPlay

Grandpa's in the armchair, the kids are on the couch, and someone's in the kitchen. Hit play once. Everyone moves together — play, pause, seek — locked to the same frame via NTP-style sync. No one misses the good part because someone paused to get snacks.

SyncPlay feature →

3

Every screen, every room — the client family

Roku on the big screen, Tizen in the bedroom, Windows at the desk, Mobile in the kitchen. DLNA for the old TV in the corner. One server, five screens, zero friction. Everyone picks their seat.

See all clients →

4

Grow your features — plugins

Want genre tags? Subtitle providers? A custom theme? Phlix's plugin system has a versioned manifest contract. Drop it in, it's there. See the plugin example to grow your own.

Explore plugins →

5

Reach the meadow from anywhere

Hub mode means you never have to open a port or configure dynamic DNS. Sign in once, pick your server, and the reverse-tunnel relay connects you — whether you're in the next room or across the country.

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Access any of your servers from anywhere. No port forwarding required.

What the Hub Does

The Phlix Hub solves a fundamental problem: your media server sits behind your router's NAT, making it impossible to reach from outside your home network without manual port forwarding or complex VPN configuration.

The Hub provides two key services:

  • Cloud directory — A central registry that lets you find your servers no matter where they are. Your phone or client signs into the Hub, which tells it how to reach your home server.
  • Reverse-tunnel relay — When direct connectivity isn't possible (symmetric NAT, carrier-grade NAT, restrictive firewalls), the Hub relays traffic between your client and server through a persistent tunnel.

Self-host or Use the Public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free for anyone to use. It requires no account — your server registers with an anonymous tunnel ID and you connect with a secure token.

If you prefer to run your own infrastructure, the Hub is open-source and self-hostable. Run it on a VPS with a public IP and your family can connect through your own relay server.

# Self-host the Hub
git clone https://github.com/detain/phlix-hub.git
cd phlix-hub
composer install
php bin/hub-server.php

Hub Mode in Clients

Each Phlix client supports connecting directly to a server on your local network or through the Hub for remote access. Enable Hub mode to:

  • Access your home server when you're away
  • Switch seamlessly between multiple servers
  • Use the same account across devices without complex VPN setups

When Hub mode is active, the client first connects to the Hub to locate your registered servers, then establishes either a direct connection (when possible) or a relayed connection through the Hub.

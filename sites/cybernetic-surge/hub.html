Relay

The neural relay that connects your servers across NAT boundaries. One sign-in. Access anywhere.

What the Relay does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that handles NAT traversal. Instead of exposing your server to the internet or configuring port forwards, your server establishes an outbound tunnel to the Hub. Clients connect to the Hub, which routes traffic through the tunnel to your server.

This means you can access your media server from a friend's house, your phone on cellular, or any network that blocks incoming connections — without opening a single port.

Two deployment options

Public Hub

Use the hosted Hub operated by the Phlix project. No configuration required — just sign in and your server appears.

Self-hosted

Run your own Hub instance. Full control, complete privacy. The Hub is open-source and self-hostable on any PHP 8.3+ server.

Hub mode in clients

All native clients support Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects to the Hub instead of directly to your server IP. The same sign-in works across all your devices and all your servers.

Switching between your home server and a friend's server is seamless — your library list updates, and playback starts through the appropriate relay.

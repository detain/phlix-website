Phlix Hub

Reach any of your servers from anywhere

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. Sign in once, and the Hub keeps a live connection to your servers behind NAT — no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no exposing your network.

Reverse-tunnel relay

The Hub maintains an outbound connection from your server to the relay. When you access the Hub, it routes traffic through that established connection — your server stays unreachable from the internet, but you can still reach it.

NAT traversal

No router configuration needed. The Hub handles the NAT problem by never requiring incoming connections to your server in the first place.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at phlix-hub.detain.io is free to use. If you prefer to run your own infrastructure, the Hub is open source and self-hostable.

Public Hub

No setup. Sign up and go. Great for personal use.

Self-hosted

Full control. Run it on your own infrastructure.

Hub mode in clients

Phlix clients support Hub mode, which means they can discover and connect to your servers through the Hub automatically. When you're away from home, your client finds your server via the Hub without any manual configuration.

  • Sign in once with your Hub account
  • All your servers appear automatically
  • No port forwarding or network config needed
  • Works on Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and Mobile

Features

Every capability you need, grown to fit your media library

Library that organizes itself

Add a file, see it appear. Phlix watches your folders for changes, recognizes naming like S01E02 and (2020), and fills in the titles, descriptions, and artwork for you — no renaming rituals, no manual entry. The library evolves with your collection, automatically detecting changes and updating without manual intervention.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Watch together, perfectly in step. Phlix keeps every device's playback clock tightly synced, so play, pause, and seek move together for everyone in the room — or an ocean away. Movie night stays in sync whether you're in the same room or across the globe.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

Every device gets the right quality on its own — dedicated profiles for phones, web, and 4K TVs. When a file won't play directly, Phlix transcodes it on the fly with FFmpeg (H.264 defaults to CRF 23, the sweet spot between quality and size) and delivers it as adaptive HLS that rides out network hiccups. If your server has a compatible Intel or AMD GPU, hardware profiles run the heavy work on it instead of the CPU. Every device gets exactly what it can play best.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

One account, up to 5 personal profiles, each lockable with a 4- or 6-digit PIN. Passwords are protected with Argon2ID — today's strongest widely recommended hashing — and sign-ins stay secure with tokens that refresh themselves. Rating filters from G to NC-17 keep kids' profiles to kids' content. Bring your existing identity too: OIDC, LDAP, and passkeys all work, so Phlix fits the sign-in you already run. Keep your library organized and your family safe.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

Watch, pause, and record live TV. Schedule a recording once and it happens on time; the on-screen guide opens straight to what's on now — no clicking through menus. Watch live TV or record your favorites for later.

DLNA for the devices you already own

Phlix speaks DLNA — the streaming standard already built into most older smart TVs, game consoles, and receivers — so the devices you own can play your library with no app to install. Discovery via SSDP finds devices automatically on your network.

Plugin system with a real contract

A versioned plugin contract. Install it at Settings → Plugins, and the server loads it once enabled. Write metadata providers, new data sources, or entirely new features. See phlix-plugin-example for a working starter.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. A relay you run reaches your home server without opening ports. Fully self-hostable. Access your media from anywhere without exposing your server to the internet.

Every Trick in the Bag

The Lineup

A full trick set for your media library. SyncPlay keeps everyone locked in. Library serves instantly. Transcoding handles any format. And that is just the warm-up run.

Lead Trick

SyncPlay

Synchronized playback across every device in your network — or across the world. One play command, everyone locked to the exact same frame. Perfect for movie nights, sports viewing, or staying in step across distances.

Lead Trick

Library

Instant access to your entire collection. Auto-metadata fetching, poster art, resume points, watched states, ratings, and smart collections — all organized and served at race speed.

Hardware Transcoding

GPU-accelerated format conversion handles any codec, any container, any device. Remux, H.264, H.265, VP9, AV1 — if it exists, Phlix plays it. No quality loss, no stuttering, no compromise.

Live TV + DVR

Plug in a tuner, scan for channels, and cut the cord. Live TV with full program guide, recording schedules, and cloud DVR storage. Sports, news, local broadcasts — all on the board.

User Management

Multi-user with fine-grained access control. Parental locks, user profiles, watch history, ratings, and custom PINs. Everyone gets their own run — nobody crashes into anyone else.

DLNA / Chromecast

Broadcast to any DLNA device or Chromecast on your network. Cast a title directly from your library to the big screen — no app, no remote, just your library in the room.

Plugin Ecosystem

Extend the run with the plugin library. Anime catalogs, international sports, auto-download, notification triggers, and custom metadata sources — built by the community, for the community.

Phlix Hub

The remote access relay that keeps your server reachable from anywhere. SSL encrypted, zero-config, and runs on your existing server — no third-party cloud required.

Pick Your Platform

Server, client, or both. Phlix runs on everything from a Raspberry Pi to a dedicated rack.

Extend the Run

Plugins

The community has built an arsenal. Anime catalogs, international sports streams, auto-downloads, notification bots, custom metadata sources — if it is not in the core, a plugin has it.

AniList Catalog

v2.4

Syncs with your AniList watchlist. Auto-downloads airing episodes, fetches episode summaries, and pulls artwork directly from the AniList database.

Anime Catalog

LiveSports Tracker

v1.8

Aggregates live sports streams from multiple community sources. Soccer, basketball, MMA, motorsport — with auto-recording for when you cannot watch live.

Sports Live TV

AutoDownload

v3.1

Monitors RSS feeds, Trakt lists, and Sonarr/Radarr for new content. Queues downloads automatically based on your quality preferences and storage limits.

Automation Downloads

Push Notifications

v1.5

Sends alerts to Discord, Telegram, Slack, and email when new content is added, recordings complete, or user activity is detected.

Notifications Automation

Tautulli Sync

v2.0

Syncs watch history, ratings, and user profiles with Tautulli for enhanced analytics and Plex compatibility layer support.

Analytics Sync

Webhook Triggers

v1.2

Trigger custom webhooks on any event: playback start, library update, user login, recording complete. Connect to any service with a URL.

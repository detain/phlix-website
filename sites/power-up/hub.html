Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere — without opening a port.

What the Hub does

The Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that sits between your Phlix client and your server. When you're on your home network, the client connects directly. When you're elsewhere, the Hub maintains a persistent outbound connection to your server and relays traffic through itself — so you never need to open inbound ports, configure DDNS, or expose your server to the internet.

The Hub also acts as a cloud directory: sign in once, see all your servers listed, pick which one to connect to. One account, many servers, zero port forwarding.

Use the public Hub

No setup required. The public hub at hub.phlix.io is available for anyone with a Phlix server. Sign in, register your server, and you're connected.

Self-host the Hub

The Hub is open-source (phlix-hub on GitHub, MPL-2.0). Self-host it on your own VPS or home server for full control over your relay infrastructure.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client has a Hub toggle in its settings. With Hub mode enabled, the client automatically routes connections through the Hub when you're off your home network, and connects directly when you're at home. No manual switching required.

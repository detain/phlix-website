Plugins

Extend Phlix with a real, versioned plugin contract. Drop in, it works.

How the plugin system works

1

Implement LifecycleInterface in your plugin class.

2

Create a manifest.json with your plugin name, version, and entry point.

3

Drop the plugin folder into plugins/ — the loader picks it up on the next request.

The loader reads the manifest, instantiates your class, and calls your lifecycle hooks at the right moments. No registration, no restart.

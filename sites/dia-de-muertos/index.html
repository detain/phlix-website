Why gather here?

Why settle for a library that isn't yours?

A rented library is someone else's altar — it changes without warning, and one night the story you loved is simply gone. Phlix hands the altar back to you — your hardware, your catalogue, your rules about who may gather around the flame.

"Death is not the end — it is the reunion." — the Día de Muertos conviction

  • 100% self-hostable — your library never leaves your hardware unless you say so
  • Native clients on Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile, plus any DLNA device
  • Real-time SyncPlay with NTP-style time sync so movie night actually stays in sync
  • Multi-source metadata: TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO with 24-hour cache
  • Adaptive HLS streaming, FFmpeg transcoding, per-device quality profiles
  • Live TV with DVR + EPG guide integration
  • Plugin system with a versioned manifest contract
The precise term: NTP-style time sync

Plainly: every screen agrees on what "now" means. Phlix measures the round-trip delay to each device several times and takes a weighted mean of the offsets — the same trick the internet's clocks use — so a pause in one room lands on the same frame in the next.

Your altar awaits — press play.

Light your first candle in three unhurried steps. The first is one line in a terminal. 

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/detain/phlix-server/master/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash

Installs PHP 8.3+, MySQL and FFmpeg, then registers the service — on a fresh Ubuntu or Debian host.

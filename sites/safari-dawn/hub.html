Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

The Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that handles NAT traversal for you. When your server is behind a router without port forwarding, the Hub connects your clients to it through an outbound tunnel. No firewall configuration, no static IP, no third-party tunnel service.

Self-host or use the public hub

Run your own Hub instance for full control, or use the public Hub operated by the project. Both speak the same protocol — your clients connect the same way either way. The public Hub is free for personal use.

Hub mode in clients

Each native client supports Hub mode: sign in once, and the client lists all servers registered to your account. Pick one, stream. The client maintains the tunnel connection in the background — pause, resume, switch servers without reconnecting.

Phlix Hub

Reach any of your servers from anywhere — no port forwarding, no third-party tunnel, no drama.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. It solves the NAT traversal problem so you can stream from your home server to your phone at a friend's house, or access your parents' library from across the country — without touching your router settings.

When your Phlix server registers with the Hub, it establishes an outbound tunnel. Clients connect to the Hub, which routes traffic through that tunnel. Nothing needs to be opened on your firewall.

Self-host or use the public hub

You have options:

  • Public Hub — Use the hosted hub at phlix-hub. No setup, sign in and go. Fine for most people.
  • Self-hosted Hub — Run your own hub on a VPS or a always-on machine. Full control, zero third-party involvement.

The Hub mode in your client settings lets you point at either. Switching takes seconds.

Hub mode in clients

Each client app has a Hub setting in its server connection flow. Enable Hub mode, sign in once, and the client will list all servers registered to your account — whether they're in the next room or on another continent.

This works with:

  • Roku channel
  • Samsung Tizen app
  • Windows desktop app
  • Mobile (iOS + Android) app

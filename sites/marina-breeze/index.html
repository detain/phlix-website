Why Anchor Here?

Library that organizes itself

Every film and episode you own, catalogued and ready to stream to any screen in your harbor.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Movie night across any distance — everyone locked to the same frame, perfectly in sync.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k with HLS master playlists.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with Argon2ID, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filters.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal. Self-hostable or use the public hub.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder — scheduled recordings with a guide that doesn't make you click through menus.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up.

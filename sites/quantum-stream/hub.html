How the relay functions

No Port Forwarding Required

The Quantum Relay creates an outbound connection from your server to our relay infrastructure. No inbound ports opened on your router. No VPN configuration. Just observe.

1. Server Connects

Your quantum core establishes a secure outbound WebSocket tunnel to the relay infrastructure.

2. Observer Requests

You access your relay URL from any device. The relay routes your request through the established tunnel.

3. State Collapses

Your media library responds through the relay. The wave function is observed from anywhere.

Security Specifications

End-to-end AES-256 encryption between server and relay
TLS 1.3 for all observer connections
Token-based authentication with rotating session keys
No media data stored on relay infrastructure — only tunneled
Connection automatically terminates after 30 days of inactivity

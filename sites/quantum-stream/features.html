Technical specifications

Core Modules

Each module collapses the wave function of your media infrastructure into precise, measurable performance.

Neural Sync

Synchronized playback across all connected observers. When one device starts, pauses, or seeks, all devices follow. The quantum state remains coherent across your entire network — multiple screens, one reality.

  • Sub-100ms sync latency across LAN
  • Supports up to 16 simultaneous observers
  • Automatic quality adjustment based on network state
  • Chat overlay for synchronized commentary

Adaptive Decoherence

Real-time quality transcoding that measures your bandwidth and device capabilities. The observer effect in action — your network conditions shape the exact output quality you receive.

  • Hardware-accelerated encoding (QSV, NVENC, VCE)
  • Per-profile quality memories that persist
  • 4K HDR to 720p SDR automatic fallback
  • Audio pass-through for DTS-HD, TrueHD, Atmos

Quantum Relay

Access your server from anywhere without opening ports or configuring VPN. Your library exists in superposition — reachable from any location until you observe it.

  • End-to-end encrypted tunnel
  • No port forwarding required
  • Static relay endpoints available
  • Bandwidth-optimized for cellular connections

Library State

Your entire media collection organized, tagged, and ready for measurement. Automatic metadata fetching from multiple sources with conflict resolution.

  • Movie, TV, and music libraries
  • Automatic subtitle scraping
  • Poster and fanart retrieval
  • Collection detection and marking

Live TV Observation

Integrate broadcast TV, DVR, and IPTV streams into your quantum field. Collapse the uncertainty of what's on right now into definitive programming.

  • HDHR and DVR integration
  • EPG guide data with recording
  • Commercial skipping on recorded content
  • Streaming client for mobile and web

Additional modules

Authentication

Multi-user access with role-based permissions. Each observer gets their own collapsed state of the library.

Plugin Ecosystem

Extend functionality with community modules. Authentication, metadata, and notification plugins.

DLNA Observation

Legacy device support via DLNA/UPnP. Older observers can still measure your quantum field.

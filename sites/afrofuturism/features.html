What Phlix offers

The Constellations

Eight pillars of power — each one a star in Phlix's cosmos. Together they form a media sanctuary that stays true to you.

SyncPlay

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together, across the room or across the country. One moment. One heart. All of you.

Works with all native clients: Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile. No special configuration — everyone just presses play and stays in sync.

Library

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) title formats, ItemRepository hydrates the metadata JSON. Add a file, and it appears — your heritage organized and enriched.

Multi-source metadata: TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO with a 24-hour cache. QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and TV-4K.

Transcoding

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and TV-4K. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. The right quality for every connection, automatically.

Direct play when the client supports it; adaptive HLS otherwise. Per-device quality profiles pick the right bitrate so your library looks its best on every screen.

Multi-User Auth

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4 or 6-digit PINs, and a rating filter from G to NC-17. Your cosmos, your family's cosmos.

Each profile has its own watch history, continue-watching queue, and preferences — isolated and private.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that does not make you click through menus. Watch live, record what matters, come back to it.

Built-in electronic program guide integration. Set recordings once, come back anytime.

DLNA

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV does not need a new app. Any DLNA device on your network can see and play your library — immediately.

Discovery via SSDP. No configuration, no install — the TV just sees your Phlix server and plays.

Plugin System

LifecycleInterface plus a versioned manifest contract. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. Build your own stars into the cosmos.

See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. Extend metadata, add new sources, build entirely new capabilities.

Hub — The Bridge Home

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal. Self-host the hub on your own server, or use the public one. Either way, your library follows you anywhere.

No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no third-party tunnel service required. Access every server you own from a single Hub account.

What Phlix does

Eight pillars of power
for your cosmic library.

Every feature is built to honor your media and your sovereignty. No subscription. No tracking. No compromises.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. Phlix handles the busywork so your library builds itself — automatically, intelligently, without friction.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Movie night doesn't drift. Distance doesn't diminish the communal experience.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. The right quality for every device — automatically, without manual intervention.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Every profile is protected. Every household member has their own space.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. The future of live television is already here — and it's yours to own.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. The devices in your home are already part of your Phlix — discoverable, accessible, ready to play.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working implementation. Extensibility that doesn't require forking the core.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Your library follows you across any network boundary — without configuring routers or paying for a static IP.

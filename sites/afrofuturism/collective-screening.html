How we watch together

Collective Screening

Three screens. One film. Zero drift. Here is how Phlix turns self-hosting and SyncPlay into a lived ritual for the community.

The ritual

1

Keep your library on your own ground

The films live on a server in your home — a Raspberry Pi, an old desktop, a mini PC that fits behind the TV. It is quiet, it is yours, and it does not call home to anyone.

Phlix Server runs on PHP 8.3+ with MySQL. One command on Ubuntu or Debian gets you a running server, a database, a systemd service, and an optional HTTPS proxy with Let's Encrypt — all in under ten minutes.

Start the install
2

Open a bridge — not a port

Your family and friends do not need to be on your LAN. Phlix Hub uses a reverse tunnel: your server reaches out to the Hub and keeps the connection open. From anywhere, through any network, they open the Hub and see your server listed.

No router configuration. No dynamic DNS. No third-party tunnel service. If your server can reach the internet, the Hub works — even through CGNAT, captive portals, and hotel WiFi.

Learn about the Hub
3

Lock to the same frame, always

When everyone joins a SyncPlay session, Phlix samples the clock offset between all clients five times, computes a weighted mean, and keeps every screen locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. One grandmother in Lagos, one cousin in New York, one friend in Toronto.

SyncPlay works with every native client: Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and Mobile (beta). No plugins, no special setup. Create a session, share the room code, and everyone presses play at the same moment.

See how SyncPlay works
4

The collective in context

Phlix was built for the diaspora family, the film club, the heritage collector — people who share stories across distances and need those stories to arrive intact and in sync. The stack that makes this possible: a self-hosted library (heritage, control), a reverse tunnel (reach, no infrastructure), and a time-sync engine (the shared moment).

Every ancestor was a star. Every story, a galaxy. This is what it looks like when that runs on modern infrastructure.

Our philosophy

The stack, in brief

SyncPlay

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples. Play, pause, seek — all synchronized. Supported on all native clients.

Hub

Reverse-tunnel relay via WebSocket. Self-hostable or public. Works through CGNAT and captive portals.

Library

Folder-watcher, TMDB/TVDB/Fanart.tv metadata, 24-hour cache, local NFO support. Your archive, enriched.

Clients

Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile (beta), plus any DLNA device. Five portals, no new hardware required.

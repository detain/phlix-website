Remote access

The cosmos was always yours.
Now it follows you anywhere.

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects you to your home server from anywhere in the galaxy — without touching your router.

What the Hub does

Reverse-tunnel relay

Your server opens an outbound tunnel to the Hub. Your client connects to the Hub. NAT traversal solved — no port forwarding, no static IP, no configuring your router.

Cloud directory

Sign in once, see all your servers. The Hub maintains a lightweight directory of your registered servers — their names, their status, their connection endpoints.

Self-hostable

Run your own Hub. The Hub is open-source, built on the same stack as Phlix server. You control the relay — no third party in the middle of your stream.

Self-host or use the public hub

Public Hub

Use the hosted public Hub at no charge. No setup, no maintenance. Your server registers with the Hub, and your clients connect through it. Works immediately.

Learn about the public Hub

Self-hosted Hub

Deploy phlix-hub on any PHP 8.3 server. Point your Phlix servers at it. All tunnel traffic flows through your own infrastructure — you have the keys.

phlix-hub on GitHub

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode: when enabled, the client connects through the Hub rather than directly to your LAN server. This means you can be on a different network entirely — at a friend's house, traveling, anywhere — and your library appears as if you're on the same local network. All streaming is relayed through the Hub tunnel. SyncPlay works over Hub mode too.

Each client (Roku, Windows, Mobile) has a Hub toggle in its connection settings. Enter your Hub credentials once, and the client remembers.

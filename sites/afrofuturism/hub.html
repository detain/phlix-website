The Bridge Home

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles the NAT. Your library follows you — to a friend's house, a hotel room, a second home. Anywhere.

How the bridge works

Your Phlix server on your home network cannot be reached from the internet — that is how NAT works. Phlix Hub solves this by reversing the connection: your server reaches out to the Hub, and the Hub keeps that connection open.

When you open the Hub app or web interface from anywhere, the Hub routes your request through the already-open tunnel to your server. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no third-party tunnel service.

The Hub can be self-hosted on a VPS or small server you control, or you can use the public Hub operated by the Phlix project. Either way, the tunnel is encrypted and only you have access.

Public Hub

Use the Hub operated by the Phlix project. No setup, no server to maintain. Sign in with your Hub account and add your servers.

Free for personal use.

Self-Hosted Hub

Run the Hub on your own VPS or a spare machine. Full control over your tunnel infrastructure. The Hub is open source.

Requires a server with a public IP.

What Hub enables

  • Access any of your servers from a single Hub account
  • Encrypted tunnel — only you have access
  • No router configuration, no static IP, no third-party tunnel service
  • Works behind CGNAT, captive portals, and hotel networks
  • Hub mode in all native clients — Windows, Roku, Tizen, Mobile
  • Share temporary access with friends and family — time-limited tokens

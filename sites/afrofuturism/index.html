Heritage Vault

Adaptive Transcoding

CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Per-device quality profiles pick the right bitrate automatically.

Multi-User Auth

JWT with Argon2ID, up to 5 profiles per user, 4 or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17.

Hub — The Bridge Home

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Access any of your servers from anywhere — or self-host the hub.

Also: Live TV with DVR + EPG, DLNA for the devices you already own, and a plugin system with a versioned manifest contract.

Why the ancestors built this for you

Power kept in your own hands

  • 100% self-hostable — your library never leaves your hardware unless you say so.
  • Native clients on Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile, plus any DLNA device — no new hardware required.
  • Real-time SyncPlay with NTP-style time sync so movie night actually stays in sync across every screen.
  • Multi-source metadata: TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO with 24-hour cache — your library, enriched.
  • Adaptive HLS streaming, FFmpeg transcoding, per-device quality profiles — right quality for every connection.
  • Live TV with DVR + EPG guide integration — channel manager, guide manager, recorder, all built in.
  • Plugin system with a versioned manifest contract — drop in an extension, the loader picks it up. Build your own stars.

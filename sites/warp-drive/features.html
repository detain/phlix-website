Features

Everything you need to stream, organize, and share your media — on your own hardware.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. Phlix watches your directories and automatically picks up new media, organizes it by series and season, and pulls in poster art, descriptions, and cast info from TMDB, TVDB, and Fanart.tv. Local NFO files are respected when present, and metadata is cached for 24 hours to keep your library snappy without hammering external APIs.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. No more "wait, did you see that?" messages. SyncPlay in Phlix uses NTP-style time synchronization to keep all connected clients at exactly the same playback position, even when network latency varies. Start a movie night, invite friends, and watch in perfect sync whether they're in the next room or across the country.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Phlix automatically detects each client's capabilities and picks the right quality profile — mobile devices get lower bitrate, 4K TVs get full quality. When direct play isn't supported, FFmpeg steps in to transcode on the fly. Per-device quality profiles remember your preferences, so your phone doesn't re-encode every time you start a new session.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Every family member can have their own profile with their own watch history, continue-watching queue, and ratings. Parents can lock profiles with a PIN and set content restrictions by rating level. All passwords are hashed with Argon2ID, and JWT tokens provide secure, stateless authentication with refresh token rotation.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Connect an antenna or cable feed, and Phlix scans for available channels, organizes the EPG guide data, and lets you schedule recordings in advance. The guide shows what's on now and coming up next, and recordings are stored in your library alongside your other media — no separate app needed.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Phlix implements the standard DLNA/UPnP protocols — ContentDirectory for browsing media, AvTransport for playback control, and SSDP for device discovery. Point any DLNA-compatible device at your Phlix server and start streaming immediately. No app to install, no account to create.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example. The plugin system is not an afterthought — it's a first-class extension point with a versioned manifest contract. Implement LifecycleInterface, describe your plugin in the manifest, drop it into the plugins directory, and Phlix loads it automatically. Write metadata providers, custom transcoders, new authentication backends, or entirely new features without forking the core.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. Sign in once and see all your servers, whether they're at home, at a friend's place, or in a data center. The Hub handles NAT traversal so you never need to configure port forwarding or expose your server to the internet. Self-host the Hub for full control, or use the public instance — both are fully functional.

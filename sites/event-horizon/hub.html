The Hub

The Singularity at the Center

Your personal web portal. The last stable orbit before you cross into the full singularity of your media library. Accessible from anywhere on any device.

Access from anywhere

The Hub is a web interface that lives at the center of your Phlix installation. Open it in any browser — desktop, mobile, tablet — and your entire library is there, pulled into a single gravitational point.

No port forwarding required with built-in tunneling. No complex VPN setup. The Hub is your photon sphere — the last orbit before the plunge.

Hub Capabilities

Global Search

Search your entire library from any device. Metadata, titles, descriptions — the gravitational index of everything you own.

User Management

Local accounts, LDAP integration, OAuth2 SSO. Control who crosses the horizon and what they can access.

Media Upload

Upload files directly through the Hub. Drag and drop onto the singularity. The library absorbs it.

Settings & Config

Configure the singularity's mass. Library paths, transcoding preferences, user permissions, plugin management — all through the Hub.

Library Browser

Navigate your media as a visual grid. Poster views, list views, filtering by genre, year, resolution. The gravity organizes everything.

Activity & Logs

See what's being streamed, who's watching, and when. The singularity records everything that passes through its horizon.

Features

Everything you need to stream your media, your way — with the control that only self-hosting provides.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. Metadata is cached for 24 hours and refreshed on change.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Works through the Phlix Hub for remote sessions.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Direct play when the client supports it to save CPU.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Per-profile watch history and resume positions.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. EPG data from standard sources, recordings stored locally.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Discovery via SSDP. Works with any DLNA-certified device on your network.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. Plugins can extend metadata providers, notification handlers, and more. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal so you can reach your home server from anywhere. Self-host the hub, or use the public instance at phlix-hub.

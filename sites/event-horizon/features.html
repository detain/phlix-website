The Eight Gravity Wells

Every function has mass. Every capability warps the space around it. These are the forces that hold your media library together.

Library that warps around your content

Folder-watcher tracks mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) formats, gravitational lensing makes your metadata shimmer at the edges. Add a file, watch it spiral into place.

SyncPlay — everyone falls together

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — the entire room moves in unison toward the same singularity.

Transcoding that bends spacetime

FFmpeg remuxes H.264, H.265, VP9, AV1 in real time. Hardware acceleration on Intel/AMD/NVIDIA. The bottleneck is not the code — it is the speed of light.

Auth — the gateway to the void

Local accounts, LDAP, and OAuth2 (Google, GitHub). Jellyfin/API key auth. The login screen is the event horizon — you can turn back, but you won't.

Live TV pulls in the spectrum

DVR with EPG from tv_grab_zzip. HDHomeRun, analog tuner, and IPTV support. The program guide is your map of the electromagnetic spectrum.

DLNA — the universal gravitational constant

Every device on your network sees your library as if by magic. TVs, game consoles, speakers — all orbiting the same singularity of content.

Plugins — dark matter of your library

Community plugins add new capabilities: notifications, automation, metadata enrichment. The plugin ecosystem is the unseen mass that holds the galaxy together.

The Hub — the photon sphere of your identity

Your personal web portal follows you anywhere. The Hub is the last stable orbit before you cross into the singularity of your media library.

Phlix Hub — Relay

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere. No port forwarding. No exposing your home network.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay. Your server connects outbound to the Hub and maintains that tunnel. When you open your Phlix client, the Hub routes traffic through that existing connection — your server never opens an inbound port.

Two ways to run it

Use the public hub

No setup. Point your server at the public Hub address and your clients at the same. Works immediately.

Self-host the Hub

Run phlix-hub on any VPS. Full control; your metadata never touches a third party.

Hub mode in clients

Roku, Windows, and the Mobile app all include Hub mode — connect to your server through the Hub with a single server URL. No configuration files, no certificates to manage. Enable Hub mode in your client settings and enter your Hub address.

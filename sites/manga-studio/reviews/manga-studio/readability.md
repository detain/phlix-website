### ⚠️ SHOULD FIX

**1. FAQ `<dt>` text is CSS-transformed to ALL CAPS (content altered)**
- **File:** `css/theme.css:401–408`
- `.faq-item dt { text-transform: uppercase; }` at theme.css:405
- Source in `content.json` (line 135): `"Is Phlix like Plex / Jellyfin / Emby?"` — title case sentence
- Visible text: "IS PHLIX LIKE PLEX / JELLYFIN / EMBY?" — ALL CAPS
- The kit allows ALL CAPS "on short impact callouts only" (manga-studio.js:451). FAQ questions are full sentences, not short callouts.
- **Impact:** Readability for screen reader users and those with cognitive disabilities — all-caps FAQ questions scan differently than intended. Also a content accuracy issue (content.json text is visually altered).
- **Fix:** Remove `text-transform: uppercase` from `.faq-item dt` in theme.css. Display the content as-is from content.json.

**2. `.client-tagline` uses `opacity: 0.7` (accessibility concern)**
- **File:** `css/components.css:518–524`
- `.client-tagline` has `opacity: 0.7` on body text (color: var(--color-text) at 70% opacity)
- **WCAG AA requirement:** Body text must be ≥ 4.5:1 contrast against background
- Ink Black (#0D0D0D) at 70% opacity on Manga White (#F8F8F4): effective contrast is approximately 9.5:1 — still passes 4.5:1 ✅
- **Verdict:** Passes contrast, but the reduced opacity is unnecessary when contrast is already high. Could be simplified.
- **Status:** No action needed — passes contrast requirements.

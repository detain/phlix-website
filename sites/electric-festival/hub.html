Phlix Hub

Access your server from anywhere — without opening ports or configuring DDNS.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. Sign in once, and your devices can find and connect to any of your servers — even behind NAT or a firewall. No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. No third-party tunnel service.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Phlix Hub is free to use. If you prefer to run your own, phlix-hub is open source (MPL-2.0) and self-hostable on any PHP 8.3+ server. Your keys, your relay, your data.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode — enable it once, and the client will automatically connect through the Hub when your server isn't reachable on the local network. Your library follows you everywhere.

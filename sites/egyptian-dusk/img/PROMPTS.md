## UI Generation Rules (from brand kit, for reference)

1. Background is always Black Silt Night (#0A0603) or Khufu's Shadow (#130C05).
2. At most two accent colors per screen — Gold and Lapis, or Gold and Terracotta.
3. Primary CTA is always Pharaoh Gold with Black Silt Night text.
4. Near-square corners (1px radius) except cartouche/pill for badge shapes.
5. Use the spacing scale; prefer generous spacing — darkness reads as ceremony.
6. Max content width 1400px.
7. All text must meet WCAG AA against its dark background.

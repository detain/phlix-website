Your archive organizes itself

Files carved into place, metadata illuminated by sacred scrolls. Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 titles, and your library appears — ready for the eternal archive.

Every device locked to the same eternal frame

Passage and play remain one across all halls of the kingdom. NTP-synchronized playback — everyone moves together, whether in the same room or across the Nile.

Transcoding

QualitySelector profiles for every device. CRF 23/28 HLS with variant playlists — the right quality, chosen automatically.

Multi-profile auth

JWT with Argon2ID, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, and parental controls from G to NC-17.

Phlix Hub

Reverse-tunnel relay reaches your server anywhere. Self-hostable, or use the public hub — your archive, always within reach.

Live TV + DVR

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that does not make you click through menus.

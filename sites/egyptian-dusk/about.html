The Cartouche

Phlix is the keeper of your archive. The philosophy beneath the code.

Our Philosophy

Three thousand years before the first cinema, pharaohs commanded their scribes to carve stories into stone — not for entertainment, but for eternity. The walls of Karnak, the papyrus of the Book of the Dead, the golden mask of Tutankhamun: each was a screen on which the greatest stories of civilization played out against the backdrop of gods, death, and rebirth.

Phlix was built at that threshold — the dusk hour when Ra crosses the horizon and the eternal kingdom opens its gates. We believe watching is a sacred act. Your library is your archive, your collection an offering to the stories that endure. Phlix is the keeper; you are the pharaoh.

Every design decision we make serves permanence: a server you run, files you own, a system that asks nothing of the cloud unless you ask something of it first.

Contributing

Phlix lives in the detain GitHub organization. The server, hub, clients, shared libraries, documentation, and plugin example are all open to issues, pull requests, and community contribution.

If you find a bug or have a feature request, open an issue on the relevant repository. If you want to build a plugin, start with phlix-plugin-example — it is the smallest working implementation and the best place to learn the contract.

Kheper's Answers from the Archive

The eternal archivist speaks from the depths of the kingdom's archive.

Is Phlix like Plex / Jellyfin / Emby?
Yes — same job, different stack. Phlix is built in PHP 8.3+ on Workerman, ships with a versioned plugin contract, and includes a hub for accessing remote servers behind NAT without a third-party tunnel.
Do I need to expose my server to the internet?
No. Run Phlix on your LAN and use the Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay to reach it from your phone or Roku at a friend's house. You can self-host the hub, or use the public one.
What formats are supported?
Anything FFmpeg can read. Direct play when the client supports it; transcoded HLS otherwise. Per-device quality profiles pick the right bitrate automatically.
Is there a mobile app?
Yes — React Native, available on iOS and Android. Currently in beta.
Can I write plugins?
Yes. Implement LifecycleInterface, ship a manifest, drop it in the plugins directory. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Build Your Archive

A ceremonial walkthrough — from the first inscription to the eternal archive. Three passages, each a step toward becoming the keeper.

I

Open the tomb — install Phlix Server

One line on a fresh Ubuntu or Debian host. The installer carves out a dedicated system user, creates the database, sets the environment, runs migrations, and lights the systemd service. Within minutes, the archive stands ready at http://your-server:8096.

See the inscription
II

Choose your sanctums — install the clients

Native clients on Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and Mobile (iOS + Android) — plus any DLNA device that shares your network. Five sanctuaries where the archive is always within reach. Install once; return forever.

Explore the Pantheon
III

Illuminate the scrolls — organize your library

Drop files into your watch folder. Phlix watches the mtimes, parses S01E02 or (2020) titles, and calls on the metadata providers — TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, local NFO — to illuminate each entry like a scroll in the Hall of Two Truths. The archive organizes itself.

IV

Set the guards — configure auth and profiles

JWT with Argon2ID, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, and a rating filter from G to NC-17. Set the guards at the entrance to your archive — so the kingdom admits only those you invite.

V

Open the Mirror — reach your archive anywhere

Register your server with the Phlix Hub. Sign in on any client with your Hub credentials — and the archive follows you across the Nile. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no third-party eyes on your library.

Enter the Mirror
VI

Write your own scrolls — extend with plugins

Implement LifecycleInterface, ship a manifest, drop it in the plugins directory. The loader discovers and activates it. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter — the foundation of your own additions to the archive.

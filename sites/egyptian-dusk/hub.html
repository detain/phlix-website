The Mirror

A sacred place where your archives reflect across the Nile to distant shores — and return unchanged.

What the Mirror Does

Reverse-tunnel relay

Your Phlix Server stays on your LAN. The Hub's reverse tunnel opens a temporary, authenticated path outward — so your phone or a Roku at a friend's house can reach the archive without you exposing any ports.

NAT traversal

No manual port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. The Hub handles the traversal — you only need an internet connection and a Phlix Server running behind it.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

The Hub is self-hostable — run it on your own infrastructure and the relay stays entirely within your control. Or connect to the public Hub maintained by the project, which routes your tunnel traffic but never stores or inspects your media library.

Once your server is registered with the Hub, sign in once on any client and the Mirror does the rest — Ra's light travels the tunnel and finds you wherever you are.

Hub Mode in the Clients

Every official client supports Hub mode. On first launch, sign in with your Hub credentials — the client learns your server's address through the Hub's directory and connects directly when you're home, or through the tunnel when you're away.

The transition is seamless: you do not notice when the path changes, and no action is required on your part.

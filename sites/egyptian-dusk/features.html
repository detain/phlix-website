Sacred Scrolls

Eight chambers of the eternal archive — each a scroll revealing a facet of the kingdom's power.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. The archive maintains itself like the stars — eternally and without instruction.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Ra's light falls equally on all who gather in the kingdom.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Every frame rendered at its appropriate resolution.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, and rating filter from G to NC-17. The kingdom admits only those you invite.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that does not make you click through menus. The archive watches while you rest.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV does not need a new app. The kingdom extends to every corner of the house without ceremony.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter — write your own additions to the archive's ceremony.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal. Self-hostable, or use the public hub — your archive follows you across the Nile to distant shores.

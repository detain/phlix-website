**Tool:** Inline SVG (hand-crafted)
**Prompt used:** Phlix logo with waveform pulse indicator — ambient/meditative aesthetic with purple (#7B68EE) to turquoise (#00CED1) to coral (#FF6B9D) gradient wordmark, animated waveform bars suggesting music visualization.

## favicon.svg

**Tool:** Inline SVG (hand-crafted)
**Prompt used:** Square favicon with dark background (#0D0D1A), simple waveform bars in purple-to-turquoise gradient, rounded corners.

**Tool:** `node tools/gen-og.mjs --site ambient-pulse`
**Prompt:** Ambient Pulse brand kit social share card — dark navy background (#0D0D1A), Phlix wordmark with gradient, waveform decorative elements, tagline "Your media. Your library. Your Phlix."

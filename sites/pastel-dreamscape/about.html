Our Dream

The soft, open-source story of Phlix.

The belief behind the build

Somewhere above the everyday world, past the last row of houses and just beyond where the sky turns the softest shade of pink, there is a place made entirely of clouds and color. This is where Phlix was born — the belief that finding something magical to watch should itself feel magical.

Phlix is open-source software you can run on your own hardware. Your library never leaves your home unless you choose to share it. No subscription, no accounts required for your own server, no algorithm deciding what you should watch next.

We believe media servers can be gentle — easy to run, easy to use, and easy on the eyes. You shouldn't need a computer science degree to host your own streaming. You also shouldn't need to stare at a cold, utilitarian dashboard while you do it.

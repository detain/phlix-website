Download

Self-host Phlix in minutes. PHP 8.3+ and you're ready to float.

Server Requirements

  • PHP 8.3 or higher
  • Workerman 5.x (installed via Composer)
  • FFmpeg for transcoding
composer require workerman/phlix-server
php start.php start

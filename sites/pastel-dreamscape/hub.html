Phlix Hub

Reach your server from anywhere — without opening a single port.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. Instead of exposing your server directly to the internet, your server establishes an outbound connection to the Hub. When you open any Phlix client from outside your network, the Hub routes traffic through that already-open connection — no port forwarding, no UPnP, no dynamic DNS.

It's the reason you can be watching something on your Roku at a friend's house while your server sits quietly on your home network, unseen by the outside world.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.app is free for anyone to use. All traffic is end-to-end encrypted between your server and connected clients.

If you prefer to run your own relay, Phlix Hub is open-source. Deploy it on any VPS with a persistent connection to both your server and the internet. The configuration is identical — point your server at your self-hosted Hub URL instead.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every Phlix client — Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile — supports Hub mode. When you sign in with the same Hub account on multiple devices, they automatically find each other through the relay, regardless of where they are in the world. Your media library appears as one seamless thing, no matter which device you're using.

What makes it dreamy?

Somewhere above the everyday world, past the last row of houses and just beyond where the sky turns the softest shade of pink, there is a place made entirely of clouds and color. It smells faintly of cotton candy. Rainbow bridges arch between floating islands. Tiny glowing bubbles carry your favorite films from island to island.

This is Pastel Dreamscape — the belief that finding something magical to watch should itself feel magical. Phlix brings that soft, joyful world into every living room, every bedtime, every lazy afternoon.

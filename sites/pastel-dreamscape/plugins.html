The versioned plugin system with LifecycleInterface — extend your dreamscape in any direction.

How it works

A real contract, not just conventions.

LifecycleInterface

Every plugin implements LifecycleInterface — onInstall, onActivate, onDeactivate, onUninstall. The loader calls them at the right moment, every time.

Manifest schema

A versioned manifest tells Phlix what your plugin needs, what it provides, and which Phlix version it requires. No guessing, no mystery.

Drop and go

Drop your plugin folder into the plugins directory. The loader finds it on next restart. No config editing, no command-line flags.

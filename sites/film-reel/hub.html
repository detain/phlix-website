Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

The Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your Phlix clients to your servers behind NAT — without opening ports, without a static IP, without a third-party tunnel service.

When you enable Hub mode on a client, it registers with the Hub and waits. When you open the app from anywhere else, the Hub brokers the connection and routes the stream through its relay. No configuration changes on your router.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.io is free for personal use. It requires no setup — point your client at it and you're done.

For stricter privacy or multi-server setups, you can self-host the Hub. It's open source (phlix-hub) and runs on any PHP 8.3+ host with a persistent connection to the internet.

phlix-hub on GitHub Hub docs

Hub mode in clients

Hub mode is built into every Phlix client. When you add a server, toggle "Use Hub" — the client handles the registration and reconnection automatically.

  • Works with any server behind NAT
  • Automatic reconnection on network changes
  • End-to-end encrypted tunnel
  • Same SyncPlay and playback features

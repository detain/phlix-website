Phlix Hub

Your media server, reachable from anywhere — without opening ports or configuring DNS.

What the Hub does

The Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your server to the outside world. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no third-party tunnel service. Sign in once, and your devices find your server automatically.

Self-host or use the public Hub

Run your own Hub instance for full control, or use the public Hub at phlix-hub — no account required, no tracking. Either way, the protocol is the same and your traffic stays encrypted.

Hub mode in clients

Enable Hub mode in your Phlix client settings. It will automatically discover and connect to your server through the Hub — whether you're on the same LAN or halfway across the world.

Under the hood

The Hub uses a reverse-tunnel relay architecture. Your server initiates an outbound connection to the Hub and keeps it alive. Clients connect to the Hub, which proxies traffic through that tunnel. No inbound ports opened, no NAT traversal hacks needed.

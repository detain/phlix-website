Features

Eight core capabilities, built for the way you actually watch.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. The library scans your directories, extracts episode and movie identifiers, and pulls in rich metadata — posters, descriptions, cast info — from TMDB, TVDB, and Fanart.tv. Everything caches for 24 hours so you're not hammering external APIs on every refresh.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. No more "wait, what timestamp are you on?" conversations. SyncPlay uses the same NTP-style time synchronization that keeps your computer's clock accurate, applied to media playback. Everyone sees the same frame at the same time, whether they're on the same LAN or connected through the Phlix Hub.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Not every device can play every format. Your phone on LTE needs different bitrates than your 4K TV on gigabit ethernet. Phlix's QualitySelector automatically picks the right transcode profile based on the device's capabilities and your network conditions, then generates the appropriate HLS variant playlist on the fly.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Family viewing without the surprises. Each user gets their own profile with watch history and resume positions. Parental controls let you lock profiles with a PIN and filter content by rating — from G all the way up to NC-17. JWT tokens with refresh token rotation keep sessions secure without constant re-authentication.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Connect an antenna, scan for channels, and you're watching Live TV. The EPG (Electronic Program Guide) pulls data from your source and presents a clean grid of what's on now and coming up. Set recordings for specific shows or entire series, and the Recorder handles the rest — storing your DVR files alongside your regular media library.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. DLNA (Digital Living Network Alliance) is the protocol your TV, PlayStation, and Xbox already speak. Phlix implements ContentDirectory for browsing media and AvTransport for playback control, with SSDP discovery so devices on your network automatically find your server. No install required on the client side — your TV just sees a new source.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example. The plugin system isn't an afterthought — it's a first-class extension point with a versioned manifest contract. Implement LifecycleInterface to define install, enable, disable, and uninstall hooks. Write metadata providers, custom transcoders, or entirely new content sources. The manifest schema documents what's available; the loader handles discovery and lifecycle.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Your server sits behind your router's NAT? The Hub's reverse-tunnel relay connects outbound from your server to the Hub, creating a persistent path for inbound connections. Use the public Hub at hub.phlix.dev, or self-host the Hub on your own VPS — it's the same software. Sign in once, see all your servers, stream from anywhere.

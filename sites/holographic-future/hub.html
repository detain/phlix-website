Phlix Hub

Your server. Your network. Accessible from anywhere.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your home server to your remote clients — without opening ports on your router, without a third-party cloud service, and without a subscription.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

Public Hub

Sign in with your Phlix account. Zero configuration. Available for immediate use.

Self-Hosted

Deploy phlix-hub on your own infrastructure. Full control. No data leaves your servers.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. Enable it once, and your client discovers your server through the relay — whether you are on the same LAN or on the other side of the world.

  • Sign in once to the Hub
  • Select your server from the directory
  • Hub mode keeps you connected anywhere

Reach your library from anywhere.

Access from anywhere

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere. Reverse-tunnel relay handles the NAT traversal.

How Hub works

Phlix Hub is a relay service that connects your client app to your home server when you're away. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no configuring your router.

End-to-End Encrypted

All traffic through the relay is encrypted. The Hub relay never sees your media — just encrypted tunneled data.

Self-Hostable

Run your own Hub relay. Clone phlix-hub, point it at a VPS, and you're the operator. Or use the public Hub — no account required for basic use.

Low Latency

The relay handles signaling only — media streams direct from your server to the client when possible. The Hub relay isn't a proxy, it's a matchmaker.

Use cases

Traveling with your phone
Connect to your home library from anywhere in the world. The Hub relay finds your server, the stream comes from home.
Sharing with family
Give your parents access to your server through their own Hub account. They never need to know your home IP.
No port forwarding
Many users can't open ports on their home router. Hub works around that entirely. Your router stays locked down.

Get started with Hub

Hub comes pre-configured in the Phlix server. Enable it in your server settings, create a Hub account, and point your clients at your server URL.

What Phlix brings to the fire

Features that ignite your library

Every feature burns hot and clean — no fluff, no filler, just the sharpest tools in self-hosted media.

The core burns brightest

SyncPlay Time Sync

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples. Play, pause, seek — every device locked to the same frame. Movie night stays in sync whether you're across the room or across the country.

Auto-Scanning Library

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) title formats, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Drop a file, see it appear. No manual refresh needed.

Adaptive Transcoding

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Right quality for every device, every time.

Multi-User + Parental Controls

JWT auth with Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Your household, your rules.

Live TV + DVR

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder. Scheduled recordings, EPG guide that doesn't make you click through five menus. Cut the cord without cutting features.

DLNA Built In

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry. Your old Samsung TV, your neighbor's Sony, that vintage Windows Media Center — none of them need a new app.

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Reach your home server from anywhere — or self-host the relay. Your media follows you.

Plugin System

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the contract. Extend without forking.

Metadata Aggregation

TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO. Multi-source with a 24-hour cache. Fan art, posters, subtitles — your library deserves to look the part.

Open-source media, on your terms.

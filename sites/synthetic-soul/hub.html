Phlix Hub

Access your server from anywhere — no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no compromises.

What the Hub Does

A reverse-tunnel relay that puts you in control.

Reverse-Tunnel Relay

Your Phlix server at home establishes an outgoing connection to the Hub. When you want to access your library, the Hub routes the request through that tunnel. No inbound ports need to be opened on your router.

NAT Traversal

Most home connections are behind Carrier-Grade NAT or have firewall rules. The Hub's reverse tunnel bypasses all of that — your server reaches out, and the Hub keeps the connection path available for you.

Single Sign-On

Sign in once to the Hub, and you can reach any of your registered servers. The Hub maintains a directory of your servers, so you don't need to remember IP addresses or hostnames.

Service Discovery

Your servers register with the Hub and advertise their presence. Clients can browse available servers, select one, and connect — all through the Hub's relay infrastructure.

Your Choice of Hub

Run the public Hub or host your own.

Public Hub

Use the shared Hub operated by the Phlix team. Sign up, register your server, and go. No configuration, no server costs — just stream from anywhere.

The public Hub is free to use. Your server traffic is relayed, not stored. The Hub doesn't have access to your media or credentials.

Self-Hosted Hub

Run phlix-hub on your own VPS or home server. You control the relay infrastructure completely. Ideal for power users who want full ownership of their connection metadata.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every native client supports Hub connectivity.

The Roku, Windows, and Mobile clients all include Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub instead of directly to your server's IP. This means you can access your home library from anywhere, on any network.

  • Roku — Hub mode for remote access without VPN
  • Windows — Hub mode with media key support
  • Mobile — Hub mode with offline downloads to your device

DLNA devices on your local network work as always — they discover Phlix via SSDP and connect directly without needing the Hub.

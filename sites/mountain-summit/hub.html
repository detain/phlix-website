Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Access any of your servers from anywhere — without exposing your server to the internet.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay. When your server is behind a NAT or firewall, the Hub lets your clients reach it anyway — without you having to configure port forwarding, Dynamic DNS, or any other infrastructure.

Reverse-tunnel relay

Your server opens an outbound connection to the Hub. Clients connect to the Hub, which proxies traffic to your server through that tunnel. No inbound ports needed.

NAT traversal

Phone on LTE, Roku at a friend's house, laptop traveling — they all connect to the Hub and reach your server, even when your server is on a home connection with no public IP.

Cloud directory

The Hub also maintains a registry of your servers so you don't have to remember IP addresses or hostnames. One account, all your servers, wherever they live.

Self-host or use the public hub

The Hub is open-source (MPL-2.0) like the server. You can self-host it for full control, or use the public Hub operated by the project at no charge.

Public Hub

Zero configuration. Point your server at the public Hub address, create an account, and everything works. Run by the Phlix project, free to use.

Self-hosted

Deploy your own Hub instance. Full control over the relay infrastructure, user accounts, and connection logs. Ideal for paranoia-conscious deployments.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. Enable it, sign in once, and your client discovers all registered servers automatically — LAN or remote, it Just Works.

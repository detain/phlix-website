Phlix Hub

Reach your server from anywhere — no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay and cloud directory. When you enable Hub mode on your server, it establishes an outbound connection to the Hub. Clients connect to the Hub instead of your LAN — and the Hub bridges them to your server.

This means you can access your home server from your phone on a different network, from a friend's Roku, or from anywhere you travel — without touching your router settings.

Self-host or use the public hub

Public Hub

Sign up once, point your server at hub.phlix.dev, and you're connected. No infrastructure to manage.

Self-hosted

Run phlix-hub on your own VPS. You control the relay, the directory, and the connection keys.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client — Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile — supports Hub mode. Enable it in settings, sign in once, and your client stays connected to your server no matter where you are.

The client works exactly the same way whether it's on your LAN or connected via the Hub — browse library, start playback, control SyncPlay. The tunnel is invisible to the user.

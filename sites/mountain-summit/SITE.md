# Mountain Summit — Brand Kit Site

## Concept & Vision

The Mountain Summit theme draws from the stark beauty of the world's highest peaks — Everest base camp, thin air, snow-capped granite, prayer flags, altitude climbing, and cloud layers below. The visual language is cool, vast, and serene, evoking the feeling of standing above the world. Phlix sits at the summit of self-hosted media: powerful, accessible, above the noise.

## Aesthetic Direction

Cold mountain palette with warm gold accents. Deep slate blues and grays dominate, grounded by a dark charcoal for contrast, with a golden amber accent that recalls sunlight on rock and prayer flags at altitude. The overall feel is serene, cool, and capable.

- **Display / Headings:** Libre Baskerville — a refined serif with mountain gravitas
- **Body / UI:** Source Sans Pro — clean, readable, modern
- **Monospace:** Fira Code — for code blocks and technical content

## Motion Philosophy

Subtle, purposeful motion. Scroll reveals on feature cards and client cards. Toggle animations on FAQ items. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Hero gradient shifts on a slow loop to suggest the shifting light on a mountain face.

## Brand Identity Seed

**Brand:** Moroccan Bazaar — the sensory richness of a Fes and Marrakech medina at dusk.
Hammered copper lanterns, hand-painted zellige tile mosaics, eight-pointed Islamic
geometric star patterns, warm terracotta and copper jewel tones against deep
midnight-indigo shadows. North African craftsmanship meets ancient Islamic geometric tradition.

**Art direction:** Warm lantern-lit riad interior at golden hour — honeyed light
through carved mashrabiya screens onto hand-set tile floors. Intimate, layered,
sensory. Warm and saturated in lights (terracotta, saffron, copper); deep in shadows
(midnight indigo, warm near-black). No cold blue light, no stark modernism, no empty
white space.

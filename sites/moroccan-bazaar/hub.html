Reach from Afar

Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

The Hub solves the NAT traversal problem for self-hosted media. Your Phlix server sits behind your home router. Without the Hub, nothing outside your LAN can reach it. The Hub's reverse-tunnel relay creates an outbound connection from your server to the Hub, keeping a persistent path open — so your Roku at a friend's house, or your phone on mobile data, can reach your library the same way it reaches anything on the internet.

It's a directory and a relay. Your server announces itself to the Hub; your clients look up the server by name. The relay passes streaming traffic — it doesn't store it.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.tv is free for personal use. If you want full control — or a hub for your household or organisation — you can self-host phlix-hub on any VPS. Self-hosting gives you your own directory, your own relay, and your own user management.

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client — Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and Mobile — has a Hub mode setting. When enabled, the client looks up your server through the Hub rather than scanning the LAN. This means the same client works whether you're at home or across the world.

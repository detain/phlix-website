Phlix Hub

One sign-in. Every server. Everywhere. The lantern that lights every passage in the bazaar.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your Phlix server sits behind a NAT or firewall, the Hub gives your clients a way to find it without opening any ports on your router.

Sign in once, list your servers, and every registered client can reach any of them — whether you're at home on the LAN or at a friend's house across the country.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at github.com/detain/phlix-hub is free to use. If you prefer to run your own, the Hub server is open-source and self-hostable — deploy it on any VPS with a public IP.

Either way, the interface your clients see is identical. The relay handles NAT traversal; the directory maps accounts to servers.

Hub mode in clients

The Roku, Windows, and Mobile clients all support Hub mode. Enable it in the client's settings, sign in to your Hub account, and your registered servers appear automatically. No configuration, no port forwarding.

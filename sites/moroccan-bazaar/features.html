The Masterworks

Every feature, built with intention

Eight pillars of a media server designed to stay out of your way while it stays in sync with everything you own.

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. The library reads your naming conventions and places every film and episode where you would have put it by hand — without you lifting anything.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. No leader, no guessing. Movie night stays in sync whether you're in the same room or across the country.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. When direct play isn't available, the server picks the right bitrate for your device and your network — automatically.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Everyone in the household gets their own view of the library, and young viewers only see what they should.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Pick a show, set it once, and the server handles the rest — including keeping track of season passes.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Phlix announces itself on your network and the TV finds it the same way it finds any other media renderer — no configuration, no install.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. The contract is versioned — a plugin you write today will still work when Phlix ships new releases. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal so your server behind a home router is reachable from anywhere. Self-host the hub, or use the public one — either way, your library follows you.

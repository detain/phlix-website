The Masterworks

Every feature, built with intention

Eight pillars of a media server designed to stay out of your way while it stays in sync with everything you own.

Library that organizes itself

Add a file, see it appear. Phlix watches your folders for changes, recognizes naming like S01E02 and (2020), and fills in the titles, descriptions, and artwork for you — no renaming rituals, no manual entry. The library reads your naming conventions and places every film and episode where you would have put it by hand — without you lifting anything.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Watch together, perfectly in step. Phlix keeps every device's playback clock tightly synced, so play, pause, and seek move together for everyone in the room — or an ocean away. No leader, no guessing. Movie night stays in sync whether you're in the same room or across the country.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

Every device gets the right quality on its own — dedicated profiles for phones, web, and 4K TVs. When a file won't play directly, Phlix transcodes it on the fly with FFmpeg (H.264 defaults to CRF 23, the sweet spot between quality and size) and delivers it as adaptive HLS that rides out network hiccups. If your server has a compatible Intel or AMD GPU, hardware profiles run the heavy work on it instead of the CPU. When direct play isn't available, the server picks the right bitrate for your device and your network — automatically.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

One account, up to 5 personal profiles by default, each lockable with a 4- or 6-digit PIN. Passwords are protected with Argon2ID — today's strongest widely recommended hashing — and sign-ins stay secure with tokens that refresh themselves. Rating filters from G to NC-17 keep kids' profiles to kids' content. Bring your existing identity too: OIDC, LDAP, and passkeys all work, so Phlix fits the sign-in you already run. Everyone in the household gets their own view of the library, and young viewers only see what they should.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

Watch, pause, and record live TV. Schedule a recording once and it happens on time; the on-screen guide opens straight to what's on now — no clicking through menus. Pick a show, set it once, and the server handles the rest — including keeping track of season passes.

DLNA for the devices you already own

Phlix speaks DLNA — the streaming standard already built into most older smart TVs, game consoles, and receivers — so the devices you own can play your library with no app to install. Phlix announces itself on your network and the TV finds it the same way it finds any other media renderer — no configuration, no install.

Plugin system with a real contract

A versioned plugin contract. Install it at Settings → Plugins, and the server loads it once enabled. The contract is versioned — a plugin you write today will still work when Phlix ships new releases. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. A relay you run reaches your home server without opening ports so your server behind a home router is reachable from anywhere. Self-host the hub, or run your own Hub — either way, your library follows you.

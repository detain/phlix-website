Phlix Hub

Your server, reachable anywhere. No port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no third-party tunnel service required.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your server registers with the Hub, it opens an outbound connection that stays open. Your clients connect to the same Hub — the relay moves data between them without your server ever accepting inbound connections.

This means your server can live behind a home NAT, a CG-NAT, or any firewall — as long as it can make an outbound connection, the Hub connects it to your devices.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free for anyone to use. If you prefer to run your own, Phlix Hub is open source: clone it, deploy it on any VPS, and point your clients at your instance. Your credentials and traffic never touch a third-party server.

phlix-hub on GitHub

Hub mode in clients

Every Phlix client supports Hub mode. Enable it, sign in once, and your client discovers all the servers registered to your account — whether they are in the next room or on another continent. No configuration, no manual IP addresses.

  • Roku — Hub mode built into the channel
  • Samsung Tizen — Hub mode in settings
  • Windows desktop — Hub mode toggle in the tray menu
  • Mobile (iOS + Android) — Hub mode in account settings
  • Any DLNA client — discovery via SSDP, no Hub needed

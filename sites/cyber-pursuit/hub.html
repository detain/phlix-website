Reach Anywhere

No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. No third-party relay. Just you and your server.

Connection Protocol

How the tunnel works.

01

Your server calls home

Phlix Hub client running on your server maintains an outbound encrypted connection to the Hub relay. No inbound ports needed.

02

Hub assigns a tunnel address

Your server gets a unique tunnel address like tunnel.phlix.io/abc123. Share this with your mobile app or remote browser.

03

Traffic routes through the tunnel

All traffic is encrypted end-to-end. The Hub relay sees only encrypted packets — it can't read your media or metadata.

Security Model

The relay never sees your data.

encryption.enabled active

End-to-End Encryption

Every byte is encrypted before it leaves your server. The relay is a blind forwarder — it can't decrypt anything.

no_third_party_data enforced

Zero Data Retention

The Hub relay doesn't log media streams, file names, or metadata. Traffic is forwarded and discarded.

auth.token required

Your Credentials Stay Yours

Authenticate with your server's own user database. The Hub never stores passwords — just tunnel state.

Quick Start

Enable Hub in two steps.

bash 
# 1. Set your Hub token in config.php
echo "PHLIX_HUB_TOKEN=your_token_here" >> config.php

# 2. Start the Hub client
./bin/phlix-server hub connect

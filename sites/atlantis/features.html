The Depths

Eight pillars of the kingdom

Every feature crafted to bring order to your media, no matter how deep your library goes.

Feature details

Library that organizes itself

Add a file, see it appear. Phlix watches your folders for changes, recognizes naming like S01E02 and (2020), and fills in the titles, descriptions, and artwork for you — no renaming rituals, no manual entry. No manual refreshing, no waiting for scans to complete. The library wakes up with your files.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Watch together, perfectly in step. Phlix keeps every device's playback clock tightly synced, so play, pause, and seek move together for everyone in the room — or an ocean away. Whether you're in the same room or across the country, movie night stays in sync.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

Every device gets the right quality on its own — dedicated profiles for phones, web, and 4K TVs. When a file won't play directly, Phlix transcodes it on the fly with FFmpeg (H.264 defaults to CRF 23, the sweet spot between quality and size) and delivers it as adaptive HLS that rides out network hiccups. If your server has a compatible Intel or AMD GPU, hardware profiles run the heavy work on it instead of the CPU. Your Chromecast doesn't support HEVC? Phlix converts it on the fly to something it can play.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

One account, up to 5 personal profiles, each lockable with a 4- or 6-digit PIN. Passwords are protected with Argon2ID — today's strongest widely recommended hashing — and sign-ins stay secure with tokens that refresh themselves. Rating filters from G to NC-17 keep kids' profiles to kids' content. Bring your existing identity too: OIDC, LDAP, and passkeys all work, so Phlix fits the sign-in you already run. Everyone gets their own space, and the kids can only see what you want them to see.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

Watch, pause, and record live TV. Schedule a recording once and it happens on time; the on-screen guide opens straight to what's on now — no clicking through menus. Set it once, forget about it, come home to your show already recorded.

DLNA for the devices you already own

Phlix speaks DLNA — the streaming standard already built into most older smart TVs, game consoles, and receivers — so the devices you own can play your library with no app to install. Phlix speaks their language natively. Discovery via SSDP, play on anything that speaks DLNA.

Plugin system with a real contract

A versioned plugin contract. Install it at Settings → Plugins, and the server loads it once enabled. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. Versioned contract means your plugins won't break across upgrades.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. A relay you run reaches your home server without opening ports. Fully self-hostable. Your server behind a firewall? The Hub punches through so you can watch anywhere.

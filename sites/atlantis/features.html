The Depths

Eight pillars of the kingdom

Every feature crafted to bring order to your media, no matter how deep your library goes.

Feature details

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. No manual refreshing, no waiting for scans to complete. The library wakes up with your files.

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Whether you're in the same room or across the country, movie night stays in sync.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Your Chromecast doesn't support HEVC? Phlix converts it on the fly to something it can play.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Everyone gets their own space, and the kids can only see what you want them to see.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Set it once, forget about it, come home to your show already recorded.

DLNA for the devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Phlix speaks their language natively. Discovery via SSDP, play on anything that speaks DLNA.

Plugin system with a real contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter. Versioned contract means your plugins won't break across upgrades.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub. Your server behind a firewall? The Hub punches through so you can watch anywhere.

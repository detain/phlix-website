# Directors Chair — Brand Kit Site

## Concept & Vision

The Directors Chair theme evokes the feeling of being behind the scenes at a film set. Think clapperboards, spotlights, and the creative power of filmmakers. The palette draws from director's chairs (rich dark fabric with gold accents) and the iconic clapperboard (black and white with gold markers). The experience should feel cinematic, professional, and slightly theatrical — like you're running the show.

## Aesthetic Direction

**Reference:** Film production studio meets modern streaming interface. Dark, moody backgrounds with warm spotlight accents. The clapperboard motif appears in icons and decorative elements. Typography is bold and uppercase, evoking title cards and film credits.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Primary (background) | Dark Charcoal | `#1C1C1C` |
| Accent | Clapperboard Gold | `#FFD93D` |
| Text/Light | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Surface | Dark Gray | `#2D3436` |
| Highlight | Director's Chair Red | `#E74C3C` |

## Typography

- **Display/Headings:** Impact or system sans-serif, uppercase, wide letter-spacing — cinematic title card feel
- **Body:** Source Sans Pro or system sans-serif — clean, readable
- **Code/Mono:** Fira Code or monospace

## Spatial System

- Spacing scale: 0.25rem base unit (4px)
- Container max-width: 1400px
- Section padding: 4rem vertical (desktop), 2.5rem (mobile)
- Card gaps: 1.5rem

## Motion Philosophy

- Subtle spotlight/glow effects on accent colors
- Fade-in-up animations for scroll reveals
- Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Quick, snappy transitions (150-250ms)

## Visual Assets

- **Logo:** Clapperboard + film reel motif combined with PHIX wordmark
- **Icons:** Single-color stroke-based inline SVGs
- **Decorative:** Spotlight gradients, subtle film grain texture
- **OG Image:** Dark background with clapperboard motif and logo

## Component Notes

- Feature cards: Dark surface with gold accent border on hover
- Buttons: Uppercase, high contrast — gold primary, outlined secondary
- 404 page: "Scene Not Found" with "Cut!" headline — film-themed

Phlix Hub

Reach any of your servers from anywhere.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that lets you connect to your home server from anywhere in the world — without opening ports or configuring DDNS.

When you're on your home network, clients connect directly. When you're away, the Hub brokers the connection through a secure tunnel. You sign in once, and the Hub routes you to the right server.

Key benefits:

  • No port forwarding required
  • Works behind carrier-grade NAT
  • Self-hostable or use the public hub
  • Secure reverse-tunnel technology

Self-host or use the public hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free to use. If you prefer to run your own infrastructure, Phlix Hub is open-source and self-hostable.

phlix-hub 
github.com/detain/phlix-hub

Self-hosting gives you:

  • Full control over your relay infrastructure
  • No dependency on third-party services
  • Custom domain support

Hub mode in clients

All native Phlix clients support Hub mode — when enabled, they automatically use the Hub to reach your server when you're away from home.

Clients that support Hub mode:

  • Roku
  • Windows (Electron)
  • Mobile (iOS + Android)

Canopy Relay

Sign in once. Reach any of your servers from anywhere — without punching holes in your firewall.

How It Works

Phlix Hub runs a reverse-tunnel relay. Your server opens an outbound connection to the Hub and keeps it alive. When you open a client, the Hub routes traffic through that connection — no port forwarding, no NAT traversal, no third-party tunnel service.

1. Your server calls home

Phlix Server opens a persistent WebSocket to the Hub on startup. This connection is outbound only — no inbound ports needed.

2. You sign in once

Authenticate with the Hub using your Phlix account. The Hub knows which servers you've registered and are authorized to access.

3. The relay connects you

When your client requests your server, the Hub multiplexes the connection through the already-open tunnel. Latency is minimal — it's a proxy, not a relay buffer.

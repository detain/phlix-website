The Evidence

Eight Tools. One Mission.

Every feature built for one purpose: making your media fight for your attention.

SyncPlay

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together.

Library Management

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear.

Transcoding

Adaptive HLS streaming, FFmpeg transcoding, per-device quality profiles. Your files, optimized for every screen.

User Profiles

Up to 5 user profiles with individual watch states, ratings, and preferences. Everyone fights for their own progress.

Live TV + DVR

Live TV with DVR and EPG guide integration. Record the fights, watch on your schedule.

DLNA / Casting

Broadcast to any DLNA device on your network. No native client needed — just tap and stream.

Phlix Hub

Access your library from anywhere. The hub follows you — stadium, airport, hotel, anywhere the fight continues.

Plugin System

Versioned manifest contract for extending functionality. Write your own plugins or install community additions.

Your library. Your arena. Your fight.

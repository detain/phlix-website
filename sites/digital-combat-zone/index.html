The Briefing

In the arena, there is no second place.

Media doesn't just stream — it fights for screen time. Phlix was built for the viewer who cranks the volume, leans forward, and demands their content fight back. Every frame is a knockout. Every transition is an uppercut.

  • 01 100% self-hostable — your library never leaves your hardware unless you say so
  • 02 Native clients on Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile, plus any DLNA device
  • 03 Real-time SyncPlay with NTP-style time sync so movie night actually stays in sync
  • 04 Multi-source metadata: TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO with 24-hour cache
  • 05 Adaptive HLS streaming, FFmpeg transcoding, per-device quality profiles
  • 06 Live TV with DVR + EPG guide integration
  • 07 Plugin system with a versioned manifest contract
Knockout Moves

Two finishes that end the debate.

FIGHT NO. 01 / SYNCPLAY

SyncPlay across the room or across the country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together.

Every device locked to the same frame — no lag, no drift, pure synchronization.

FIGHT NO. 02 / LIBRARY

Library that organizes itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear.

Your archive, organized by the machine, searchable from anywhere, always live.

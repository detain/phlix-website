Chapter 01

The Arena

In the arena, there is no second place. Media doesn't just stream — it fights for screen time. Phlix was built for the viewer who cranks the volume, leans forward, and demands their content fight back.

Every frame is a knockout. Every transition is an uppercut. Every hover state explodes off the screen.

Chapter 02

The Philosophy

Phlix is self-hosted. Your library never leaves your hardware unless you say so. No monthly fees. No cloud dependency. No corporate middleman between you and your content.

Built on PHP 8.3+ with a focus on performance, flexibility, and staying true to the open source ethos. Modify a Phlix file and it stays open.

Chapter 03

The Stack

PHP for the server. Native apps for Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and mobile. FFmpeg for transcoding. SyncPlay for synchronized viewing. Live TV and DVR for the broadcast sports addicts.

Everything connected through a hub that follows you anywhere — stadium, airport, hotel, anywhere the fight continues.

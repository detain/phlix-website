Reach your garden from anywhere

The Commons (Hub)

Sign in once. Your servers — wherever they are — appear. The reverse-tunnel relay handles the NAT so you don't have to.

How the Commons works

1. Register your server

Point your server at the Hub — self-hosted or public — and it registers its address. No port forwarding, no static IP required.

2. Sign in once

Authenticate with the Hub using your server credentials. The Hub maintains a directory of your registered servers.

3. Reach any of your servers

Pick a server from your Hub directory and you're connected — whether it's behind a home router, at a friend's house, or in a data centre across the country.

4. Invite friends and family

Grant access to your server through the Hub — no VPN, no teamviewer, no exposing your LAN. Revoke any time.

Two ways to tend the Commons

Public Hub

Zero-configuration — just point your server at the public Hub relay. No server of your own required for the Hub.

View on GitHub

Self-hosted Hub

Run your own relay. Complete control over the directory and tunnel infrastructure. Phlix Hub is MPL-2.0 like the server.

Garden Questions

Do I need to expose my server to the internet?
No. Run Phlix on your LAN and use the Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay to reach it from your phone or Roku at a friend's house. You can self-host the hub, or use the public one.
What's the license on the Hub?
Phlix Server and the Hub are MPL-2.0 — open source, and you can build on them commercially. If you modify a Phlix file, that file stays open; anything you add alongside it is yours.

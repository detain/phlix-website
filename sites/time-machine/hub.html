Phlix Hub

One sign-in to reach any of your servers — at home or across the world.

What the Hub does

The Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that handles NAT traversal for you. When your Phlix server can't be reached directly (because it's behind a router without port forwarding), the Hub bridges the connection so your clients can still reach it.

Your Server ←→ Hub ←→ Your Clients

Sign in once. The Hub keeps track of your servers and routes you to the right one.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.dev is free to use. If you want full control, you can self-host the Hub — it's open source (phlix-hub on GitHub) and runs on any PHP 8.3+ host.

Hub mode in clients

All native clients support Hub mode — just sign in with your Hub credentials and your servers appear automatically. No firewall changes, no port forwarding.

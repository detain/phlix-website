Phlix Hub

Reach your server from anywhere — no port forwarding required.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. When your server is behind a NAT or firewall, the Hub connects your clients to it without requiring you to open ports or configure DDNS.

Sign in once to the Hub, add your server, and access it from any client — anywhere in the world.

Self-host or use the public hub

Free to use, maintained by the Phlix team. Sign up, add your server, connect.

Hub mode in clients

All official Phlix clients support Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub instead of directly to your server's IP address.

This means you can stream from your home server while at a friend's house, or access your parents' server from your own devices — without touching their router settings.

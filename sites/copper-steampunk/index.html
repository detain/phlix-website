The Instruments

Every reel catalogued in one mechanical ledger

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear.

Time-locked across the room or across the globe

NTP-weighted precision keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together.

Transcoding that picks the right quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists.

Multi-user, multi-profile, parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17.

Phlix Hub — reach any of your servers from anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT. Self-hostable, or use the public hub.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus.

What sets this apparatus apart?

In the autumn of 1887, Professor Aldous Pemberton-Hale sealed himself inside his copper-clad workshop beneath the gas lamps of Whitechapel and did not emerge until spring — having assembled, from cog and conduit, pipe and parchment, the first automated moving-picture archive the world had never seen. His machine hummed and hissed, sorting reels by steam-driven index arms, projecting them through porthole lenses onto mahogany screens.

Phlix carries that same obsessive craftsmanship into the modern home: a media server built with the precision of a master watchmaker, the ambition of an airship engineer, and the soul of an eternal explorer.

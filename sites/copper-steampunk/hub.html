Phlix Hub

Your server, your network, your rules — yet reachable from anywhere. The Hub is the reverse-tunnel relay that makes NAT traversal disappear.

What the Hub Does

Phlix Hub is a lightweight relay service. When your Phlix server is behind a NAT or firewall — which it always is on a home network — incoming connections from your clients have nowhere to land. The Hub fixes this: it acts as a persistent outbound relay from your server, maintaining a reverse tunnel that clients can use to reach it.

The connection is initiated by your server — nothing needs to be opened on your router. Clients connect to the Hub, the Hub forwards to your server, and the archive plays as if you were on the same LAN.

Two Ways to Run

Public Hub

Use the hosted relay at hub.phlix.io. Sign in once with your Phlix account, register your server, and all your clients activate Hub mode automatically. No configuration required.

View Hub source

Self-Hosted

Deploy phlix-hub on your own VPS or always-on machine. All relay traffic stays within infrastructure you control. Configuration via environment variables; Docker image available.

View Hub source

Hub Mode in Clients

When a client cannot locate your server on the local network — the default on mobile networks, or when visiting a friend's house — it falls back to Hub mode automatically. The transition is smooth: the same interface, the same catalogue, the same playback experience.

Windows and mobile clients have a persistent Hub toggle in settings. Roku and Samsung Tizen clients use Hub mode automatically when LAN discovery fails.

The relay is always open. Your library awaits.

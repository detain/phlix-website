The Relay Station

Reach your workshop from anywhere — without opening a port.

Reverse-tunnel relay

Your server stays behind your firewall. The Hub reaches out and holds a tunnel open. When you connect from a client, the traffic flows through that tunnel — no port forwarding, no dynamic DNS, no exposing your network.

Self-host or use the public hub

Deploy your own relay at a VPS or a always-on machine you control. Or connect to the public hub for zero-configuration access — your server enrolls, your clients connect, it just works.

Hub mode in the clients

Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile — every client speaks Hub. One enrollment per server, used everywhere. When you're on a different network, the client reaches your relay instead of your LAN address.

Enroll your engine in the relay

Hub mode is built in. One setting, zero configuration.

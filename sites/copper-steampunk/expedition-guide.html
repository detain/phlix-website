Expedition into Your Collection

A guided walkthrough for your first expedition into the archive of moving pictures.

01

Commission the engine

One line on a fresh Ubuntu or Debian host. PHP 8.3+, MySQL, ffmpeg — the workshop comes fully operational. Default ports: HTTP on :8096 behind HAProxy on :80/:443, DLNA discovery on 1900/udp. See the full commission order →

02

Point it at your library

Add a folder. Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json from TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO. Add a file, see it appear.

03

Pick your gallery wall

Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, Mobile (iOS + Android beta), or any DLNA device. Five platforms, zero configuration beyond installing the client. Browse the engineering gallery →

04

Configure profiles and access

Up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. JWT auth with Argon2ID hashing. Run on your LAN, or enroll in the Hub relay for remote access without opening a port.

05

Explore the instruments

SyncPlay keeps everyone on the same frame across the room or across the country. Live TV with DVR and an EPG that doesn't make you click through menus. Transcoding that picks the right quality for every device. Read the full instrument manual →

06

Extend the workshop

Write a plugin. Implement LifecycleInterface, ship a manifest, drop it in the plugins directory. phlix-plugin-example →

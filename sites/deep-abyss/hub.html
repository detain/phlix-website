Phlix Hub

Access your server from anywhere — without opening ports.

What the Hub does

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that connects your devices to your server, even when your server is behind a NAT or firewall. No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS. Just sign in and stream.

Reverse-tunnel relay

Your server opens an outbound tunnel to the Hub. Devices connect through the Hub to reach your server.

NAT traversal

No router configuration needed. The tunnel handles firewall and NAT traversal automatically.

Multi-server support

Sign in once and access all your servers. Perfect for home and vacation setups.

Self-host or use the public Hub

The Hub is available in two modes:

  • Public Hub — Use the hosted service at phlix-hub.dev. No setup required.
  • Self-hosted — Run your own Hub instance for full control. The phlix-hub repo is open source.

Hub mode in clients

The Roku, Windows, and Mobile clients support Hub mode. Connect to your Hub account and your servers appear automatically — no IP addresses or port numbers needed.

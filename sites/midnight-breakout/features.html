Features

Everything you need to run a media library that actually works — and stays under your control.

Library that Organizes Itself

Folder-watcher hashes mtimes, scanner parses S01E02 / (2020) titles, ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json. Add a file, see it appear. No manual metadata entry, no clicking through 20 dialogs. Drop your media in the right folder and Phlix handles the rest.

SyncPlay Across the Room or Across the Country

Weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together. Movie night stays in sync whether you're in the same room or across the country.

Transcoding That Picks the Right Quality

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. Phlix works out the right bitrate for your device and your network — you don't have to.

Multi-User, Multi-Profile, Parental Controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, rating filter from G to NC-17. Everyone in the house gets their own library view without stepping on each other's watch history.

Live TV with DVR + EPG

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and a guide that doesn't make you click through menus. Set it once, miss nothing. Full EPG integration with program info and poster art.

DLNA for the Devices You Already Own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Any DLNA-certified device on your network sees your Phlix library immediately, without configuration.

Plugin System with a Real Contract

LifecycleInterface + manifest schema. Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. Metadata providers, transcode pipelines, or anything you can code — if you can write PHP, you can extend Phlix. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

Phlix Hub — Reach Any of Your Servers from Anywhere

Sign in once. Reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT so you can access your server from your phone, your Roku at a friend's house, or any device anywhere in the world. Self-hostable, or use the public hub — no configuration required.

Phlix Hub

The reverse-tunnel relay that breaks the NAT barrier — access your server from anywhere without exposing a port.

What the Hub Does

Sign in once. The Hub's reverse-tunnel relay handles NAT traversal so you can access your server from your phone, your Roku at a friend's house, or any device anywhere in the world. Your server stays behind your firewall — the Hub reaches out to it, not the other way around.

No port forwarding. No dynamic DNS configuration. No third-party tunnel service to trust with your traffic. The Hub is a simple relay: it passes encrypted data between your server and your client, and it never stores your media or your credentials.

Self-Host or Use the Public Hub

You can run your own phlix-hub instance, or use the public relay — no configuration required on either side. The public hub is hosted by the Phlix community and is available to all users. If you need more control, deploy your own hub on any server with a public IP.

Hub Mode in Clients

Every official client supports Hub mode. When enabled, the client connects through the Hub relay instead of directly to your LAN server. Toggle it on when you're away from home, off when you're on the same network — Phlix clients handle the switch automatically.

This means one app works everywhere: at home on your local network at full LAN speed, and abroad through the relay when you need remote access.

Hub

Your servers, accessible from anywhere.

What is Phlix Hub?

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay that lets you reach your home servers without configuring NAT, port forwarding, or VPNs. Sign in once, and your devices can find your servers wherever you are.

How it works

  • Sign in — Create a Hub account or self-host your own Hub instance
  • Register your server — Your Phlix server connects to Hub and establishes a reverse tunnel
  • Access anywhere — Use any Phlix client with Hub mode enabled to connect to your server from anywhere

Self-host or use the public Hub

The public Hub at hub.phlix.online is free for personal use. If you need privacy or control, you can self-host the Hub — it's open-source and runs anywhere PHP does.

git clone https://github.com/detain/phlix-hub.git
cd phlix-hub
composer install
php start.php server:start

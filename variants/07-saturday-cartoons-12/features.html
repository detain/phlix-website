Features

Everything you need to stream your media, your way.

Auto-Updating Library

Folder-watcher monitors your media directories and hashes mtimes to detect changes. The scanner parses S01E02 patterns and (2020) release years. ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json from TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO files with a 24-hour cache.

SyncPlay — Together, Anywhere

Real-time SyncPlay with weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together whether they're in the same room or across the country.

Adaptive Transcoding

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. FFmpeg transcoding with per-device quality profiles.

Multi-User & Parental Controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, and rating filter from G to NC-17.

Live TV with DVR

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder give you scheduled recordings and an EPG guide that doesn't make you click through endless menus.

DLNA Support

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and a DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Push content to any DLNA-certified device on your network.

Plugins

Extend Phlix with community-built plugins.

Official Plugins

  • OpenSubtitles — Download subtitles from OpenSubtitles.org
    Automatic subtitle matching for movies and TV shows
  • Fanart.tv — Enhanced artwork from Fanart.tv
    Movie backgrounds, season thumbs, artist art
  • Trakt.tv — Sync watch history and ratings
    Track what you've watched across all devices
  • Sonarr / Radarr — Automatic media acquisition
    Integrate with your existing automation stack

Build Your Own

Phlix has a versioned plugin manifest contract. Write plugins in PHP and distribute them through the plugin hub.

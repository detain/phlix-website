Hub

Your library, profiles, and watch history — synced everywhere.

Unified Profiles

Your watch history, bookmarks, and preferences follow you from the living room Roku to the bedroom tablet to the office web browser. Each profile has its own progress, ratings, and PIN-protected parental controls.

Continue Watching

Start a movie on your TV, pause it, and pick up exactly where you left off on your phone during the commute. Phlix Hub keeps playback position synced in real time.

Cross-Device Resume

No more manually scrubbing to find where you left off. Phlix Hub persists the exact timestamp and even predicts the best resume point based on intro/outro detection.

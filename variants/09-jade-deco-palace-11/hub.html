Phlix Hub

Access your server from anywhere, even behind NAT.

How it works

When your Phlix server starts, it establishes an outbound WebSocket to the Hub relay. The Hub brokers the connection to your client — no inbound ports required on your router.

You sign in once, and your server appears in the Hub dashboard. From there you can:

  • Browse and stream your library on any device
  • Manage recordings and scheduled tasks
  • Invite family members and assign profiles
  • Monitor server health and storage

Self-host or use the public relay

The public Hub at hub.phlix.app is free for personal use. If you want full control or commercial deployment, the Hub software is open source and runs on any PHP 8.3+ host with a persistent WebSocket connection.

Public relay

No configuration. Your server connects to hub.phlix.app on first launch. You get an account at the Hub, your server appears, and you're done.

Self-hosted

Deploy phlix-hub on a VPS or any always-on host. Point your servers at your own relay. Fully under your control, no data leaves your infrastructure.

Security

All traffic through the Hub is TLS-encrypted. The relay sees only encrypted WebSocket frames — it cannot read or modify your media streams or API calls. Authentication tokens are short-lived JWTs with Argon2ID-hashed refresh tokens stored server-side.

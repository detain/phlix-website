About Phlix

Your media. Your server. Your rules.

Philosophy

Self-Hosting First

Phlix exists because your media library is yours. We believe in self-hosting by default — your files stay on your hardware, your metadata stays in your database, and your streaming history never leaves your server.

We don't build cloud services that harvest your viewing habits. There's no mandatory account, no telemetry, no "pro" tier that gates basic playback. The Hub exists for convenience, not surveillance.

Phlix is built on PHP because it runs everywhere. On that old Intel NUC gathering dust. On the Raspberry Pi 5 in your closet. On the $10/month VPS you already have running something else. You shouldn't need a dedicated build server to host your own media.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my viewing data private?

Yes. Phlix stores all data locally on your server. No analytics, no telemetry, no accounts required. Your server only connects to external services for metadata (TMDB, TVDB) and optional Hub relay — both of which you can disable.

How hard is it to set up?

Phlix installs via a single composer command and runs a first-run wizard to configure your media paths, database, and admin account. If you can install WordPress, you can install Phlix. Advanced users can configure everything via YAML or environment variables.

How does SyncPlay work?

Phlix SyncPlay implements weighted-mean NTP offset calculation over 5 samples to keep every client frame-locked to the same timeline. When someone pauses, seeks, orbuffer, everyone in the session adjusts. Works over LAN and through the Hub relay.

What devices are supported?

Native apps for Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows (x64), iOS, and Android. Also works with any DLNA/UPnP client on your network — smart TVs, game consoles, Kodi, and more. Web playback via any modern browser.

What does it cost?

Phlix is 100% free and open-source under the BSD-3 license. You only pay for hosting — any PHP 8.2+ server with MySQL 8.0+ will do. The Hub relay is free to use, or self-host your own.

Features

Everything you need to stream your media, your way.

Library Management

Auto-organizing library

Folder-watcher monitors your media directories, hashes mtimes to detect changes, and the scanner parses S01E02 and (2020) title patterns. ItemRepository hydrates metadata from TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO files with a 24-hour cache.

Adaptive HLS streaming

QualitySelector applies per-device quality profiles (mobile-low, mobile-high, web, tv-4k). FFmpeg transcoding uses CRF 23/28 for libx264/libx265, generating HLS master and variant playlists.

SyncPlay

Real-time sync across devices

Weighted-mean NTP offset calculation over 5 samples keeps every device locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek — everyone moves together whether they're in the same room or across the country.

Live TV & DVR

ChannelManager + GuideManager + Recorder

Schedule recordings from the EPG guide. DVR integration lets you record live TV and watch it later, all without clicking through endless menus.

Security & Profiles

Multi-user with parental controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens and Argon2ID password hashing. Up to 5 profiles per user with 4- or 6-digit PINs and rating filters from G to NC-17.

DLNA Support

Works with devices you already own

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and DeviceRegistry mean your old smart TV doesn't need a new app. Just open a DLNA browser and start streaming.

Plugin System

LifecycleInterface + manifest contract

Drop a plugin in, the loader picks it up. Versioned manifest schema ensures compatibility. See phlix-plugin-example for the smallest working starter.

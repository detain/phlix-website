Phlix Hub

Access your servers from anywhere.

What is the Hub?

Phlix Hub is a cloud directory and reverse-tunnel relay. Sign in once, add your servers, and access them from anywhere — no static IP or port forwarding required.

How it works

Cloud directory
Your Phlix Hub account holds the list of your servers — hostname, port, and a short-lived auth token. No media leaves your network.
Reverse tunnel
Each server opens an outbound WebSocket to hub.phlix.dev. When you connect, the hub routes traffic through those tunnels. No inbound ports needed.
Self-hostable
The Hub is also open source. Run your own relay if you want full control or have specific compliance requirements.
SyncPlay over Hub
SyncPlay works over the tunnel too — everyone stays locked to the same frame, even when watching from different locations.

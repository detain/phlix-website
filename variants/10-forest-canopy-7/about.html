About

Why we built Phlix and what it stands for.

The problem

Most streaming platforms require an internet connection, push your viewing data to third parties, and may cancel shows you love without warning. If you have a large media library, you deserve better.

What Phlix is

Phlix is a self-hostable PHP media server. Install it on any PHP 8.3+ host, point it at your media files, and stream to every screen you own. Your library stays on your hardware.

We built native clients for Roku, Samsung Tizen, Windows, and mobile because browser playback is constrained by platform DRM. SyncPlay support means movie night works remotely, not just on the same LAN.

License

Features

Everything your library needs, nothing it doesn't.

Library Management

Folder-watcher tracks mtime changes and fires rescans automatically. Scanner parses S01E02 patterns, (2020) year-in-title, and dozens of other filename conventions. ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json from TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO — with a 24-hour cache to keep your API quotas happy.

SyncPlay

Real-time group session sync using a weighted-mean NTP offset calculated over 5 samples. Play, pause, seek, skip — every client moves together. Works across WAN so friends and family can join from anywhere.

Transcoding

QualitySelector ships profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. FFmpeg handles H.264/H.265 with CRF 23/28, producing HLS master and variant playlists. Per-device profiles detect client capability and pick the right encoding on the fly.

Multi-User & Parental Controls

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing, up to 5 profiles per user, 4- or 6-digit PINs, and rating filter from G to NC-17. Per-profile watch history and continue-watching lists.

Live TV & DVR

ChannelManager discovers tuner devices on your network. GuideManager pulls EPG data from XMLTV sources. Recorder schedules and processes recordings with post-processing scripts. All accessible through the same unified UI.

Plugin System

Versioned manifest contract (v1, v2) lets plugin authors ship with confidence. LifecycleInterface hooks cover install, enable, disable, upgrade, and uninstall. phlix-plugin-example is the smallest possible working starter.

DLNA / UPnP

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and RenderingControl via the UPnP stack. DeviceRegistry persists discovered devices and their capabilities. Works with every DLNA-capable TV, game console, and media receiver without installing anything.

Phlix Hub

Reverse-tunnel relay lets you reach your server from anywhere without configuring routers or exposing ports. Self-host the hub or use the public relay. TLS end-to-end with optional wireguard overlay.

About

Self-hosted media. Open source. No lock-in.

Philosophy

Phlix keeps your library on your hardware, ships under a permissive license so you can fork it, and lets the community drive what gets built next.

FAQ

Is Phlix really free?
Yes. Phlix is 100% open source under the BSD-3-Clause license. No premium tier, no feature gates, no cloud subscription required.
What devices does Phlix support?
Roku, Samsung Tizen TVs, Windows (x86_64 and ARM64), iOS, Android, and any device with DLNA/UPnP support.
How does SyncPlay work?
Phlix uses a weighted-mean NTP offset calculation over 5 samples to keep all clients synchronized within milliseconds, even across the internet.
Can I use my own metadata sources?
Yes. Phlix aggregates TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, and local NFO files with a 24-hour cache. You control what gets used.
Does Phlix work with existing media libraries?
Yes. Phlix scanner automatically parses S01E02 and (2020) title formats, handles folder structures, and imports existing metadata from NFO files.

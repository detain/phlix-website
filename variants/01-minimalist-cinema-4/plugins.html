Extensibility

Plugin System

A versioned manifest contract means your plugins won't break when Phlix updates.

LifecycleInterface

Implement install, upgrade, and uninstall hooks. Phlix calls your plugin at the right moments.

Manifest Schema

Declare your plugin's name, version, dependencies, and capabilities in a typed manifest.

Drop-in Loading

Drop your plugin folder into the plugins directory. The loader picks it up automatically.

Example Plugin

See phlix-plugin-example for a reference metadata-provider plugin. It demonstrates the smallest working starter with manifest, lifecycle hooks, and service registration.

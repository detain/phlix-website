What Phlix does

Features

Everything you need to stream your media, your way.

Library Management

Folder-watcher monitors your media directories, automatically detecting new files via mtime hashing. The scanner parses S01E02, Season X Episode Y, and (2020) title formats. ItemRepository hydrates metadata_json from TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv, or local NFO files with a 24-hour cache.

SyncPlay

Real-time group playback with weighted-mean NTP offset over 5 samples. Every device stays locked to the same frame. Play, pause, seek, or browse — everyone moves together, across the room or across the country.

Adaptive Transcoding

QualitySelector profiles for mobile-low, mobile-high, web, and tv-4k. CRF 23/28 libx264/libx265 with HLS master and variant playlists. FFmpeg transcoding adjusts on the fly to your network conditions.

Multi-User & Profiles

JWT auth with refresh tokens, Argon2ID password hashing. Up to 5 profiles per user with 4- or 6-digit PINs. Parental controls with rating filters from G to NC-17. Everyone gets their own watchlists and resume positions.

Live TV & DVR

ChannelManager, GuideManager, and Recorder handle scheduled recordings and real-time EPG data. Set recordings from the guide, manage upcoming shows, and watch live TV alongside your on-demand library.

DLNA Support

ContentDirectory, AvTransport, and DeviceRegistry implementations mean your old smart TV, game console, or any DLNA-certified device can stream from Phlix without needing a dedicated app.

Plugin System

Versioned manifest contract for extending Phlix. Authentication plugins, metadata providers, transcoding pipelines, and custom clients can all hook into the core system without forking the server.

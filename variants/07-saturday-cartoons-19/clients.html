Native apps

Clients

Phlix runs on your favorite devices. Here are the supported clients.

Roku

Stable

Native Roku channel for streaming directly from your Phlix server.

  • Direct playback
  • SyncPlay
  • Subtitles

Samsung Tizen TV

Stable

Native Tizen app for Samsung Smart TVs released after 2017.

  • Remote control
  • Deep link support
  • HEVC support

Windows

Stable

Desktop app for Windows 10 and 11 with full feature parity.

  • Desktop notifications
  • Background sync
  • Local playback

iOS / iPadOS

Stable

Native app for iPhone and iPad from the App Store.

  • AirPlay
  • Offline downloads
  • CarPlay

Android

Stable

Native Android app from Google Play and F-Droid.

  • Cast support
  • Background playback
  • Widgets

DLNA / UPnP

Stable

Works with any DLNA-certified device without a dedicated app.

  • Smart TVs
  • Game consoles
  • Stereo receivers

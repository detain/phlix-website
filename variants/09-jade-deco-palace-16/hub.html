Phlix Hub

Access your servers from anywhere, no port forwarding required.

How it works

Phlix Hub uses a reverse-tunnel relay to connect your clients to your servers through a secure cloud relay. Sign in once, and all your registered servers become accessible from any network.

End-to-end encrypted

All traffic through the relay is encrypted. The relay operator cannot see your media or credentials.

Always available

If your server is online, it's accessible through Hub. No need to check router settings.

Self-hostable

Deploy your own Hub relay, or use the public Hub at hub.phlix.app. Both are free for personal use.

Getting started with Hub

Enable Hub in your Phlix server settings, generate a registration token, and add the server to your Hub account. The process takes about two minutes.

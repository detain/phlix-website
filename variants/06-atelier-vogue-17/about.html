About

Open-source media server built for self-hosters.

Why Phlix?

Phlix started because existing media servers were either too closed (SaaS-only, no self-hosting), too complex (Jellyfin/Emby learning curve), or too expensive for what they offered. We wanted something that:

  • Runs on cheap hardware — even a Raspberry Pi 5
  • Streams to every device you already own
  • Stays out of your way when you're just watching something
  • Doesn't phone home without your permission

Project history

Phlix began in 2019 as a PHP rewrite of a smaller project. The first version could only serve a single Roku channel. Over time it grew: transcoding, library scanning, SyncPlay, live TV, multi-user, and plugins.

Today Phlix powers media libraries for hundreds of self-hosters. It's BSD-3 licensed, so you can fork it, build on it, and ship your own products.

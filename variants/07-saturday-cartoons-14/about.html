About Phlix

Open-source media, on your terms.

Project

Phlix is a self-hostable PHP media server that puts you in control of your media library. No subscriptions, no cloud dependencies, no data harvesting — just your media, streamed to your devices.

The project is maintained by Joe Huss and released under the BSD-3-Clause license. Contributions are welcome — whether it's code, bug reports, documentation improvements, or plugin development.

License

Phlix is BSD-3-Clause licensed. This means you can use it personally or commercially, modify it, distribute it, and even build closed-source products on top of it — as long as you preserve the copyright notices.

Support

For bugs and feature requests, use the GitHub issue tracker. For general questions and discussion, check the GitHub discussions or reach out through the community channels.

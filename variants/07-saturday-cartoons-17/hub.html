Hub

One server, every screen. Phlix Hub brings your media to any device in your home.

How It Works

Phlix Hub is the central server that organizes your media library and streams it to any device. Install it once on a server, NAS, or even a Raspberry Pi, then access your content from every screen in your home.

Streaming Protocols

DLNA / UPnP

Built-in DLNA server streams to TVs, game consoles, and any DLNA-certified device without additional apps.

Chromecast

Cast videos, music, and photos directly from the WebUI or mobile apps to Chromecast-enabled devices.

AirPlay

Stream audio content to AirPlay 2 speakers and receivers throughout your home.

HLS Streaming

Adaptive HTTP Live Streaming ensures smooth playback on any connection, from mobile data to fiber.

Device Detection

Phlix Hub automatically discovers devices on your network. Smart TVs, streaming sticks, game consoles, and network audio systems appear in the Phlix interface for easy one-tap streaming.

No IP addresses to remember, no configuration files to edit. Phlix handles the networking so you can focus on watching.

Multi-User Support

Phlix Hub supports multiple users with their own libraries, watch histories, and preferences. Parents can have separate profiles from kids, each with their own content restrictions and recommendations.

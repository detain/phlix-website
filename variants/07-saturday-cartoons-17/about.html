About Phlix

Open-source media server software built with PHP, designed for self-hosting enthusiasts.

Our Mission

Phlix exists because we believe you should control your own media. No subscriptions, no cloud dependencies, no tracking. Your library stays on your hardware, streamed to your devices.

We built Phlix for the self-hosting community — people who want the convenience of streaming services without giving up ownership of their content or privacy of their viewing habits.

Open Source

Phlix is and always will be free and open source under the BSD-3-Clause license. The server software, all client apps, and the website are developed in the open on GitHub.

We welcome contributions from developers, translators, designers, and users. Check out our GitHub repository to get involved.

Technology

Phlix is built with PHP for broad hosting compatibility — from shared web hosting to dedicated servers. The architecture prioritizes reliability and low resource usage over flashy dependencies.

  • Server: PHP 7.4+ with MySQL/MariaDB
  • Clients: Native apps for Roku, Tizen, Windows, iOS, Android
  • Streaming: HLS, DLNA, FFmpeg transcoding
  • Metadata: TMDB, TVDB, Fanart.tv integration

Community

Phlix has an active community of users and developers. Join us on Discord for support, feature discussions, and announcements. The community also contributes plugins, translations, and client apps.

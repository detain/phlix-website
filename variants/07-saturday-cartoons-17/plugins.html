Plugins

Extend Phlix with community-built plugins. Import from other services, add features, and customize your experience.

Official Plugins

Official Trakt.tv Sync

Sync your watch history, ratings, and watchlists with Trakt.tv. Keep your progress tracked across platforms.

Official Plex Import

Import your existing Plex library, watch history, and user data into Phlix seamlessly.

Official Anime Module

Dedicated support for anime with AniDB and AniList metadata sources and specific sorting options.

Official Emby Import

Migrate from Emby or Jellyfin with library structure, metadata, and user data preservation.

Community Plugins

Steam Library

Display and launch your Steam games alongside your media. Perfect for a home theater PC setup.

News Aggregation

RSS and Atom feed reader with media-aware formatting. Stay updated without leaving your couch.

Weather Display

Current conditions and forecasts displayed on the home screen. Because why not?

Custom Themes

Create and share custom color schemes and layouts for the Phlix interface.

Phlix has a plugin architecture that lets developers extend functionality. The documentation includes a plugin development guide with examples and API reference.

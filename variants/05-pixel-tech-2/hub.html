Phlix Hub

Sign in once. Access anywhere.

Cloud Directory + Reverse-Tunnel Relay

Run Phlix on your LAN and use the Phlix Hub's reverse-tunnel relay to reach it from your phone or Roku at a friend's house. You can self-host the hub, or use the public one.

How It Works

Register Your Server

Connect your Phlix server to the Hub once. Get a unique server ID.

Sign In

Use any Phlix client. Sign in to the Hub with your credentials.

Access Anywhere

The Hub relays the connection. No port forwarding needed.

Self-Host or Use Public

phlix-hub is open source. Run your own relay, or use the public Hub at phlix-hub.example.com.

## Notes
- All generated images should use the brand palette: #C0392B (retro red), #F5E9D4 (cream), #D4A017 (mustard), #1ABC9C (teal)
- Style: Thick black outlines, rounded corners, slight halftone/texture overlay
- Mood: Nostalgic, fun, friendly, warm, Americana, vintage cinema vibe
- Avoid: Neon or cyberpunk colors, ultra-modern fonts, cold technology feel, dark moody tones

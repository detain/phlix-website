About Phlix

Open-source media server. Your library. Your hardware. Your rules.

What is Phlix?

Phlix is a self-hostable PHP media server built to stream your video library to every device you own. It started because existing solutions were either locked to a cloud you didn't control or required expensive hardware to run.

Phlix runs on any PHP 8.1+ host — a Raspberry Pi, an old desktop, a VPS. Connect your storage, point Phlix at your files, and stream anywhere.

Project History

Phlix began as a personal project to solve a specific problem: streaming media to a Samsung TV without relying on Plex, Jellyfin, or Emby. The first version handled DLNA only. SyncPlay came after friends complained about keeping movie night in sync.

Today Phlix has native clients, a plugin system, and a relay service that makes remote access work without configuring your router.

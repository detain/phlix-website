FAQ

How does SyncPlay work?
Phlix clients sync to a weighted mean of 5 NTP samples from the server, keeping all viewers at the same playback position within ~100ms.
Can I use external storage?
Yes. Mount NFS, SMB, or USB drives and add them as library paths in Phlix settings.
Does Phlix support 4K transcoding?
Yes. The tv-4k quality profile uses CRF 23 libx265 with HLS output, adaptive to client bandwidth.

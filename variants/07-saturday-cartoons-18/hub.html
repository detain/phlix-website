Hub

Your library, synced across every device and server.

Phlix Hub is a cloud service that syncs your metadata, watch progress, ratings, and preferences across multiple Phlix servers. Start a movie on your home server, continue on your phone while commuting, and finish on a friend's server — all with seamless sync.

Features

  • Metadata Sync — Your ratings, watchlists, and progress follow you
  • Cross-Server Playback — Continue watching on any connected server
  • Profile Bridging — Link profiles across multiple Phlix instances
  • Secure & Private — End-to-end encrypted, you control your data

Getting Started

Sign up at hub.phlix.online and link your Phlix server through the admin interface. The Hub is optional — Phlix works great without it.

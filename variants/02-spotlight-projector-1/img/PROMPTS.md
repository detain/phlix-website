All assets follow the "Classic Cinematic" brand kit for Spotlight Projector V1:
- Warm gold tones (#F5C542, #FFB84D)
- Deep black backgrounds
- Burgundy accents
- Soft spotlight effects
- Theater curtain ambiance hints
- Cinzel-inspired typography feel

The projector motif represents the classic cinema experience that Phlix delivers - bringing the theater quality experience to self-hosted media.

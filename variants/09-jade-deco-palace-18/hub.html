Phlix Hub

Access your servers from anywhere, even behind NAT.

What is Phlix Hub?

Phlix Hub is a reverse-tunnel relay that lets you connect to your home server from anywhere in the world — without opening ports on your router or paying for dynamic DNS.

Sign in once. The hub handles NAT traversal. Your media follows you.

Two Options

Public Hub

Use the hosted hub at hub.phlix.app. No setup required — just sign in and register your server.

Sign In

Self-Hosted

Run your own Hub relay. Full control, full privacy. Deploy alongside your Phlix server on the same hardware.

View on GitHub

How It Works

When your Phlix server starts, it establishes an outbound connection to the Hub relay. The Hub assigns it a unique ID. When you open your Phlix client, it connects to the Hub and requests the server with that ID. The Hub bridges the two connections — no ports opened, no NAT punch-through needed.

Security

All traffic through the Hub is encrypted. The Hub only sees tunneled connections — it doesn't store your media, credentials, or any data beyond connection metadata.

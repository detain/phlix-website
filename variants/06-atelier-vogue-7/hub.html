Hub

Access your server from anywhere.

No NAT, no port forwarding

Phlix Hub uses a reverse-tunnel relay. Your server initiates an outbound connection to the hub, and clients connect to the hub to reach your server — NAT and firewalls just work.

Self-host or use the public hub

The public hub at hub.phlix.app is free for personal use. For enterprise or self-hosted scenarios, you can run your own Hub server — it's open source.

Security

All traffic through the Hub is TLS-encrypted. The Hub only sees encrypted tunnel traffic — it cannot read or modify your media streams.

Get started

Enable Hub mode in your Phlix Server settings. Sign in once, add your servers, and they're accessible from anywhere.
